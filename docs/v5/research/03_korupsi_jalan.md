# Corruption and Waste in Road Construction & Maintenance in Indonesia (2019–2026): Fact Base for PAVEMENT-PINN

Scope note: Indonesia only. Years are marked. A case is included only when an official body (KPK, Kejaksaan, court, BPK) has named suspects, indicted, convicted, or issued an audit finding, as reported by that body or by major media. Legal status is given **as of the cited source date**. Later developments may exist. Where sources disagree, both versions are shown.

## 1. Concrete road/bridge corruption cases 2019–2026 (who, agency, value, modus, loss, status)

### Takeaway
Road and bridge cases come in two families, and both are well documented. (a) **Bribery/fee cases** handled mainly by KPK through OTT operations. Here a regional head, a Kadis PUPR or DPRD members take a fixed % "commitment fee" (1–22%) to arrange tenders or e-katalog awards. (b) **State-loss (Pasal 2/3) cases** handled mainly by Kejaksaan. Here a contractor builds less volume, thickness or quality than the contract requires, while the PPK/PPTK/supervision consultant certifies 100% progress and payment. Family (b) is the one PAVEMENT-PINN's physics/durability audit can actually detect.

### Inferences
- Of the 20 cases, the Kejaksaan "state-loss" cases (#10–#19) share one physical signature: **paid progress (95–100%) far exceeds real physical progress (51–65%) or delivered volume/thickness**. A citizen-report + physics audit that estimates the as-built layer and lifespan targets exactly this gap.
- KPK cases (#1–#8) are mostly bribery/extortion. The fee (1–22%) is taken out of the contract value and is usually recovered by the contractor through reduced quality. That link is plausible and supported by a former KPK investigator's statement (see Q4), but the case documents fetched here do not prove it for each case.
- Several cases involve **DPRD members** (OKU, Labuhanbatu, Tobasa), which shows that budget/pokir capture is a recurring route, not an exception.

### Gaps
- Final verdicts not verified for: OKU, Riau, Kaltim BBPJN, Bengkalis Wika cluster, PPU sentence length, Tol MBZ kasasi, Batu Bara, Kukar, Mogoy–Merdey, Bengkulu Tengah, Konsel, Tebo. Check SIPP/putusan3.mahkamahagung.go.id before publishing.

## 2. "Fee proyek" percentages and KPK/ICW statistics on infrastructure/PBJ corruption

### Takeaway
KPK officials have described a ~10% kickback as a "norma umum" (2017). Recent road cases show 1–5% for officials (Sumut, Riau), up to 15% (Labuhanbatu) and 22% (OKU pokir). Procurement is ≈22–25% of all KPK cases. ICW counts **utilities/infrastructure** as the #2–3 most-prosecuted sector, after villages.

- **Fee % in recent road cases:** Sumut 4% (Kadis) + 1% (PPK), [detik](https://www.detik.com/sumut/berita/d-8218259/eks-kadis-pupr-topan-ginting-didakwa-terima-suap-di-kasus-korupsi-jalan-sumut). OKU 22% (20% DPRD + 2% Dinas PUPR), [Jawa Pos](https://www.jawapos.com/kasuistika/2511200384/kpk-tahan-wakil-ketua-dan-anggota-dprd-kabupaten-oku-karena-fee-proyek-pupr-pantas-apbd-naik-2-kali-lipat). Riau 2.5% raised to 5% of the budget increase, [CNN](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/nasional/20251105154920-12-1292270/gubernur-riau-minta-jatah-preman-rp7-m-dari-kenaikan-anggaran).
- **KPK, "10% norma umum" (2017, pre-window context):** Wakil Ketua KPK Laode M. - **KPK, PBJ share of cases (2026):** KPK spokesperson Budi Prasetyo: ≈25% of KPK cases (446 of 1,782) relate to procurement; PBJ remains in the "zona merah" on MCSP/SPI assessments (21 Apr 2026). — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/kpk-ingatkan-25-persen-perkara-korupsi-berkaitan-dengan-pengadaan-lt69e75d09c3674/?page=all)
- **KPK, earlier figure:** Wakil Ketua Alexander Marwata said almost 90% of corruption cases *at trial* concern PBJ. The same article gives 339 of 1,512 KPK cases (≈22%) as PBJ, the 2nd-largest category after bribery. Year not stated in the source. — [VOI](https://voi.id/en/amp/363193)
- **KPK, "systemic" infrastructure corruption (secondary):** a Media Indonesia/Metro TV editorial (2023) quotes KPK that "korupsi sudah sistemik di sektor infrastruktur". It also says KPK estimates only ≈50% of contract value is actually used for construction, and cites 5–10% bribes in the DJKA railway case. Treat as secondary until confirmed against a KPK primary source. — [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/NleC043m-darurat-korupsi-infrastruktur)
- **ICW totals 2019–2023:** 2019: 271 cases / 580 suspects / Rp8.4 T. 2020: 444 / 875 / Rp18.6 T. 2021: 533 / 1,173 / Rp29.4 T. 2022: 579 / 1,396 / Rp42.7 T. 2023: 791 / 1,695 / Rp28.4 T. — [Tempo English, 20 May 2024](https://en.tempo.co/amp/1869866/icw-logs-significant-hike-in-corruption-cases-in-the-past-5-years)
- **ICW 2023 by sector:** villages 187; government 108; **utilities 103, with 51% in infrastructure subsectors such as roads and bridges**; banking 65; education 59. — [GoodStats 28 Jun 2024](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/5-sektor-rawan-korupsi-di-indonesia-pada-tahun-2023-ExTbY)
- **ICW 2024:** 364 cases, 888 suspects, ≈Rp279 T potential loss (inflated by mega-cases). Sectors: villages 77, **utilities 57 (#2)**, government 41, health 39, … **transportation 11 (#10)**. — [Databoks 1 Oct 2025](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/economics-macro/statistics/68dcd879b05ee/10-sectors-with-the-most-corruption-cases-in-indonesia-in-2024); [Databoks totals](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/political/statistics/68de023e6a5c7/there-are-364-corruption-cases-in-indonesia-in-2024-with-potential-state-losses-of-idr-2799-trillion)

### Inferences
- A defensible statement for the app: "Kasus suap proyek jalan yang telah diputus/didakwa menunjukkan komitmen fee antara 1% hingga 22% dari nilai proyek." Avoid a single universal "10–20%" figure; the 10% "norm" quote is from 2017.
- ICW changed its sector taxonomy over time (infrastructure → utilities/transportation), so year-to-year comparisons of "infrastructure cases" should be avoided.

### Gaps
- ICW 2022 and 2025 sector-level infrastructure/road counts and losses were not obtained. The ICW 2024 primary PDF was not fetched.
- The KPK "50% of contract value" estimate was not found in a KPK primary document.
- A KPK press statement explicitly ranking infrastructure as "the most corrupt sector" was not found. Available KPK statements rank PBJ as #2 after bribery by case count.

## 3. BPK findings on road works (kekurangan volume, kelebihan pembayaran, quality)

### Takeaway
BPK routinely reports **kekurangan volume pekerjaan** and the resulting **kelebihan pembayaran** in regional capital spending. Road packages dominate local examples. Nationally, IHPS II 2024 reports Rp141.13 bn of volume shortfalls in 83 pemda. These findings are usually settled administratively (refund to kas daerah), not criminally.

### Cited Findings
- **IHPS II 2024 (BPK):** "kekurangan volume pekerjaan dan/atau barang sebesar Rp141,13 miliar pada 83 pemda" (Pengelolaan Belanja Pemda). Also "belanja tidak sesuai atau melebihi ketentuan" Rp91.11 bn in 66 pemda. Overall: 511 LHP, 4,658 temuan, 6,857 permasalahan worth Rp46.98 T, 14,823 rekomendasi. Ketidakpatuhan: 2,614 problems worth Rp43.43 T, incl. 1,391 "kerugian" (Rp1.17 T) and 268 "potensi kerugian". — [BPK, IHPS II 2024 PDF](https://jatim.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ihps_ii_2024_1762309845-red-1.pdf)
- **Aceh Tamiang (LHP LKPD TA 2023, No. 1.A/LHP/XVIII.BAC/03/2024, 25 Mar 2024):** 17 Dinas PUPR packages, mostly roads (long segment, pengerasan, pengaspalan, rekonstruksi) plus one bridge and talud, with kekurangan volume > Rp3 bn. Largest shortfalls: Rp865 jt on a Rp7.8 bn DAK long-segment road and Rp864 jt on a Rp12.8 bn road. Cause given: the Kadis (as PA) did not optimally supervise. Recommendation: refund to kas daerah. — [BPK clipping](https://sultra.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2.21-Temuan-BPK-RI-17-Paket-Pekerjaan-di-Aceh-Tamiang-Kekurangan-Volume-Rp-3-Miliar.pdf)
- **Lombok Tengah (LKPD 2022, reported 27 Jun 2023):** 22 Dinas PUPR packages (18 road, 4 irrigation) with kekurangan volume/kelebihan bayar > Rp4.2 bn; Rp2.99 bn already deposited back. — [detikBali](https://www.detik.com/bali/nusra/d-6795762/bpk-temukan-22-proyek-di-lombok-tengah-kelebihan-bayar-rp-42-miliar)
- **North Sulawesi (BPK Perwakilan Sulut, 27 May 2025):** 14 pemda, 597 work packages, kekurangan volume Rp17.12 bn; only Rp1.37 bn repaid; Rp15.74 bn still recommended for recovery. — [BPK Sulut clipping](https://sulut.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/BPK-Sulut-temukan-kekurangan-volume.pdf)
- **Kementerian PUPR (LK 2023, reported 21 Jul 2024):** BPK found in goods and capital spending on physical works: **volume shortfalls**, **unit-price calculation errors**, **unbalanced (timpang) unit prices not negotiated**, additional work priced at unbalanced unit prices, contract volumes not adjusted to design changes or field conditions, and **payments ahead of physical progress** (e.g., BPPW Kaltim). The opinion was still WTP (issues judged not material). — [DDTCNews](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1804167/bpk-temukan-beberapa-isu-pada-belanja-pekerjaan-fisik-kementerian-pupr)
- **Administrative vs criminal:** in Lombok Tengah the pemda "agreed with the findings" and ordered providers to repay ([detik](https://www.detik.com/bali/nusra/d-6795762/bpk-temukan-22-proyek-di-lombok-tengah-kelebihan-bayar-rp-42-miliar)). In Mogoy–Merdey, by contrast, Kejaksaan stated that repaying (Rp1.44 bn fine for volume/quality shortfall + Rp2 bn) "does not erase the criminal offense" ([Antara](https://papuatengah.antaranews.com/berita/61749/kejaksaan-terima-pengembalian-rp2-miliar-dari-kasus-korupsi-jalan-mogoy-merdey)).

### Inferences
- "Kekurangan volume" in BPK language is a *financial-compliance* finding (paid quantity > measured quantity). PAVEMENT-PINN can mirror this vocabulary. An estimated as-built shortfall would be an "indikasi kekurangan volume/mutu yang perlu verifikasi uji lapangan", not an accusation.
- BPK samples packages. Many small road packages (Rp185 jt–Rp3 bn in Aceh Tamiang) carry shortfalls of 5–35% of contract value. Citizen reports could help auditors prioritize which packages to sample.

### Gaps
- IHPS I 2023, II 2023, I 2024 and I 2025 national figures for kekurangan volume were not extracted, and no road-specific national total exists in what was fetched.
- No BPK document fetched quantifies "ketebalan aspal tidak sesuai" as a separate category. Thickness deficits appear in criminal forensic audits (Simeulue, Pidie) rather than in IHPS summaries.

## 4. Technical modus (thickness, materials, compaction, quantities, prices, e-katalog, etc.)

### Takeaway
Court and investigation records document: thinner asphalt than design, missing base layers, wrong Laston/prime coat volumes, out-of-spec aggregate and concrete, manual (non-mechanized) work, fictitious work items, falsified progress or completion documents, falsified company experience, rigged e-katalog awards and unbalanced unit prices. Several of these are physically detectable.

- **Materials not to spec + volume short (Pidie maintenance):** Politeknik Lhokseumawe expert finding. — [Antara Aceh](https://aceh.antaranews.com/berita/382865/kejati-kasus-korupsi-pemeliharaan-jalan-di-pidie-sudah-p21)

- **Manual work instead of to-spec execution; volume less than contract (Tobasa).** — [Antara Sumut](https://sumut.antaranews.com/berita/587359/bambang-pardede-sebut-dakwaan-jpu-kabur-tidak-ada-bukti-korupsi-jalan-di-tobasa)
- **Volume reduction in quantity and quality (Batu Bara, 12 suspects).** — [detik](https://www.detik.com/sumut/hukum-dan-kriminal/d-8091434/kejati-sumut-kembali-tahan-4-tersangka-korupsi-jalan-batu-bara-total-12-orang)
- **Fictitious work items (Tebo/Jambi).** — [detik](https://www.detik.com/sumut/hukum-dan-kriminal/d-6034744/kabid-bina-marga-pupr-jambi-tersangka-korupsi-perbaikan-jalan)

- **E-katalog manipulation (Sumut):** PPK and contractor "mengatur proses e-catalog" so PT DNG won Jalan Sipiongot–Batas Labusel. — [detik](https://www.detik.com/sumut/berita/d-8218259/eks-kadis-pupr-topan-ginting-didakwa-terima-suap-di-kasus-korupsi-jalan-sumut). OKU pokir projects were also run through e-katalog. — [Jawa Pos](https://www.jawapos.com/kasuistika/2511200384/kpk-tahan-wakil-ketua-dan-anggota-dprd-kabupaten-oku-karena-fee-proyek-pupr-pantas-apbd-naik-2-kali-lipat)
- **Falsified tender qualifications (Purbalingga):** fake company experience and technical capability documents. — [Dandapala](https://dandapala.com/article/detail/tok-pt-jateng-perberat-hukuman-terdakwa-korupsi-pembangunan-jalan-di-purbalingga)
- **Fabricated billing / fictitious vendors (Lampung toll, under investigation).** — [CNN](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/nasional/20250417053313-12-1219632/kasus-korupsi-jalan-tol-lampung-kejati-lampung-sita-rp163-m)
- **Core-drill QC literature (legitimate projects):** Padang–Pekanbaru toll Sec. 2: AC-BC thickness mostly ≥6.0 cm, but several points were below 6.0 cm. Density 97.19–99.47%, with some points below the ≥98% requirement. Variation was linked to mix temperature and the delay between production and spreading. — [Jurnal PNJ](https://jurnal.pnj.ac.id/index.php/cmj/article/view/7938). Bypass Mamminasata AC-WC: field density 2.25 vs lab 2.27 g/cc; all samples met thickness and density spec (Univ. Bosowa, 2026). — [JPTSK Unibos](https://journal.unibos.ac.id/jptsk/article/view/5104)

### Inferences
- Detectable by PAVEMENT-PINN: (1) thickness or base-layer deficits → premature rutting, cracking or settlement soon after PHO; (2) poor compaction/density → early ravelling and potholes; (3) progress-vs-payment gaps visible in photos. Not detectable physically: tender rigging, fee bribery, fake documents. Those need procurement data (Q7).
- Core-drill studies show that some under-thickness and under-density occurs even on supervised toll projects. The app's model must allow for tolerance and natural variance before raising a flag.

### Gaps
- No court-documented case fetched explicitly cites **missing tack coat**, **lower bitumen content (kadar aspal)** or **falsified lab test reports**. These remain plausible but unverified here.
- No documented case of **pemecahan paket** (package splitting to avoid tender) on roads was obtained.
- No Indonesian study systematically comparing contract vs field asphalt thickness across many projects, or unit-price mark-up vs AHSP/HSPK, was found within the search budget.

## 5. Where in the project cycle corruption occurs, and which roles are involved

### Takeaway
Documented cases cover every stage: **budgeting** (pokir/budget increases with pre-agreed fees), **procurement** (rigged tenders/e-katalog, borrowed companies, fake qualifications), **execution and supervision** (volume/quality cuts, consultants not controlling), **progress certification, PHO and payment** (paying 95–100% at 51–65% physical progress, documents faked to 100% before PHO) and **maintenance** (damage after the maintenance period). Charged roles include Gubernur/Bupati, DPRD members, Kadis/PA, KPA, PPK, PPTK, Kepala Satker, procurement bureau heads, konsultan pengawas and contractors.

### Cited Findings
- **Perencanaan/penganggaran:** OKU — DPRD pokir quota Rp45 bn cut to Rp35 bn with a 20% fee, and the PUPR budget doubled (Rp48 bn to Rp96 bn) in APBD 2025; KPK suspects the increase is tied to the DPRD quota ([Jawa Pos](https://www.jawapos.com/kasuistika/2511200384/kpk-tahan-wakil-ketua-dan-anggota-dprd-kabupaten-oku-karena-fee-proyek-pupr-pantas-apbd-naik-2-kali-lipat)). Riau — fee extracted from a 147% increase in the UPT road/bridge budget ([CNN](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/nasional/20251105154920-12-1292270/gubernur-riau-minta-jatah-preman-rp7-m-dari-kenaikan-anggaran)).

### Inferences
- The **PHO/payment certification point** is where physical fraud becomes financial loss: the PPK, PPTK, supervision consultant and PPHP/inspection team sign that work is complete. PAVEMENT-PINN's highest-value insertion point is a durability check at PHO and through the maintenance period (FHO), when the contractor is still liable.
- Roles to model in the app's data schema: PA/KPA (Kadis), PPK, PPTK, Pokja/UKPBJ (Kepala BPPBJ), konsultan pengawas, kontraktor, PPHP/tim PHO, Inspektorat (APIP), BPK.

### Gaps
- No fetched case explicitly charges a **PPHP/Panitia Penerima Hasil Pekerjaan** member for signing a PHO berita acara without testing. This is plausible from the progress-vs-payment cases but not documented here.

## 6. Short life of repairs / "repair fails → budget again" cycle, and supporting studies

### Takeaway
There is case-level evidence of roads and structures failing **days to months** after work, and of the same segment being re-contracted in successive years. However, no national dataset on repair lifespan or repeat-patching frequency was found. This evidence gap is exactly what PAVEMENT-PINN's longitudinal citizen reports could fill.

### Cited Findings
- **Alor, NTT (2021–22):** provincial road Kalabahi–Kokar (14.91 km, pagu Rp28.2 bn, PT Karya Baru Calisa). Hotmix cracked **1–2 days after paving**, with ≈9 spots patched and three rounds of damage before the work was finished. — [BPK NTT clipping of Kompas, 23 May 2022](https://ntt.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Jalan-Provinsi-di-Alor-NTT-Senilai-Rp-28-Miliar-Rusak-Sebelum-Tuntas-Dikerjakan.pdf)
- **Pidie, Aceh:** after the maintenance period the road surface **sank and cracked**. The case reached P21 in 2025. — [Antara Aceh](https://aceh.antaranews.com/berita/382865/kejati-kasus-korupsi-pemeliharaan-jalan-di-pidie-sudah-p21)
- **Repeated works on the same road (opentender case study):** OCP/ICW describe a road re-worked over three consecutive years (square conblock, then hexagonal conblock, then asphalt), and a bridge that **collapsed ≈6 months after construction**. — [Open Contracting Partnership, 20 Feb 2025](https://www.open-contracting.org/2025/02/20/6-steps-for-civic-monitors-to-identify-fraud-risks-in-procurement-in-indonesia/)
- **Tapin, Kalsel:** a retaining wall completed end of 2018 collapsed ≈April 2019 "not due to natural disaster", and was prosecuted. — [Antara Kalsel](https://kalsel.antaranews.com/berita/260042/kasus-korupsi-di-kalsel-asn-dinas-pupr-tapin-dan-seorang-kontraktor-resmi-jadi-terdakwa)
- **Tol MBZ:** court expert said reduced stiffness could increase vibration, cause fatigue and **shorten service life**, and that retrofitting the ~40 km structure would be very expensive. — [GridOto](https://otomotifnet.gridoto.com/read/234092483/terkuak-di-sidang-korupsi-mutu-kekuatan-tol-layang-mbz-berkurang-6-persen?page=all)
- **Policy response (2013, context):** at KPK's suggestion, the Ministry of PU began **performance-based contracts**: contractors are paid for delivered quality, sanctioned or not paid for substandard work, and must repair damage during the maintenance period. — [JPNN 28 Jul 2013](https://m.jpnn.com/news/jalan-kembali-rusak-bayaran-kontraktor-dipangkas)
- **Core-drill evidence of variance even on supervised projects:** see Q4 ([Jurnal PNJ](https://jurnal.pnj.ac.id/index.php/cmj/article/view/7938)).

### Inferences
- The "fail → re-budget" loop works like this: fee/volume cut → under-designed layer → early distress → new rehabilitation or maintenance package (often to the same or related contractors) → new fee opportunity. Each step is documented in some case above, but no single source documents the whole loop for one segment. Present it as a *hypothesized mechanism supported by case fragments*.
- Asset-level tracking (segment ID + contract history + distress reports over time) is the missing evidence. PAVEMENT-PINN can produce it.

### Gaps
- No national statistics on average repair lifespan, re-damage rates or repeat contracting of the same segment.
- No peer-reviewed Indonesian study comparing contract unit prices with AHSP/HSPK fair cost across road packages was retrieved.
- A 2023 KPK statement on why many Lampung roads are damaged ([RMOL headline](https://rmol.id/politik/read/2023/05/18/574533/kpk-ungkap-penyebab-banyak-jalan-rusak-di-lampung)) could not be fetched. Its content is unverified.

## 7. Existing transparency/anti-corruption tools and remaining gaps

### Takeaway
Indonesia has rich *procurement-stage* transparency: LPSE/SPSE, SiRUP, SIKaP, the INAPROC blacklist, e-katalog, and ICW's opentender.net red-flag scoring. But contract documents and **as-built physical condition** are not openly linked to these records. That is the gap PAVEMENT-PINN fills.

### Cited Findings
- **opentender.net (ICW, co-developed with LKPP):** covers procurement of >600 ministries, agencies and local governments. It scores tenders high/medium/low risk using ICW's Potential Fraud Analysis with 7 indicators: long notice-to-award gap; very small or large deviation between contract and estimated price (HPS); high contract value; short tender title (<20 chars) or description (<60 chars); Q4 procurement; a company winning ≥4 contracts per year. It shows company administrators and win histories. — [Open Contracting Partnership 2025](https://www.open-contracting.org/2025/02/20/6-steps-for-civic-monitors-to-identify-fraud-risks-in-procurement-in-indonesia/)
- **Data sources in the civic-monitoring method:** LPSE (e-procurement), SiRUP (sirup.lkpp.go.id), SIKaP (vendor performance), the LKPP blacklist (inaproc.id), AHU company deeds, BPK audit reports and OSINT. — [OCP 2025](https://www.open-contracting.org/2025/02/20/6-steps-for-civic-monitors-to-identify-fraud-risks-in-procurement-in-indonesia/)
- **Stated gaps:** "contract documents are not disclosed openly in Indonesia, which makes construction specification analysis difficult"; not all projects are audited by BPK. Civic reports to LKPP were often rejected (2021: 70 of 90 rejected; 2022: 13 of 33 rejected). — [OCP 2025](https://www.open-contracting.org/2025/02/20/6-steps-for-civic-monitors-to-identify-fraud-risks-in-procurement-in-indonesia/)
- **E-katalog as a manipulation channel:** in both Sumut and OKU, the projects were awarded through e-katalog and the award was arranged ([detik](https://www.detik.com/sumut/berita/d-8218259/eks-kadis-pupr-topan-ginting-didakwa-terima-suap-di-kasus-korupsi-jalan-sumut); [Jawa Pos](https://www.jawapos.com/kasuistika/2511200384/kpk-tahan-wakil-ketua-dan-anggota-dprd-kabupaten-oku-karena-fee-proyek-pupr-pantas-apbd-naik-2-kali-lipat)). This shows that moving from tender to e-katalog does not by itself remove collusion.
- **KPK on PBJ risk:** PBJ remains in the "zona merah" on MCSP and SPI assessments (Apr 2026). — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/kpk-ingatkan-25-persen-perkara-korupsi-berkaitan-dengan-pengadaan-lt69e75d09c3674/?page=all)

### Inferences
- None of opentender's 7 indicators uses **physical outcome data** (road condition, durability, re-damage). Combining a PFA-style procurement risk score with a PINN durability score per segment would be novel. Join keys: kode RUP/kode tender ↔ ruas/segment ↔ geotagged reports.
- The "very small or very large deviation between contract and HPS" indicator pairs naturally with PAVEMENT-PINN's "fair cost" estimate, which is an independent check on the HPS itself.

### Gaps
- **CoST (Infrastructure Transparency Initiative):** this research found no evidence that Indonesia is a CoST member. Searches returned other countries only. Verify at infrastructuretransparency.org before claiming either way.
- KPK JAGA (jaga.id) features, LKPP e-katalog v6/INAPROC changes, and Open Contracting Data Standard adoption by LKPP were not verified in this session.

## 8. Legal language: wording audit flags without defamation; presumption of innocence

### Takeaway
After **Putusan MK 105/PUU-XXII/2024 (29 Apr 2025)**, the UU ITE defamation article (Pasal 27A) protects only **individuals**. Government institutions and corporations cannot be complainants, and public-interest criticism is exempt. Named individuals (a Kadis, a contractor owner) can still complain, and the KUHP 2023 (in force 2026) keeps complaint-based offenses for insulting the government or state institutions. The app should flag *projects and data discrepancies*, not people, and use BPK/APIP-style neutral vocabulary.

### Cited Findings
- **MK 105/PUU-XXII/2024 (read 29 Apr 2025):** "orang lain" in Pasal 27A and Pasal 45(4) UU ITE is conditionally unconstitutional unless read to **exclude lembaga pemerintah, groups with specific identity, institutions, corporations, professions or positions**. "Orang lain" means individuals. The offense is complaint-based (delik aduan), requiring the harmed person's complaint. Pasal 45(7) excludes statements made **in the public interest** (protest, criticism) or in self-defense. The Court said restricting expression weakens public oversight. — [Antara](https://kupang.antaranews.com/berita/155665/mk-pasal-menyerang-nama-baik-di-uu-ite-dikecualikan-untuk-lembaga-pemerintah); see also [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/NrWCoodB-mk-putuskan-menghina-pemerintah-tak-lagi-bisa-dijerat-uu-ite-ini-pasal-yang-diubah)
- **KUHP 2023 (UU 1/2023, in force 2026):** Pasal 240 (publicly insulting the Pemerintah or Lembaga Negara) and Pasal 241 (spreading such insults, including via IT; max 3 years or category IV fine) remain. Both are delik aduan, requiring a complaint by the government/institution. — [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/NLMCJJWZ-kuhp-2023-masih-buka-celah-jerat-pengkritik-pemerintah-walau-uu-ite-tak-bisa-dipakai)
- **BPK neutral vocabulary (from IHPS):** "temuan", "permasalahan", "ketidakpatuhan terhadap ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan", "kelemahan sistem pengendalian intern", "kekurangan volume pekerjaan", "kelebihan pembayaran", "potensi kerugian", "rekomendasi", "disetor ke kas daerah". — [BPK IHPS II 2024](https://jatim.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ihps_ii_2024_1762309845-red-1.pdf); [BPK Aceh Tamiang clipping](https://sultra.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2.21-Temuan-BPK-RI-17-Paket-Pekerjaan-di-Aceh-Tamiang-Kekurangan-Volume-Rp-3-Miliar.pdf)
- **Law-enforcement vocabulary to mirror:** media consistently attribute KPK claims as "diduga" (allegedly) and distinguish tersangka, terdakwa and terpidana (e.g., [detik](https://news.detik.com/berita/d-5897299/kpk-tetapkan-bupati-ppu-tersangka-suap-begini-konstruksi-perkaranya)). Kejaksaan computes "kerugian negara" only through BPK/BPKP/Inspektorat or expert audits, and defendants contest loss figures not issued by BPK (Tobasa defense argument, [Antara](https://sumut.antaranews.com/berita/587359/bambang-pardede-sebut-dakwaan-jpu-kabur-tidak-ada-bukti-korupsi-jalan-di-tobasa)).
- **Uncharged persons:** KPK publicly stated it had not found involvement of the Sumut governor ([Bisnis headline](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20251118/16/1929704/kasus-jalan-sumut-kpk-belum-temukan-keterlibatan-bobby)), which illustrates the risk of linking officials to cases by association.

### Inferences
- Recommended flag wording (Indonesian):
  - Use: "Indikasi ketidaksesuaian antara kondisi lapangan dan spesifikasi/nilai kontrak — perlu klarifikasi dan uji lapangan (core drill) oleh instansi berwenang"; "Estimasi biaya wajar berbeda X% dari nilai kontrak (berdasarkan AHSP/asumsi model; bukan temuan audit)"; "Kerusakan dini terdeteksi N bulan setelah PHO — masih dalam/di luar masa pemeliharaan"; "Permintaan klarifikasi"; "Data publik: kode RUP/tender ..."
  - Avoid: "korupsi", "mark-up", "maling", "fiktif", "merugikan negara Rp…" (a legal conclusion reserved for BPK/court), and naming individuals.
- Name a person only when they appear in an official charging or court document. Always state the status (tersangka/terdakwa/terpidana), the date, the source link, and that the person is presumed innocent until the verdict is inkracht. Attach flags to the **paket/ruas**, not the person.
- Even with MK 105/2024, a named contractor *owner* or Kadis (as an individual) can still file a Pasal 27A complaint. Provide a right-of-reply/klarifikasi channel and a correction log, and keep model assumptions transparent. That supports the Pasal 45(7) public-interest exemption.
- Distinguish administrative BPK outcomes (kelebihan bayar refunded) from criminal findings. A refunded BPK finding is not a crime finding, while Kejaksaan holds that refunds do not cancel a crime (Mogoy–Merdey). The app should not make either judgment.

### Gaps
- The specific statutory text of the presumption of innocence (commonly cited as UU 48/2009 on Kekuasaan Kehakiman, Pasal 8 ayat 1, and the KUHAP general elucidation) was **not source-verified in this session**. Verify before quoting.
- The exact KUHP 2023 defamation articles for individuals (pencemaran/fitnah) and their penalties were not fetched. Verify the article numbers before use.
- Dewan Pers / journalism-code guidance on reporting suspects (initials, etc.) was not researched.
- Overall research limitation: the session's web-search budget was exhausted. Remaining verification (verdicts, IHPS 2023/2025 figures, CoST status, presumption-of-innocence text) needs a follow-up.
