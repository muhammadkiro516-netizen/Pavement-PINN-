## 1. AHSP (Permen PUPR 8/2023 + SE Dirjen Bina Konstruksi): method, Bina Marga item codes, coefficients, overhead/profit, PPN

### Takeaway
The current legal method is Permen PUPR No. 8/2023. Its technical lampiran is published and updated through SE Dirjen Bina Konstruksi No. 73/2023 and then SE No. 30/SE/Dk/2025, whose Lampiran V holds AHSP Bina Marga. Unit price = labor + material + equipment (direct cost), plus indirect cost (Biaya Umum + Keuntungan) of 10–15% of direct cost per the Permen body. Bina Marga practice and training material use 10%. Pothole repair is paid under item **11.1.(9) "Perbaikan Campuran Aspal Panas" per m³**. Its official coefficients were **not retrievable** in this session.

### Cited Findings
**Legal structure**
- Permen PUPR No. 8/2023 body: Pasal 11(1) defines biaya tidak langsung as Biaya Umum + keuntungan. Pasal 11(3) says it is "dihitung sebesar 10% (sepuluh persen) hingga 15% (lima belas persen) dari biaya langsung". Pasal 11(2) says Biaya Umum includes repair costs and the handling of construction-accident impacts. Pasal 1(5) says the HPS already accounts for PPN but sets no rate. — [Permen PUPR 8/2023 (peraturan.go.id)](https://peraturan.go.id/files/permen-pupr-no-8-tahun-2023.pdf)
- Permen 8/2023 Pasal 7(1)(a): the labor HSD comes from "upah minimum provinsi atau upah minimum kabupaten/kota di luar pajak". Pasal 7(2): the wage = upah pokok + tunjangan. Pasal 7(3): it is computed per worker. — [Permen PUPR 8/2023](https://peraturan.go.id/files/permen-pupr-no-8-tahun-2023.pdf)
- Permen 8/2023 Pasal 5(5): "paling banyak 2 (dua) orang" per mechanical equipment unit (operator + helper). Pasal 15(1) lists Bina Marga work groups including "perkerasan aspal", "pekerjaan preventif" and "pekerjaan pemeliharaan". Pasal 15(2): SMKK is a separate pokok pekerjaan. — [Permen PUPR 8/2023](https://peraturan.go.id/files/permen-pupr-no-8-tahun-2023.pdf)
- Permen 8/2023 Pasal 4(3) (quoted in training): for lump-sum work, the unit price does not include biaya tidak langsung. — [Kaltim DPUPR-PERA training "Pengantar Permen PUPR 08/2023 & SE Dirjen Binkons 73/2023", Samarinda 21 Feb 2024](https://sibikon-dpuprpera.kaltimprov.go.id/download-materi-acara/materi-8)
- SE Dirjen Bina Konstruksi No. 73/2023 is the implementing technical guidance for Permen 8/2023. Its Lampiran I has Tabel A.2.d "Berat Isi Campuran Beraspal". Lampiran III has "Contoh Tarif Upah dan Analisis HSD Upah (Tenaga) per Jam" and example analyses for "Pekerjaan Preventif", "Lapis Perkerasan Berbutir" and "Perkerasan Beraspal". The original is at bit.ly/SEDJBKAHSP. — [Kaltim training, Dian Arief Prawira Ramadhan (Dit. Keberlanjutan Konstruksi), 21 Feb 2024](https://sibikon-dpuprpera.kaltimprov.go.id/download-materi-acara/materi-5)
- SE Dirjen Binkons No. 30/2025 updates the lampiran. Lampiran V (Bina Marga): non-JBH sheets went from 1664 to 1723 and JBH sheets from 449 to 469. Tabel A.2.d (berat isi campuran beraspal) went from 13 to 9 rows. Tabel A.10 (bucket factor) is no longer used. Lampiran VII now refers to Perpres 180/2024 and Permen PU 1/2024. Also: "kadar aspal minimum tidak disarankan". In Bina Marga items, F.40 (AC-WC NR) was removed; AC-BC/AC-Base Asb Murni were added; the Fog Seal (D.1–D.3) emulsion coefficients changed. — [Kaltim training "Pengantar Permen PUPR 08/23 & SE Dirjen Binkons 30/25"](https://sibikon-dpuprpera.kaltimprov.go.id/download-materi-acara/PengantarPermenPUPR0823_SEDirjenBinkons3025)
- The same training deck states Biaya Umum & Keuntungan (OHP) is capped at **10%**, citing SE Dirjen Bina Marga No. 01/SE/Db/2019. Project estimate = Σ(unit price × quantity) + PPN, with SMKK paid separately (Permen PUPR 10/2021). — [Kaltim training (SE 30/25)](https://sibikon-dpuprpera.kaltimprov.go.id/download-materi-acara/PengantarPermenPUPR0823_SEDirjenBinkons3025). This conflicts with the Permen 8/2023 body (10–15%), see Inferences.
- A 2025 academic study cites "Lampiran V - AHSP Bidang Bina Marga Nomor 30/SE/Dk/2025" as "AHSP Binamarga 2025". — [Suryaman & Wacono, PNJ SNTS 2025](https://prosiding.pnj.ac.id/index.php/snts/article/download/4889/3221/30517)
- Equipment efficiency (training): "Faktor manajemen kerja" is max 0.83 (50 effective minutes per hour), and "umumnya diambil 0,65 – 0,83". Modified hot mix uses about 1.5× the fuel of normal hot mix. — [Kaltim training materi-8](https://sibikon-dpuprpera.kaltimprov.go.id/download-materi-acara/materi-8)

**PPN**
- From 1 Jan 2025 the nominal rate is 12%. For non-luxury goods and services (PMK 131/2024 Pasal 3), the DPP is "nilai lain" = 11/12 of price, so the effective rate is **11%** (example: Rp100,000 → Rp11,000 PPN). Full 12% on full price applies only to luxury goods (Pasal 2). — [DDTCNews, 2 Jan 2025](https://news.ddtc.co.id/review/konsultasi/1807964/tarif-ppn-di-2025-jadinya-11-atau-12-begini-ketentuan-terbarunya). The article does not explicitly mention jasa konstruksi or government procurement.
- Kendari 2023 RAB applied PPN 11%. — [Sabara et al. 2024](https://medkons.uho.ac.id/index.php/journal/article/download/31/15/63)
- DDTC reported a 2026 "PMK omnibus" revising the DPP nilai lain / PPN besaran tertentu rules. Its content was not fetched. — [DDTCNews](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1808691/pmk-omnibus-terbit-revisi-aturan-dpp-nilai-lain-ppn-besaran-tertentu)

### Inferences
- Item 11.1.(9) is paid by volume (m³), so a PAVEMENT-PINN pothole estimate should convert to cut-out volume. It should then use the AHSP's internal per-m³ material (agregat, aspal), labor and equipment coefficients, or equivalently multiply by compacted density to tons and apply AC-WC/AC-BC per-ton prices.
- The Kendari prime-coat coefficient of 1.7775 liters emulsion per liter of pay item is consistent with the pay quantity being **residual bitumen** (1/1.7775 ≈ 56% residue). This is an inference, so confirm against the SE 30/2025 sheet.
- OHP conflict: the Permen body allows 10–15%, while Bina Marga training cites a 10% cap (SE DJBM 01/SE/Db/2019), and both academic RABs used 10%. A transparent calculator should default to **10% for Bina Marga road items** and expose it as a parameter up to the 15% legal maximum. It should flag any HPS above 15% as non-compliant.
- PPN should be a parameter, default 11% effective (12% × 11/12). Some contracts and documents still write "PPN 11%" or "12%".

### Gaps
- **Official coefficients for 11.1.(9) Perbaikan Campuran Aspal Panas, 11.1.(11) CPHMA repair, 7.1.(2a) tack coat, 7.2 laburan, 11.1.(4) LPA repair and any "galian perkerasan beraspal" item were not found.** They are in SE Dirjen Binkons 30/SE/Dk/2025 Lampiran V (Excel sheets, about 1,723 non-JBH sheets). The Permen PDF text extraction had blank lampiran pages. Next step: obtain the SE 30/2025 Lampiran V Excel from simantu.pu.go.id / binakonstruksi.pu.go.id / bit.ly/SEDJBKAHSP.
- Tabel A.2.d berat isi campuran beraspal (ton/m³ per mix) and loss factors (faktor kehilangan) exist in SE 73/2023 → SE 30/2025 Lampiran I/II, but the values were not retrieved.
- Whether the 2026 PMK omnibus changed the effective PPN on construction services was not verified.

## 2. Spesifikasi Umum Bina Marga 2018 Revisi 2: patching procedure, measurement & payment, thickness, density

### Takeaway
In Spesifikasi Umum 2018 (and Revisi 2) there is **no "Divisi 8 Pengembalian Kondisi & Pekerjaan Minor"**. That name belongs to the older Spec 2010 Rev 3. In Spec 2018, maintenance sits in the Pemeliharaan (Kinerja) division, and the AHSP pay item for asphalt patching is 11.1.(9) per m³. The patching procedure text (cut margins, tack-coat rate, density) could not be retrieved this session.

### Cited Findings
- Spesifikasi Umum 2018 was issued under SE No. 02/SE/Db/2018 (September 2018), with an October 2020 edition. Its table of contents shows Seksi 4.8 "Penambalan Dangkal" and 4.9 "Penambalan Penuh" (rigid JPCP), Seksi 6.1 "Lapis Resap Pengikat dan Lapis Perekat", and Seksi 10.1 "Pemeliharaan Kinerja Jalan" (pp. 10-1 to 10-19). — [JICA reference copy of Spesifikasi Umum 2018](https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/1000050082_02.pdf)
- In the AHSP list aligned to Spec 2018 Revisi 2, Perkerasan Aspal is **Divisi 7** and Pekerjaan Pemeliharaan is **Divisi 11**. Rigid patching moved to Divisi 5 (5.8/5.9), and asphalt patching is **11.1.(9) "Perbaikan Campuran Aspal Panas", m³**. — [AHSP Bina Marga Bagian III (Wonosobo copy)](https://disperkimhub.wonosobokab.go.id/media/upload/20220608095209_847.pdf)
- The Spec 2018 page for Revisi 2 is at binamarga.pu.go.id. — [Bina Marga: Spesifikasi Umum 2018 Revisi 2](https://binamarga.pu.go.id/index.php/peraturan/detail/spesifikasi-umum-2018-revisi-2) (the full text was not fetched).
- Minimum nominal layer thicknesses (as listed in a Bina Marga interim spec that cross-references Spec 2018): AC-WC 4.0, AC-BC 6.0, AC-Base 7.5. Thickness tolerances are −3.0 (AC-WC), −4.0 (AC-BC) and −5.0 (AC-Base). The OCR'd source rendered the units as "mm", but the values match layer thicknesses in cm and tolerances in mm. Binder: "Aspal Tipe I, yaitu aspal Pen 60/70". — [Bina Marga SKh-1.6.27 (2019)](https://binamarga.pu.go.id/uploads/files/1825/preview_1825-1-5.pdf)
- A Kota Bekasi road tender method statement (Spec-based) gives: placement temperature 130–150 °C; breakdown rolling with tandem roller at 125–145 °C (1–2 passes); intermediate rolling with PTR at 100–125 °C; finish rolling with tandem roller above 95 °C; 15 cm roller overlap. Tack-coat rate is set by "paper test" on site, and prime coat uses "aspal 80% : kerosin 20%". — [Kota Bekasi tender method (indotender)](https://indotender.com/tender/bekasikota/pl/10542223000)
- Literature on patching: full-depth patching removes all material in the damaged area and replaces it with fresh asphalt mix, and is suitable for permanent repair. Surface-only patching is temporary and "sebaiknya hanya digunakan untuk perkerasan dengan tebal minimal 10 cm". — [Thesis literature review (core.ac.uk)](https://core.ac.uk/download/489575217.pdf)

### Inferences
- PAVEMENT-PINN should cite **Spec 2018 Rev 2, Divisi 11 (Pemeliharaan) / Seksi 11.1** and **AHSP item 11.1.(9)** rather than "Divisi 8". The divisions renumbered between Spec 2010 Rev 3 → Spec 2018 → Spec 2018 Rev 2: Spec 2018 has Seksi 10.1 Pemeliharaan, and Rev 2 adds SMKK and Preventif divisions, so Pemeliharaan becomes Divisi 11. This chain is inferred from the two TOCs above.
- Because 11.1.(9) is paid in m³, the payable quantity is the compacted repaired volume. A practical rule: payable volume = (cut length × cut width) × compacted repair depth. Excess beyond the approved cut lines or design depth would be non-payable. This is the standard logic of unit-price contracts, but the exact Spec wording was not retrieved.

### Gaps
- **Not found:** the Spec 2018 Rev 2 Seksi 11.1 / 10.1 text on patching procedure. Specifically: the cut-out margin beyond the damage (the "~30 cm" rule could not be verified, so it should not be hard-coded as official), rectangular cut with vertical edges, tack-coat application rate (l/m²), lift thickness, compaction/density requirement (e.g., % of JMF/Marshall density), and the Pengukuran & Pembayaran clause.
- Not found: the tack-coat/prime-coat application rates from Seksi 6.1 (Spec 2018) / 7.1 (Rev 2).

## 3. Unit prices by region: HSPK/SSH, Pertamina asphalt, e-katalog, BPS IKK

### Takeaway
2026 asphalt prices jumped about 20%. Pertamina bulk asphalt ex-RU IV Cilacap went from **Rp9,400/kg (Mar 2026) to Rp11,565/kg (Apr 2026)**, and drum asphalt ex-Gresik from Rp11,600 to Rp13,600/kg. Regions recalculated RABs as a result. BPS IKK 2025 (reference **Surabaya = 100**) is the official regional construction-cost multiplier: Kab. Bogor 118.39, Kota Bandung 119.75, Kab./Kota Bekasi about 111, Papua Pegunungan 245.6. HSPK DKI / Jabar / Lampung / Karawang values were not retrieved.

## 4. Labor: UMP 2026 (38 provinces), UMK, AHSP labor categories, working hours

### Takeaway
UMP 2026 (PP 49/2025, effective 1 Jan 2026) ranges from **Rp2,317,601 (Jawa Barat) to Rp5,729,876 (DKI Jakarta)**. The AHSP converts monthly wage to hourly with **25 working days × 7 effective hours/day** (Permen PUPR 1/2022 lampiran). The resulting hourly rate must be at least the UMP, and Permen 8/2023 allows UMP or UMK as the basis.

## 5. Crew size, productivity and standard equipment for pothole patching / routine maintenance

### Takeaway
Official crew rules come from AHSP general provisions: pekerja:mandor 10:1 for mechanized and 20:1 for manual work, and at most 2 people per machine. Field crew data for pothole teams is sparse. Jakarta has about 750 PJLP Bina Marga workers (Feb 2026). Surabaya runs 9 task-force teams across 5 rayon, using 50–60 t of hot mix per day. No official m²/day productivity for patching was retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Permen 8/2023 Pasal 5(5) allows max 2 people per mechanical equipment unit. — [Permen PUPR 8/2023](https://peraturan.go.id/files/permen-pupr-no-8-tahun-2023.pdf)
- Labor ratios pekerja:mandor are 10:1 (mechanized) and max 20:1 (manual), with max 10 pekerja on mechanized work. — [Lampiran Permen PUPR 1/2022](https://disperkimhub.wonosobokab.go.id/media/upload/20220608095058_847.pdf)
- Jakarta: about **750 PJLP** in Dinas Bina Marga, with the Governor planning to add more (number undecided), 12 Feb 2026. About 6,000 potholes were handled in late Jan 2026, all as **temporary** patches ("Itu semua perbaikan sementara. Nanti akan kita permanenkan"). — [IDX Channel, 12 Feb 2026](https://www.idxchannel.com/news/kebut-perbaikan-lubang-jalan-jakarta-gubernur-dki-akan-tambah-pjlp-bina-marga/all); [JawaPos, 31 Jan 2026](https://www.jawapos.com/jabodetabek/2601310183/hampir-6000-jalan-berlubang-ditangani-akibat-cuaca-ekstrem)

- A defensible default patching crew for the calculator can be built from AHSP rules: 1 mandor per up to 10 pekerja (mechanized), with 1 operator + 1 helper for each machine (asphalt cutter, stamper/plate compactor, baby roller, dump truck driver + kenek). The **crew size should be derived from the AHSP coefficients** (jam per m³ × volume), not fixed headcounts.
- With 9 teams using 50–60 t/day, Surabaya's teams average about 5.5–6.7 t hot mix per team per day (arithmetic on the cited figures). That is roughly 2.4–2.9 m³ compacted per team-day if density is about 2.3 t/m³, but this density is an assumption, not a cited value.

## 6. Calculation formula and pothole-to-payable-quantity conversion

### Takeaway
The official structure is: HSP (unit price) = (Σ labor coef × wage + Σ material coef × material price + Σ equipment coef × equipment rate) × (1 + OHP). The cost estimate is Σ(HSP × quantity) + SMKK + PPN. For potholes, the pay quantity for 11.1.(9) is m³, so the measured damage must be converted to a cut-out volume. The official cut margin and waste factors were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Estimate = harga satuan per mata pembayaran × kuantitas → harga per mata pembayaran, summed to total, + PPN. SMKK is paid separately. HSD inputs: labor (HO/JO, qualifications); equipment (economic life, hours/year, purchase price, salvage value, interest, HP, fuel, lubricants); materials (quarry distance, base price, **faktor kembang susut**, **faktor kehilangan**). — [Kaltim training (SE 30/25)](https://sibikon-dpuprpera.kaltimprov.go.id/download-materi-acara/PengantarPermenPUPR0823_SEDirjenBinkons3025)
- Indirect cost is 10–15% of direct cost (Permen 8/2023 Pasal 11(3)). — [Permen PUPR 8/2023](https://peraturan.go.id/files/permen-pupr-no-8-tahun-2023.pdf)
- Equipment hours from productivity: work management factor max 0.83, typically 0.65–0.83. — [Kaltim training materi-8](https://sibikon-dpuprpera.kaltimprov.go.id/download-materi-acara/materi-8)
- Bina Marga visual-survey method used for damage quantification (Banda Aceh study): Np = damaged area / surveyed area × 100%, weights Nj (tambalan 4, retak 5, lubang 6), Nq = Np × Nj. — [Reza et al. 2025](https://ejournal.unida-aceh.ac.id/prince/article/download/1055/866)

### Inferences (proposed implementation; not official text)
- **Per-pothole quantity** (for 11.1.(9)): V_pay [m³] = (L_damage + 2m) × (W_damage + 2m) × d_repair. Here m is the cut margin, a parameter (official value not found), and d_repair = max(measured depth, minimum layer thickness of the mix). The source lists 4.0 for AC-WC and 6.0 for AC-BC as minimum thicknesses, which are cm-level per the SKh-1.6.27 table.
- Tonnage (if pricing by ton via 7.3.(5a)/(6a)): T = V_pay × ρ_compacted. Take ρ from SE 30/2025 Tabel A.2.d; it was not retrieved, so do not hard-code it.
- Tack coat: Q_tack [l] = (cut area + vertical wall area) × rate. The rate comes from the Spec (not retrieved) or a field paper test (per the Bekasi method statement).
- **Fair cost** = Σ_items [ (Σ_k coef_k × price_k,region,date) × (1 + OHP) × Q_item ] × (1 + PPN_eff). Defaults: OHP 10%, PPN_eff 11%. Price_k comes from the local HSPK/SSH, or from a reference HSPK × IKK_region/IKK_ref.
- **Mark-up indicator** for PAVEMENT-PINN: ratio = spent / fair. Flag when the ratio is above 1 plus a tolerance (e.g., the 15% legal OHP ceiling minus the default 10% leaves a 5% band, plus uncertainty in quantities and prices).

### Gaps
- Official cut margin beyond damage, waste/loss factor for hot mix in patching, and compacted density are all in SE 30/2025 / Spec 2018 Rev 2 and were not retrieved.

- The Banda Aceh figure is likely inflated by treating equipment hire as 1 hour per m² instead of using production rates. It is a useful example of the kind of mark-up the app should detect: equipment-hour coefficients far above AHSP productivity-based values.
- Using the about Rp1.25–1.6 juta/ton range for hot mix and an assumed density of about 2.3 t/m³ (assumption, not cited), the material-plus-placement cost of a 5 cm patch is roughly Rp145–185k/m² before cutting, haul and tack coat. Published per-m² figures (Rp705k Sidoarjo 2018) are therefore several times higher, implying substantial cutting/excavation, base repair, mobilization and small-quantity premiums. The calculator should itemize these separately rather than use one blended per-m² rate.
