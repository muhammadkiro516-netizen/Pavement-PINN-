## 1. Indonesian official document format (tata naskah dinas, kop, numbering, e-signature/QR), and whether a third-party app may use a government kop

### Takeaway
Permendagri 1/2023 ties each kop to the official who signs. The Kepala Daerah kop is for documents the Kepala Daerah signs. The Perangkat Daerah (e.g., Dinas PUPR) kop is for documents signed by other authorized officials. An electronic official document must carry a certified electronic signature shown as a QR code. A third-party app therefore should not produce a finished document on a government kop. The legally safer pattern has two outputs: (a) an independent citizen audit report on the app's own letterhead, and (b) an unbranded draft that the agency imports, puts on its own kop, numbers and signs itself.

### Cited Findings
- Permendagri No. 1 Tahun 2023 tentang Tata Naskah Dinas di Lingkungan Pemerintah Daerah is listed in the BPK JDIH database — [peraturan.bpk.go.id](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/245536). The Berita Negara file appears as BN 2023 No. 144 — [peraturan.go.id](https://peraturan.go.id/files/bn144-2023.pdf).
- **Two kop types (Pasal 22):** "kop Naskah Dinas jabatan Kepala Daerah" and "kop Naskah Dinas Perangkat Daerah" — [pasal.id, Permendagri 1/2023](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)
- **Who uses which kop:**
  - Pasal 23(1): the Kepala Daerah kop is for naskah signed by the Kepala Daerah. Pasal 23(2) extends this to penjabat, penjabat sementara, pelaksana tugas and pelaksana harian Kepala Daerah.
  - Pasal 24: the Perangkat Daerah kop is for naskah signed by authorized officials other than the Kepala Daerah.
  - Pasal 25: the kop's form and size are set in the lampiran.
  - Source for all three: [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)
- **Identity elements (shown in Pasal 50(2) on envelopes):**
  - Kepala Daerah: lambang negara + position name + address, "nomor telepon, faksimile, pos-el, laman, dan kode pos".
  - Perangkat Daerah: lambang daerah, name of the Pemerintah Daerah, name of the Perangkat Daerah, plus the same contact items.
  - Pasal 52(2) requires the Kepala Daerah map to carry the lambang negara in gold-yellow.
  - Source: [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)
- **Signing authority:** Pasal 64(1) says signing authority follows the official's duties and position. Pasal 65(1) allows delegation to subordinates — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)
- **Numbering:** Pasal 26 says numbering is "diatur sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan". Pasal 68(1)(a) says the unit tata usaha gives outgoing naskah its number, date and stamp. The fetched text did not show the number structure (classification code / sequence / unit code / year) — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)
- **Sifat, lampiran:**
  - Pasal 67 refers to sorting incoming naskah "sesuai dengan sifat surat", and Pasal 56 sets security classes.
  - Pasal 37(1): a lampiran is signed by the same official who signs the naskah.
  - Source: [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)
- **Berita acara and laporan are official document types:** Pasal 14 lists them as naskah dinas khusus (huruf d berita acara; huruf h laporan). The explanatory text describes a berita acara as recording the carrying-out of an activity at a set time and place, signed by the parties involved. A laporan notifies others of an activity or event — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)
- **Electronic naskah:**
  - Pasal 18(b) allows electronic recording media.
  - Pasal 20 names the permitted tools: an "aplikasi umum bidang kearsipan dinamis" or a word/data processor.
  - Pasal 43(2): electronic naskah use an electronic signature.
  - Pasal 45: (a) the signature appears as a "kode quick response" with the signer's name and position; (b) the naskah is distributed without printing; (d) it uses a sertifikat elektronik from a "penyelenggara sertifikasi elektronik Indonesia".
  - Pasal 46(2): no stamp is used on electronic naskah.
  - Source: [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)
- **Who issues certificates:**
  - BSSN issues government certificates through its Balai Sertifikasi Elektronik (BSrE), which serves "instansi pemerintah" (Perka LSN 10/2017, Pasal 2(1)).
  - Kominfo's PSrE list has both state and non-state providers.
  - PP 71/2019 Pasal 42(1) is quoted as "penyelenggaraan transaksi elektronik wajib menggunakan sertifikat elektronik".
  - Source: [Hukumonline (2020)](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/sertifikasi-elektronik-oleh-badan-sandi-dan-siber-negara-bssn-lt5eea2b0cd28c1)
- **Local rules vary:** Regencies issue their own Perbup/pedoman tata naskah dinas after Permendagri 1/2023, so kop details differ by location. Examples: [Kab. Puncak Jaya pedoman (2025)](https://puncakjayakab.go.id/storage/informasi_files/2025-10-13-03-38-22-pedoman-tata-naskah-dinas-dilingkungan-pemda-kabupaten-puncak-jaya.pdf); [Perbup Polewali Mandar 16/2024](https://jdih.polmankab.go.id/assets/uploads/2024PERBUBpolmankab16.pdf); [Perbup Demak (2024)](https://jdih.demakkab.go.id/prokumkab/download-abs/2024absperbup3321010.pdf)
- **Criminal exposure for documents made to look authentic:**
  - UU ITE Pasal 35 covers anyone who "dengan sengaja dan tanpa hak atau melawan hukum melakukan manipulasi, penciptaan, perubahan, penghilangan, pengrusakan Informasi Elektronik dan/atau Dokumen Elektronik dengan tujuan agar … dianggap seolah-olah data yang otentik".
  - The penalty under Pasal 51(1) is stated as up to 12 years in prison and/or a fine of up to Rp12 miliar.
  - Source: [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/hukum-menggunakan-identitas-orang-lain-lt6349420471173/)

### Inferences
- The kop is part of the official act, not decoration. Permendagri 1/2023 links kop use to a named, authorized signer (Pasal 23–24, 64), and the tata usaha unit assigns the number (Pasal 68). An app-made document showing a Dinas kop, a lambang daerah and a number, but not signed by the official, could be read as making a document "seolah-olah otentik" (UU ITE Pasal 35). The risk is highest if it carries a QR code that imitates a TTE (Pasal 45). **Recommendation: never render a government lambang or kop on a finished document.**
- **Two output types:**
  - **(A) "Laporan Audit Warga Independen / Independent Citizen Audit Report"**
    - Printed on PAVEMENT-PINN's own letterhead (organization name, legal entity, address, website, report ID).
    - Carries the footer "Dokumen ini bukan naskah dinas dan tidak diterbitkan oleh instansi pemerintah."
    - Addressed to the responsible agency ("Kepada Yth. Kepala Dinas PUPR Kabupaten X"), using the agency directory per location.
  - **(B) "Draf Berita Acara Pemeriksaan Lapangan untuk Instansi"**
    - An unbranded, watermarked draft ("DRAF — tidak sah tanpa penomoran dan penandatanganan pejabat berwenang").
    - Uses the Permendagri layout: placeholder kop region, nomor/sifat/lampiran/hal fields left blank, and a signature block.
    - Delivered as DOCX/JSON for the agency to import into its own TNDE/arsip system, then number and sign with a BSrE TTE.
  - In both cases the agency directory (name, address, phone, laman, email, kode pos per pemda/dinas) is used to **address** the document, never as the document's own kop.
- Pasal 14 already names berita acara and laporan as naskah dinas khusus. Structuring output (B) like a berita acara (time, place, parties, findings, signatures) lowers adoption friction for the agency.
- Pasal 20's "aplikasi umum bidang kearsipan dinamis" most likely refers to SRIKANDI (background, unverified). An integration path, if one exists, should target that system.

### Gaps
- The lampiran of Permendagri 1/2023 was not retrieved. It holds the exact kop layout, font/size and number format (kode klasifikasi / nomor urut / kode unit / tahun).
- The central-government rule was not verified. The brief's "PermenPANRB 6/2024" could not be confirmed. The general pedoman may instead be a Peraturan ANRI (e.g., Perka/Peraturan ANRI 5/2021, background, unverified). A Permen PUPR on tata naskah dinas was not found.
- UU 24/2009 (Lambang Negara) prohibitions and penalties, PP 77/2007 (Lambang Daerah), and KUHP forgery (Pasal 263 old KUHP / UU 1/2023 new KUHP, in force Jan 2026) were not verified because the search budget ran out. Get a lawyer's confirmation before shipping anything that shows a lambang.
- A Hukumonline clinic on Kepala Daerah e-signatures exists but was not read — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/tanda-tangan-naskah-dinas-kepala-daerah-lt6a017f473366a/)

## 2. What official construction-contract documents contain, and durations and liabilities relevant to auditing road repairs

### Takeaway
An audit record should place each road segment in its contract stage: before PHO, during the masa pemeliharaan, after FHO within the planned design life, or beyond it. That stage decides who must repair:
- **Before PHO and during maintenance:** the provider repairs at its own cost. Maintenance runs at least 6 months for permanent works (3 months semi-permanent), secured by a 5% maintenance guarantee or retention.
- **After FHO:** liability for "kegagalan bangunan" continues within the planned design life, capped at 10 years (UU 2/2017 Pasal 65).
- **Beyond that:** routine maintenance falls on the agency.

### Cited Findings
- **Handover (PHO):**
  - Perpres 16/2018 Pasal 57: after work is "selesai 100%", the provider requests handover in writing, the PPK inspects, and both sign a Berita Acara Serah Terima.
  - Pasal 58: the PPK hands the work to the PA/KPA, and the inspection results go into a Berita Acara.
  - Perpres 12/2021 removed PPHP/PjPHP. The PPK may now be assisted by a tim ahli, tim teknis or konsultan pengawas (the module cites "Pasal 8 huruf g").
  - Source: [Modul 04 Penyelesaian Kontrak Kerja Konstruksi, DPU Kutai Barat](https://pu.kutaibaratkab.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Modul_04_-_Penyelesian_Kontrak_Kerja_Konstruksi.pdf)
- **BA PHO contents:** references to the contract/SPK, inspection results, agreed scope and specifications, any remaining repairs, and the provider's jaminan pemeliharaan (value and validity) — [Modul 04 Kutai Barat](https://pu.kutaibaratkab.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Modul_04_-_Penyelesian_Kontrak_Kerja_Konstruksi.pdf)
- **FHO:**
  - The provider applies in writing before maintenance ends. The PPK and Tim Teknis inspect on site.
  - If the work is accepted, the PPK issues a BA, a certificate of end of maintenance, the final calculation, a BA FHO and a certificate of final completion.
  - The BA FHO references the contract and addenda, the BA PHO, the FHO request and inspection reports. It states that the maintenance obligations ran from one date to another and were completed.
  - Source: [Modul 04 Kutai Barat](https://pu.kutaibaratkab.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Modul_04_-_Penyelesian_Kontrak_Kerja_Konstruksi.pdf)
- **Minimum maintenance period:** "paling singkat untuk pekerjaan permanen adalah 6 (enam) bulan", 3 months for semi-permanent, both counted from PHO. The module gives no article number — [Modul 04 Kutai Barat](https://pu.kutaibaratkab.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Modul_04_-_Penyelesian_Kontrak_Kerja_Konstruksi.pdf)
- **Conflicting source (DJPb):** a Treasury article says the period is set in the contract and is usually "selama 3 bulan, 6 bulan atau paling lama 12 bulan", without citing an article — [DJPb KPPN Jakarta VII, "Retensi Bukan Pemeliharaan"](https://djpb.kemenkeu.go.id/kppn/jakarta7/id/data-publikasi/artikel/2885-retensi-bukan-pemeliharaan.html). The two framings ("minimum 6 months" vs "maximum 12 months") are not necessarily contradictory, but the binding article was not confirmed.
- **Guarantee or retention:**
  - "Besaran nilai Jaminan Pemeliharaan sebesar 5% (lima perseratus) dari nilai Kontrak". A 5% retention may be used instead.
  - The guarantee is valid "sampai dengan 14 (empat belas) hari kerja setelah masa pemeliharaan selesai".
  - Source: [Modul 04 Kutai Barat](https://pu.kutaibaratkab.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Modul_04_-_Penyelesian_Kontrak_Kerja_Konstruksi.pdf)
- **DJPb on maintenance:**
  - The guarantee runs from the first handover (PHO) to the second (FHO).
  - The provider must keep the work defect-free and repair defects caused by its own workmanship.
  - Damage caused by the user is not covered.
  - Source: [DJPb](https://djpb.kemenkeu.go.id/kppn/jakarta7/id/data-publikasi/artikel/2885-retensi-bukan-pemeliharaan.html)
- **Repair duty and sanctions:**
  - "penyedia wajib memperbaiki dan segala biaya yang dibutuhkan untuk perbaikan menjadi tanggung jawab penyedia". Force-majeure damage is shared.
  - If the provider fails, the PPK may withhold retention or claim the guarantee, and the provider faces blacklisting.
  - The PPK may also take over repairs or appoint another contractor at the provider's cost.
  - Source: [Modul 04 Kutai Barat](https://pu.kutaibaratkab.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Modul_04_-_Penyelesian_Kontrak_Kerja_Konstruksi.pdf)
- **Late penalty:** "denda keterlambatan senilai 1/1000 (satu permil)" (module cites Pasal 56(2)), calculated on contract value before PPN (Pasal 79(4)–(5)). The fetched extract did not explicitly confirm a per-day basis — [Modul 04 Kutai Barat](https://pu.kutaibaratkab.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Modul_04_-_Penyelesian_Kontrak_Kerja_Konstruksi.pdf)
- **Mutual checks and reports:**
  - MC-0 is the initial joint check of volumes against drawings and site conditions (setting out, drawings and calculation, cost).
  - An MC-50 may be done if there is an addendum. MC-100 is the final check.
  - Required reports: daily, weekly and monthly, plus progress photos at 0%, 50% and 100%.
  - Source: [Modul 04 Kutai Barat](https://pu.kutaibaratkab.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Modul_04_-_Penyelesian_Kontrak_Kerja_Konstruksi.pdf)
- **Building failure (UU 2/2017 Pasal 65):**
  - (1) The Penyedia Jasa "wajib bertanggung jawab atas Kegagalan Bangunan" within the rencana umur konstruksi.
  - (2) If the planned life exceeds 10 years, liability lasts "paling lama 10 (sepuluh) tahun" from final handover.
  - (3) After that, the Pengguna Jasa is responsible.
  - (4) The periods must be stated in the Kontrak Kerja Konstruksi. The page's rendering of this ayat was garbled, so check the gazette.
  - (5) Details are left to a PP.
  - Source: [api-law.crpg.info, UU 2/2017 Pasal 65](https://api-law.crpg.info/peraturan/uu/2/2017/pasal/65)
- **Perpres 46/2025** (second amendment to Perpres 16/2018) was enacted and promulgated on 30 April 2025.
  - It amends many articles, including Pasal 30, 33, 50, 52 and 59.
  - Pasal 30(1)(e) keeps "jaminan pemeliharaan" as a guarantee type. Pasal 30(5) exempts consultancy.
  - Pasal 52(1) lists "serah terima hasil pekerjaan" as a contract stage.
  - Pasal 9(1) gives the PA power to set "Sanksi Daftar Hitam". Pasal 1 angka 49 defines that sanction.
  - The fetched excerpt did not show any new maintenance-period text.
  - Sources: [pasal.id, Perpres 46/2025](https://pasal.id/peraturan/perpres/perpres-no-46-tahun-2025); [peraturan.bpk.go.id](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/318647)
- Base texts: [Perpres 16/2018](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/73586/perpres-no-16-tahun-2018); [Perpres 12/2021](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/161828/perpres)

### Inferences
- Each road segment / complaint record should carry these contract fields, taken only from public sources (SiRUP/LPSE/agency data, entered by a moderator with a source link):
  - paket/contract ID, penyedia (as an entity), contract value
  - PHO date, maintenance end date (PHO + contract maintenance period, default ≥ 6 months), FHO date
  - rencana umur konstruksi (if published)
  - status: `pra-PHO | masa pemeliharaan | pasca-FHO dalam umur rencana | di luar umur rencana | tidak diketahui`
- Damage reported **during the maintenance period** is the strongest actionable signal. The provider must repair at its own cost, and the PPK holds the 5% guarantee or retention. The app's letter can cite this neutrally: "Berdasarkan data publik, ruas ini diduga masih dalam masa pemeliharaan; mohon verifikasi oleh PPK."
- Post-FHO "kegagalan bangunan" has a technical and legal meaning, likely requiring an expert assessment (background, unverified). The app should never label damage "kegagalan bangunan". It should say "kerusakan dini yang perlu dinilai oleh pihak berwenang".
- The app's "Laporan Pemeriksaan Lapangan Warga" can mirror the BA PHO/FHO structure: reference to the package, date/time/place, method, observations per STA/segment, photos, parties present (citizen pseudonyms), and conclusion phrased as "indikasi".

### Gaps
- The exact Perpres article for the 6-month / 3-month minimum maintenance period was not confirmed (background suggests Perpres 16/2018 Pasal 50 or nearby). Perpres 46/2025 amended Pasal 50, so whether the maintenance rule changed must be checked against the full Perpres 46/2025 text.
- Permen PUPR 14/2020 (standard procurement documents) and Peraturan LKPP 12/2021 (procurement guidelines) were not retrieved. Road-specific maintenance periods (some road contracts use 12 months or longer, background, unverified) and the per-day penalty wording are therefore unconfirmed.
- PP 22/2020 (UU Jasa Konstruksi implementing rules, amended by PP 14/2021, background, unverified) was not retrieved. Rules on penilai ahli and procedures for kegagalan bangunan remain open.
- UU 2/2017 Pasal 60–64 and 66–67 were not retrieved.

## 3. How audits are actually done (BPK, APIP, BPKP): physical testing, finding categories, documentation, whistleblowing

### Takeaway
BPK physical testing of roads is sampling-based (uji petik), built on prior internal-control (SPI) testing:
- core drills at least 2 points per cross-section per lane, sections ≤100 m apart
- test pits
- measurements by STA
- results agreed with the parties in a BA Kesepakatan and a meeting record

Findings are expressed as quantity and quality shortfalls that turn into "kelebihan pembayaran". A citizen app cannot replicate this. Its value is in giving APIP or BPK well-located, time-stamped surface evidence that justifies an uji petik.

### Cited Findings
- **Basis and selection:** physical testing "harus dilakukan berdasarkan hasil pengujian Sistem Pengendalian Intern (SPI) yang memadai". Packages are chosen by the auditor's professional judgment — [BPK Perwakilan Maluku Utara, Buku Saku Pemeriksaan Pengujian Fisik Pekerjaan Konstruksi Jalan](https://malut.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/BUKU-SAKU-PEMERIKSAAN-PENGUJIAN-FISIK-PEKERJAAN-KONSTRUKSI-JALAN_SAKINAH-KAMARUDDIN.pdf)
- **Coring:**
  - Flexible pavement: "paling sedikit diambil pada 2 titik pengujian mewakili penampang melintang per lajur secara acak", with sections no more than 100 m apart.
  - Thickness is measured "minimal pada tiga sisi" of the core and averaged. Rigid pavement uses 9 measurements. The tool is a jangka sorong.
  - Test pits are used for subgrade and aggregate base. Density uses a sand cone (SNI 2828:2011) or a method agreed in the BA Kesepakatan.
  - Source: [BPK Malut Buku Saku](https://malut.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/BUKU-SAKU-PEMERIKSAAN-PENGUJIAN-FISIK-PEKERJAAN-KONSTRUKSI-JALAN_SAKINAH-KAMARUDDIN.pdf)
- **Length and width:**
  - Length is measured between STAs matching the core/test-pit points, with a tape, measuring wheel, or GPS tracking for estimates.
  - Width is measured perpendicular to the axis. If measured width exceeds design, the design width counts. If smaller, the measured width counts.
  - Source: [BPK Malut](https://malut.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/BUKU-SAKU-PEMERIKSAAN-PENGUJIAN-FISIK-PEKERJAAN-KONSTRUKSI-JALAN_SAKINAH-KAMARUDDIN.pdf)
- **Participants and agreement:**
  - The provider, on the supervisor's instructions, measures layer thickness. A tenaga ahli performs hammer tests, witnessed by the auditor and the parties.
  - Recognized volumes "harus didiskusikan dengan para pihak" and are recorded in a meeting record.
  - Methods are fixed beforehand in a "BA Kesepakatan Pengujian Fisik".
  - Source: [BPK Malut](https://malut.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/BUKU-SAKU-PEMERIKSAAN-PENGUJIAN-FISIK-PEKERJAAN-KONSTRUKSI-JALAN_SAKINAH-KAMARUDDIN.pdf)
- **Finding formula:** "Kelebihan pembayaran = (Volume Pembayaran − Volume diakui) × HS". Thickness and density shortfalls are handled with tiered payment factors (Tables 4–8). Quality non-conformity is grounds for repair or a payment reduction — [BPK Malut](https://malut.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/BUKU-SAKU-PEMERIKSAAN-PENGUJIAN-FISIK-PEKERJAAN-KONSTRUKSI-JALAN_SAKINAH-KAMARUDDIN.pdf)
- **Tolerances:**
  - AC-WC 4.0 cm nominal, tolerance −3.0 mm; AC-BC 6.0 cm, −4.0 mm; AC-Base 7.5 cm, −5.0 mm.
  - LFA Kelas A elevation +0/−1 cm; Kelas B +0/−2 cm; shoulders ±1.5 cm.
  - Hammer test: 10 points per area, at least 25 mm apart.
  - Source: [BPK Malut](https://malut.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/BUKU-SAKU-PEMERIKSAAN-PENGUJIAN-FISIK-PEKERJAAN-KONSTRUKSI-JALAN_SAKINAH-KAMARUDDIN.pdf)
- The booklet cites Keputusan BPK No. 3/K/I-XIII.2/5/2018 in its references (not retrieved) — [BPK Malut](https://malut.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/BUKU-SAKU-PEMERIKSAAN-PENGUJIAN-FISIK-PEKERJAAN-KONSTRUKSI-JALAN_SAKINAH-KAMARUDDIN.pdf)
- **Real-world follow-up:**
  - BPK NTB published a case titled "Rekanan Bayar Denda Kekurangan Volume Pekerjaan Pembangunan IGD RS Bima", showing that a volume shortfall leads to the contractor paying — [BPK NTB](https://ntb.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/IAAB_06_03_Rekanan-Bayar-Denda-Kekurangan-Volume-Pekerjaan-Pembangunan-IGD-RS-Bima_Rev-Ksb.pdf)
  - The Ombudsman highlighted BPK's kelebihan pembayaran findings on 15 DPUPR Bangka Tengah packages — [Ombudsman RI](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkmedia--ombudsman-soroti-temukan-bpk-soal-kelebihan-pembayaran-15-paket-proyek-dpupr-bangka-tengah)
  - Press reports BPK finding overpayment on 22 projects in Lombok Tengah. The amount was not verified; the headline slug reads "rp 42 miliar" — [detik](https://www.detik.com/bali/nusra/d-6795762/bpk-temukan-22-proyek-di-lombok-tengah-kelebihan-bayar-rp-42-miliar)
- **APIP definition** (Perpres 46/2025, Pasal 1 angka 22): "Aparat yang melakukan pengawasan melalui audit, reviu, pemantauan, evaluasi, dan kegiatan pengawasan lain" — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/perpres/perpres-no-46-tahun-2025)

### Inferences
- Map the app's observation categories to audit vocabulary, but phrase every category as an indication:
  - "indikasi ketidaksesuaian dimensi (lebar/tebal tampak)"
  - "indikasi kerusakan dini (retak/lubang/ambles) dalam masa pemeliharaan"
  - "indikasi pekerjaan belum dilaksanakan pada segmen STA x+xxx"
  - "Kelebihan pembayaran" is a conclusion only BPK, APIP or the PPK can make after measurement. The app must never state it.
- Use STA/segment referencing (or km-post plus GPS polyline) compatible with BPK practice. Record GPS accuracy, timestamp, device time vs server time, and photo orientation. These data let an auditor pick uji petik points.
- Low-cost citizen measurements (tape width, a ruler at a pothole edge, smartphone photos with a scale reference) can be logged as "pengukuran warga (indikatif)". They are not equivalent to core-drill evidence.

### Gaps
- Whistleblowing and complaint channels (KPK WBS/KWS, LKPP complaint system, SP4N-LAPOR!, the Inspektorat daerah) and the Perpres 16/2018 articles on oversight and public complaints (Pasal 76–77, background, unverified) were not verified because the search budget ran out.
- BPKP's role and standard APIP probity-audit formats were not retrieved.
- The full text of Keputusan BPK 3/K/I-XIII.2/5/2018 was not retrieved.

## 4. Tamper-evident technology options: pros, cons, cost, and use for procurement transparency

### Takeaway
The proportionate design is an append-only, hash-chained event log with Merkle-tree checkpoints:
- checkpoints signed by the operator and published
- each checkpoint anchored to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps (free, confirmed within hours)
- optionally, an RFC 3161 timestamp as a second, conventional anchor

Full public-blockchain or permissioned-ledger deployments add cost and complexity. The most thorough government pilot (WEF/Colombia) found the case for blockchain procurement "equivocal".

### Cited Findings
- **OpenTimestamps:**
  - Aggregates many hashes into a Merkle tree and anchors only the tip in a Bitcoin transaction, so "an unlimited number of timestamps" share one transaction and use is free.
  - Creating a timestamp takes about one second, but "it takes a few hours for the timestamp to get confirmed".
  - Once upgraded, a proof is verified "using nothing more than their local Bitcoin node". "It's Bitcoin, not the aggregation system, that proves the validity of a timestamp."
  - Limits: the public calendars are "a central point of failure" until proofs are upgraded (two calendars by default; self-hosting possible). A timestamp proves existence at a time, not authorship.
  - The author describes RFC 3161 and Certificate Transparency as possible additional notaries.
  - Source: [Peter Todd, OpenTimestamps announcement](https://petertodd.org/2016/opentimestamps-announcement)
- **Sigstore Rekor:**
  - "an append-only, tamper-evident record of signature metadata", built on Merkle trees.
  - Inclusion proofs can be checked offline and "stapled" to an artifact.
  - Signed tree heads "can be used to check that a given entry was in the log at a given time".
  - A public instance runs at rekor.sigstore.dev. Self-hosting is possible via Docker Compose, a Kubernetes operator or Helm, and requires Go, MySQL and Trillian ("an append-only log").
  - Independent monitors scan for anomalies.
  - Source: [Chainguard, What is Sigstore Rekor](https://www.chainguard.dev/supply-chain-security-101/what-is-sigstore-rekor)
- **Anchoring a transparency log to a public blockchain** is an established pattern, e.g., "Witnessing Sigstore's transparency log from the Ethereum blockchain" — [su3.io](https://su3.io/posts/witnessing-sigstore-from-ethereum) (title only; not read in full). An IETF SCITT draft on time anchors also exists — [draft-fassbender-scitt-time-anchor-05](https://ftp.kaist.ac.kr/ietf/draft-fassbender-scitt-time-anchor-05.xml) (not read).
- **WEF / Colombia pilot:**
  - The WEF worked with Colombia's Procuraduría General and the IDB. Universidad Nacional de Colombia built a proof-of-concept on public Ethereum to track supplier selection for the school-meals programme (PAE) in Medellín.
  - A "civic engagement strategy" let citizens and journalists comment and raise alerts.
  - It found "multiple challenges and unanticipated vulnerabilities" in permissionless networks.
  - Ethereum throughput (~15 tx/s) "is not currently suitable for a large-scale deployment". Vendor anonymity and privacy were the most notable challenges. Comment spam was a risk. "Integration and regulatory hurdles" caused delays.
  - The case was judged "equivocal", with a call for "cautious enthusiasm and thoughtful experimentation".
  - Sources: [Global Government Forum](https://www.globalgovernmentforum.com/colombian-blockchain-trial-cause-for-cautious-optimism-says-wef); [WEF report page](https://www.weforum.org/publications/exploring-blockchain-technology-for-government-transparency-to-reduce-corruption/)
- **ProZorro (Ukraine):** an EBRD Law in Transition 2025 article assesses the system's economic impact — [EBRD LiT 2025](https://www.ebrd.com/content/dam/ebrd_dxp/assets/pdfs/legal-transition/Law%20in%20Transition%20journal/LiTJ%202025/ebrd-lit25-the-impact-of-the-prozorro-procurement-system-on-the-ukrainian-economy.pdf). Open Contracting describes its "everyone sees everything" open-data principle — [Open Contracting Stories](https://medium.com/open-contracting-stories/everyone-sees-everything-fa6df0d00335). Neither was read in full.
- **Indonesia:** searches found only academic discussion of blockchain for government procurement and payment, not an operational government pilot:
  - "Sistem Pembayaran Lelang Pemerintah Berbasis Blockchain: Peluang dan Tantangan di Indonesia" — [Jurnal PKN STAN](https://jurnal.pknstan.ac.id/index.php/JIA/article/view/3532)
  - a UIN SGD thesis on a blockchain blind bid on layer 2 — [digilib UIN SGD](https://digilib.uinsgd.ac.id/117192/)
  - Unair on e-procurement digitalization challenges — [e-journal Unair](https://e-journal.unair.ac.id/YDK/article/view/51874)

### Inferences
Comparison of options. Costs are qualitative; public-chain fees were not verified.

| Option | Tamper-evidence | Trust model | Cost | Complexity | Notes for PAVEMENT-PINN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hash chain (each event stores prev_hash) | Detects edits, but the operator can rewrite the whole chain undetected unless heads are published externally | Operator | ~0 | Low | Necessary base layer; not enough alone |
| Merkle log with signed checkpoints (CT / Trillian / Rekor style) | Inclusion and consistency proofs; any fork is detectable by anyone holding an old checkpoint | Operator + monitors/witnesses | Low (self-host) | Medium | Best fit. Publish checkpoints on the verification page and mirror them to third parties (journalist/NGO/university) |
| OpenTimestamps anchoring of the daily Merkle root | Proves the root existed before Bitcoin block time | Bitcoin PoW | Free | Low | Recommended; latency of hours is fine for daily roots |
| RFC 3161 TSA token (background, unverified details) | A TSA-signed time on the hash | Trust in the TSA's certificate | Free to paid | Low | Familiar to lawyers and PKI auditors; check whether an Indonesian PSrE offers a TSA |
| Public smart-contract chain (Ethereum/Polygon) storing roots | Similar to OTS | Chain consensus | Per-transaction fees (volatile, unverified) | Medium | Little gain over OTS for anchoring; adds key management and a "crypto" perception risk |
| Permissioned ledger (Hyperledger Fabric) with agencies as nodes | Only as strong as the consortium's honesty | Consortium | High (ops) | High | Only makes sense if agencies join; overkill for an MVP |

- Never write personal data or photos to any immutable or public layer. Write only hashes of records whose content is stored separately and can be deleted. This avoids conflict with UU PDP Pasal 16(2)(g), the deletion principle (see section 6).
- The WEF's Colombia lessons on spam, anonymity and integration carry over directly to a citizen-reporting app: rate limiting and moderation matter more than ledger choice.

### Gaps
- RFC 3161, RFC 6962/9162 (Certificate Transparency) and Hyperledger Fabric documentation were not fetched. Their descriptions in the table are background knowledge.
- No free TSA list was verified (e.g., FreeTSA, DigiCert/Sectigo public timestamp endpoints, background, unverified). It is also unknown whether BSrE or Indonesian private PSrE offer RFC 3161 timestamping.
- Current Ethereum/Polygon fees were not checked.
- ProZorro's technical architecture (background: an open-data e-procurement platform, not mainly a blockchain) was not verified.
- No verified Indonesian government blockchain-procurement pilot was found.

## 5. Legal standing in Indonesia of electronic records, hashes, timestamps and e-signatures

### Takeaway
Under UU ITE Pasal 5, electronic information and its printouts are valid evidence that extends the recognized forms of proof. Commentators stress they are valid but rebuttable, not "perfect". Cryptographic anchoring is not a legally named instrument. It strengthens the integrity argument, likely through expert testimony. Attribution should come from a certified electronic signature by a licensed Indonesian PSrE. For agencies that is BSrE. The app operator would use a Kominfo/Komdigi-listed non-state PSrE.

### Cited Findings
- UU ITE Pasal 5(1): "Informasi Elektronik dan/atau Dokumen Elektronik dan/atau hasil cetaknya merupakan alat bukti hukum yang sah". Pasal 5(2): it is a "perluasan dari alat bukti yang sah sesuai dengan Hukum Acara yang berlaku di Indonesia". The author argues this makes such evidence valid but not "sempurna", since it can be rebutted — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/menguji-kekuatan-pembuktian-e-sertifikat-hak-atas-tanah-lt67d2de32caf8b?page=2)
- Hukumonline also reports that electronic evidence "sering kandas" (often fails) over a drafting issue in the law — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/bukti-elektronik-sering-kandas-akibat-frasa-nya-lt586531f00aa40) (title only; not read).
- **E-signature certificates:**
  - PP 71/2019 Pasal 42(1): electronic transactions "wajib menggunakan sertifikat elektronik" from an Indonesian PSrE.
  - Pasal 1 angka 21 defines a PSrE as a trusted legal entity that issues and audits certificates.
  - BSrE (under BSSN) serves government agencies. Kominfo lists both state and non-state PSrE.
  - Permenkominfo 11/2018 lets a PSrE verify identity (NIK, passport, NPWP) with consent.
  - Source: [Hukumonline (2020)](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/sertifikasi-elektronik-oleh-badan-sandi-dan-siber-negara-bssn-lt5eea2b0cd28c1)
- Permendagri 1/2023 Pasal 45 requires an electronic naskah dinas to use a certificate from an Indonesian PSrE, shown as a QR code with the signer's name and position — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)

### Inferences
- **For the app's own reports:** sign the PDF (PAdES) with a certified TTE issued to the operating legal entity, or to a named responsible person, by a licensed non-state PSrE. Add the app's own QR code that links to the public verification page.
  - Do not style the QR code like a government TTE QR.
  - Label it "Verifikasi integritas laporan PAVEMENT-PINN".
- **Evidence chain for the integrity claim:** event hash → Merkle inclusion proof → signed checkpoint → OpenTimestamps Bitcoin attestation (+ optional RFC 3161 token). This chain lets an independent digital-forensics expert show the record has not changed since time T. That supports the "keutuhan" (integrity) argument but does not by itself prove the content is true.
- Indonesian courts' acceptance of blockchain anchoring is untested in the sources found. Present it as supplementary technical evidence, not as legal certification.

### Gaps
- The text of UU ITE Pasal 5(3)–(4), Pasal 6 and Pasal 11, and PP 71/2019 Pasal 59–60 (certified vs uncertified signatures and their legal effect) was not retrieved.
- The current list of licensed non-state PSrE (Komdigi) and whether any offers RFC 3161 timestamping were not checked.
- No Indonesian case law on blockchain or hash evidence was found.

## 6. Personal data (UU 27/2022 PDP): citizen names, photos (faces, plates), location; minimization, consent, retention

### Takeaway
Reporter names and identifying combinations are personal data. Biometric data is "specific" (sensitive) data. A private operator needs a lawful basis under Pasal 20(2): explicit consent for reporters, and likely legitimate interest for incidental bystanders and plates. It must minimize data, set retention and deletion, run a DPIA for high-risk processing, secure data, notify breaches within 3×24 hours, and probably appoint a DPO because the processing is large-scale, public-facing monitoring. The two-year transition period ended in October 2024, so the law is fully in force.

### Cited Findings
- Scope covers "Setiap Orang, Badan Publik, dan Organisasi Internasional". Purely personal or household processing is exempt (Pasal 2(2)) — [pasal.id, UU 27/2022](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)
- **Pasal 4 data types:**
  - Specific: health, **biometric**, genetic, criminal records, child data, personal financial data, others.
  - General: full name, sex, citizenship, religion, marital status, and combined data that identifies a person.
  - Source: [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)
- **Pasal 16(2) principles:**
  - (a) collection "terbatas dan spesifik, sah secara hukum, dan transparan"
  - (b) processing consistent with its purpose
  - (g) destruction or deletion after the retention period or at the data subject's request, unless law requires otherwise
  - Source: [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)
- **Pasal 20(2) lawful bases:**
  - (a) "persetujuan yang sah secara eksplisit"
  - (b) contract
  - (c) legal obligation
  - (d) vital interests
  - (e) public interest, public service or official authority
  - (f) other legitimate interests, balanced against data-subject rights
  - Source: [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)
- **Risk and security:**
  - Pasal 34(1): a DPIA is required where processing "memiliki potensi risiko tinggi".
  - Pasal 35–39: security matched to risk, confidentiality, supervision of processors, prevention of unauthorized access.
  - Pasal 46: written breach notice within "3 x 24 (tiga kali dua puluh empat) jam".
  - Source: [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)
- **DPO:** Pasal 53(1) requires a DPO for processing that serves public services, involves large-scale regular and systematic monitoring, or involves large-scale specific data. MK decision 151/PUU-XXII/2024 found the word "dan" in Pasal 53(1)(b) conditionally unconstitutional — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)
- **Criminal provisions:**
  - Pasal 65 prohibits unlawfully obtaining, collecting, disclosing or using another person's personal data.
  - Pasal 67 penalties: up to 5 years and/or Rp5 miliar (collection or use) and 4 years and/or Rp4 miliar (disclosure).
  - Pasal 66/68: falsifying personal data, up to 6 years and/or Rp6 miliar.
  - Source: [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)
- **Transition:** Pasal 74 gave 2 years from promulgation (17 Oct 2022), ending about 17 Oct 2024 — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022). Official PDF: [JDIH Kemenkeu](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/download/46c17b53-4a45-411c-9b76-c6799e5769fc/UU_Nomor_27_Tahun_2022%20Perlindungan%20Data%20Pribadi.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Reporter identity:** collect nothing by default. Use a random pseudonymous ID. Ask for a real name only at the moment a certificate or signed report is requested, with explicit, separate consent (Pasal 20(2)(a)) and a clear statement of whether the name will be shown publicly.
- **Photos:**
  - Faces of bystanders and licence plates are personal data. Facial images become "biometric" if processed for identification. The exact Penjelasan definition was not verified.
  - Automatically blur faces and plates before any public display or report export.
  - Keep originals encrypted with restricted access, only for verification by an authorized auditor on request.
  - Strip EXIF data except capture time and GPS, which are stored separately as structured fields.
- **Location:** keep the damage location (snapped to the road) and drop the reporter's raw track and home-adjacent points. Do not keep continuous location.
- **Retention:** set and publish retention, e.g., raw originals deleted N months after the case closes. Hash commitments stay in the log, but the content behind them can be deleted ("crypto-shredding"). The integrity log should commit to hashes of salted or encrypted blobs only, so deletion does not break the chain.
- **Lawful basis for a private operator:** Pasal 20(2)(e) (public interest / official authority) most naturally fits public bodies. A citizen-run platform should rely on consent (reporters) and legitimate interest with a documented balancing test (bystanders/plates). This is an interpretation and needs legal review.
- Do a DPIA before launch, as high-risk processing is likely given public exposure and location data. Plan for a DPO.

### Gaps
- The Penjelasan definition of "data biometrik" and the Pasal 1 definition of personal data were not retrieved.
- Implementing PP status, establishment of the PDP agency, and any Pasal 15/50-type exemptions (e.g., for public-interest or state-administration processing, background, unverified) were not retrieved.
- The DPR is processing an amendment bill to UU 27/2022 — [DPR library](https://perpustakaan.dpr.go.id/sipinter/index/detail/id/220). Its content and status as of Nov 2026 are unknown.

## 7. Defamation and legal risk (UU ITE as amended by UU 1/2024; MK rulings) and neutral wording

### Takeaway
After UU 1/2024 and MK decision 105/PUU-XXII/2024, criminal defamation under UU ITE Pasal 27A protects **individuals only**, not government agencies, institutions, corporations, professions or positions. It is a complaint-based offense that only the individual can file. Risk remains when a person is named or identifiable, or when unprovable accusations are made, which is "fitnah" with heavier penalties. Civil tort claims also remain possible. Reports should describe observable conditions about roads, packages and entities, frame everything as an "indikasi" for verification, and never accuse named people.

### Cited Findings
- Pasal 27A (UU 1/2024): "Setiap orang dengan sengaja menyerang kehormatan atau nama baik orang lain dengan cara menuduhkan suatu hal" with intent that it becomes publicly known, via electronic information or documents. It replaced the old Pasal 27(3) — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/perubahan-penting-soal-pencemaran-nama-baik-di-uu-ite-baru-lt65a90c5004886/?page=all)
- **Penalties, as stated by a lawyer quoted by Hukumonline (not statutory text):**
  - Defamation: 2 years (down from 4) and up to Rp400 juta.
  - Fitnah under Pasal 45(6), for unprovable accusations: up to 4 years and Rp750 juta.
  - Source: [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/perubahan-penting-soal-pencemaran-nama-baik-di-uu-ite-baru-lt65a90c5004886/?page=all)
- **MK 105/PUU-XXII/2024** (reported 30 April 2025), a partial grant of review of Pasal 27A jo. 45(4):
  - "orang lain" means individuals only. The offense does not apply to government agencies, institutions, corporations, professions or positions.
  - It is to be read with KUHP Pasal 310(1).
  - It is a delik aduan that only the defamed individual can file; a legal entity cannot file through a representative.
  - Criticism is a form of oversight tied to the public interest.
  - Sources: [Bloomberg Technoz](https://www.bloombergtechnoz.com/detail-news/69820/mk-pencemaran-nama-baik-uu-ite-tak-berlaku-ke-lembaga-pemerintah); corroborated by [Suara Surabaya](https://www.suarasurabaya.net/kelanakota/2025/mk-putuskan-lembaga-pemerintah-tak-bisa-ajukan-laporan-pencemaran-nama-baik/)
- Further MK challenges to defamation articles in both the KUHP and UU ITE were pending in 2025–26 — [MKRI news](https://www.mkri.id/berita/tifa,-roy,-dan-rismon-uji-pasal-pencemaran-nama-baik-dalam-kuhp-dan-uu-ite-24584) (title only; not read).
- Civil tort (KUH Perdata Pasal 1365) is a separate route for claims over misuse of identity or information — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/hukum-menggunakan-identitas-orang-lain-lt6349420471173/)

### Inferences
- **Risk tiers:**
  - Lowest: describing road conditions and citing public contract data about an agency or company.
  - Higher: naming the PPK, kepala dinas or a contractor's director, or implying a crime ("korupsi", "mark-up", "fiktif", "maling").
  - Fitnah exposure arises when the app asserts things it cannot prove.
- **Wording rules:**
  - Use "indikasi", "diduga", "berdasarkan data yang dihimpun warga", "perlu verifikasi/pemeriksaan oleh instansi berwenang", "tanpa mengurangi asas praduga tak bersalah".
  - Refer to roles and entities from official public data ("PPK paket X", "penyedia: PT Y sesuai data LPSE"), not to private persons.
  - Moderate free-text comments and block personal names and insults.
- **Suggested standard paragraph (ID):** "Laporan ini disusun dari data yang dicatat warga melalui PAVEMENT-PINN dan data publik yang dirujuk. Laporan ini memuat indikasi kondisi lapangan yang memerlukan verifikasi oleh instansi berwenang, dan bukan merupakan kesimpulan audit, penilaian kerugian negara, maupun tuduhan terhadap pihak mana pun. Seluruh pihak dihormati haknya berdasarkan asas praduga tak bersalah."
- **Right of reply:** add a per-report channel where the agency or contractor can respond. Log responses as new events, which shows good faith.

### Gaps
- The text of Pasal 45(4)–(7) (including any public-interest or self-defence exception), Pasal 28(3), and the new KUHP (UU 1/2023) defamation articles in force since Jan 2026 was not retrieved.
- The outcome of the pending MK challenges is unknown.

## 8. Citizen identity without login: certificates, anti-abuse, privacy trade-offs

### Takeaway
Few sources were found, so this section is mostly design inference. A tiered approach balances PDP minimization (Pasal 16(2)(a)) against abuse:
- anonymous pseudonymous reporting
- optional phone OTP for higher trust
- a name entered only when a certificate or signed statement is issued

The WEF Colombia pilot showed comment spam and anonymity are real problems in open civic systems.

### Cited Findings
- PDP requires collection to be "terbatas dan spesifik" (Pasal 16(2)(a)) and specific consent where consent is the basis (Pasal 20(2)(a)) — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)
- The WEF Colombia pilot flagged anonymity/privacy and comment spam (legitimate comments drowned out by fraudulent or low-value ones) as key challenges — [Global Government Forum](https://www.globalgovernmentforum.com/colombian-blockchain-trial-cause-for-cautious-optimism-says-wef)
- Licensed PSrE may verify identity via NIK, passport or NPWP with consent (Permenkominfo 11/2018). That is the route if a citizen wants a legally attributable signed statement — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/sertifikasi-elektronik-oleh-badan-sandi-dan-siber-negara-bssn-lt5eea2b0cd28c1)

### Inferences
- **Tier 0, anonymous:**
  - A random device-held keypair creates a pseudonymous ID (e.g., "Warga-7F3K").
  - Each report is signed with the device key, so later edits are provably by the same reporter, without identity.
  - Rate limits apply per key, per IP and per area, plus a proof-of-work or CAPTCHA on submit.
- **Tier 1, phone-verified (optional):**
  - Phone OTP raises the trust weight and lets corrections be claimed after device loss.
  - Store only a salted hash of the number for de-duplication. The number itself is personal data, and OTP costs money.
- **Tier 2, named certificate:**
  - At certificate time, the user types a display name, giving explicit consent and choosing public or private display.
  - The certificate shows the name plus pseudonymous ID and report hashes.
  - The certificate states the name was "dinyatakan sendiri" (self-declared, not verified).
- **Tier 3, verified signature:** for a citizen who wants to sign a formal complaint, hand off to a licensed PSrE e-signature. The app never handles the NIK.
- **Device fingerprinting:** likely personal data and a transparency problem. Prefer a device keypair plus rate limits over covert fingerprinting. If fingerprinting is used, disclose it, minimize it and expire it.
- **Corroboration over identity:** weight reports by independent corroboration (multiple pseudonyms, different days, matching photos) rather than by identity.

### Gaps
- No Indonesian guidance was found on the legality of device fingerprinting or OTP verification for civic apps, or on PDP agency views on pseudonymous data.

## 9. Recommended minimal viable design (synthesis)

### Takeaway
Build one non-editable source of truth: an append-only, signed, hash-chained event log with daily Merkle checkpoints anchored via OpenTimestamps (and optionally RFC 3161). Generate every document only from a checkpointed snapshot, never from editable fields. Issue two document classes:
- an independent citizen audit report on PAVEMENT-PINN letterhead, with a certified PSrE e-signature and a verification QR code
- an unbranded "draft for agency" that the agency puts on its kop and signs with BSrE

Keep personal data off the immutable layer and phrase all findings as indications.

### Cited Findings
- **Legal anchors:**
  - The kop is tied to the authorized signer (Permendagri 1/2023 Pasal 23–24, 64) — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)
  - Electronic naskah dinas need a PSrE certificate shown as a QR code (Pasal 45) — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permendagri-no-1-tahun-2023)
  - Making documents "seolah-olah otentik" is criminal (UU ITE Pasal 35/51) — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/hukum-menggunakan-identitas-orang-lain-lt6349420471173/)
  - Electronic records are valid evidence (UU ITE Pasal 5) — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/menguji-kekuatan-pembuktian-e-sertifikat-hak-atas-tanah-lt67d2de32caf8b?page=2)
- **Technical anchors:**
  - OpenTimestamps is free Bitcoin anchoring with independent verification — [petertodd.org](https://petertodd.org/2016/opentimestamps-announcement)
  - Rekor-style Merkle transparency logs with signed checkpoints and inclusion proofs — [Chainguard](https://www.chainguard.dev/supply-chain-security-101/what-is-sigstore-rekor)
  - The WEF found the case for full blockchain procurement "equivocal" — [GGF](https://www.globalgovernmentforum.com/colombian-blockchain-trial-cause-for-cautious-optimism-says-wef)
- **Content anchors:**
  - BA PHO/FHO elements, maintenance ≥6 months, 5% guarantee — [Modul 04 Kutai Barat](https://pu.kutaibaratkab.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Modul_04_-_Penyelesian_Kontrak_Kerja_Konstruksi.pdf)
  - 10-year cap on building-failure liability — [UU 2/2017 Pasal 65](https://api-law.crpg.info/peraturan/uu/2/2017/pasal/65)
  - BPK STA-based uji petik — [BPK Malut](https://malut.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/BUKU-SAKU-PEMERIKSAAN-PENGUJIAN-FISIK-PEKERJAAN-KONSTRUKSI-JALAN_SAKINAH-KAMARUDDIN.pdf)
- **Privacy anchors:** minimization, deletion, DPIA, 3×24h breach notice (UU PDP Pasal 16, 34, 46) — [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)

### Inferences
1. **Event log (single source of truth)**
   - Every action is an immutable event: report created, photo added, moderator verified, contract data linked, agency response, correction, retraction.
   - Each event holds a canonical JSON serialization (e.g., RFC 8785 JCS, background), `prev_hash`, `event_hash = SHA-256(canonical)`, `actor_pseudonym`, `actor_signature` (Ed25519 device or moderator key) and `server_received_at`.
   - Photos are stored as content-addressed blobs (SHA-256). The event commits to the hash of the blurred public version and to the hash of the encrypted original.
2. **Checkpoints**
   - Every 24 hours (and on demand before a report is issued), compute a Merkle root over all events, as in RFC 6962/Rekor.
   - Sign a checkpoint (tree size, root, time) with the operator key.
   - Publish it on a public verification page and in a public Git repository or mirror. Invite 1–2 independent witnesses (university, NGO, journalist) to co-sign.
3. **Anchoring**
   - Submit each checkpoint root to OpenTimestamps (free; upgrade the proof after a few hours). Optionally get an RFC 3161 token from a TSA, preferably an Indonesian PSrE if one offers it (unverified).
   - Avoid storing data on smart contracts. An optional Polygon/Ethereum root write adds cost with little gain over OTS.
4. **Document generation (non-editable)**
   - The PDF is rendered deterministically from snapshot `(tree_size N, root R)`.
   - It embeds the report ID, the event hashes it relies on, inclusion proofs, R, the OTS proof reference and a generation timestamp.
   - Its SHA-256 is computed and itself logged as an event ("report issued").
   - A QR code points to `verify.<domain>/r/<report-id>`. The page recomputes the hash of an uploaded PDF, checks the inclusion proofs and shows the OTS/Bitcoin attestation.
   - The PDF is signed (PAdES) with the operator's certified TTE from a licensed non-state PSrE.
   - There are no UI fields to edit text. Narrative comes from templates filled only with logged values. Corrections create a new event and a new report version, with "menggantikan versi …" printed on it.
5. **Two document classes**
   - **(A) Laporan Audit Warga Independen:** PAVEMENT-PINN letterhead and a "bukan naskah dinas" disclaimer. Addressed to the responsible agency using a per-location agency directory (official name, address, phone, laman, email, kode pos from that pemda's published data). Never shows a lambang negara or lambang daerah.
   - **(B) Draf Berita Acara / Nota untuk Instansi:** neutral layout following Permendagri 1/2023 element order (kop placeholder, nomor/sifat/lampiran/hal blank, body, signature block blank). Watermarked "DRAF". Exported as DOCX/JSON plus the evidence bundle, for the agency to finalize in its own TNDE/SRIKANDI and sign with BSrE TTE. When the agency signs, the app logs the agency-issued document's hash as an "agency response" event.
6. **Contract-stage logic:** each segment is linked to package data and shows the stage (pre-PHO / masa pemeliharaan / pasca-FHO dalam umur rencana / lainnya), with sources. Letters cite the maintenance obligation neutrally when applicable.
7. **Privacy by design**
   - Pseudonymous IDs and optional self-declared names at certificate time.
   - Face and plate blurring, EXIF stripping, road-snapped locations, published retention.
   - DPIA before launch, a DPO, and a 3×24h breach playbook.
   - No personal data in the log or on any chain: hashes only, plus crypto-shredding.
8. **Wording and right of reply**
   - Indication-only vocabulary, no naming of individuals, and the standard disclaimer paragraph (section 7).
   - Agency and contractor responses are logged as events and shown alongside the report.
9. **Before launch:** get Indonesian counsel to review lambang/kop use (UU 24/2009, PP 77/2007, KUHP), the Pasal 20 lawful basis, and defamation exposure under the new KUHP.

### Gaps
- Whether any Indonesian agency would accept imported drafts, or integrate via SRIKANDI APIs, is unknown.
- The availability and cost of PSrE TTE for an organization and of RFC 3161 TSA in Indonesia were not verified.
- No Indonesian legal opinion on the evidentiary weight of OTS or Merkle proofs was found.
