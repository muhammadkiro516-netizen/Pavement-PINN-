## 1. National road-condition statistics (national / provincial / kabupaten-kota), trend, and kemantapan

### Takeaway
National roads are mostly fine (about 93–95% "mantap"). The damage sits in **regional roads**. In BPS data for 2024, **33% of kabupaten/kota roads (about 142,500 km) and 21.7% of provincial roads (about 13,000 km) were "rusak berat"**. Adding "rusak" and "rusak berat" across all three levels gives roughly **204,000 km of damaged road, about 38% of the network**, up from 31.9% in the 2021 data.

### Inferences
- The BPS 2024 table matches PUPR's kemantapan figures: baik + sedang is 95.2% national, 71.6% provincial and 57.2% kab/kota, against PUPR's 92 / 72 / 58 (my calculation).
- 2024 totals (my calculation from the GoodStats table): rusak + rusak berat ≈ 2.3k km national + 16.9k provincial + 185.2k kab/kota ≈ **204k km of ≈ 539.5k km ≈ 38%**. The 2021 figure was 174,298 km (31.91%). That looks like worsening, but BPS/PU may have changed survey coverage or method between editions, so check before calling it a trend.
- Kab/kota rusak berat almost doubled, from 79,256 km (2021) to about 142,548 km (2024). A jump that size is suspicious. Confirm it against the BPS table before using it as a headline number.
- Message for the pitch: the problem is mainly **local roads, the ones citizens use every day**. Responsibility for them is split across 500+ local governments, and that is where reporting and audit tools are missing.
- Possible conflict: Lasarus's "about 97%" earlier kemantapan does not fit the 92.2% (2020) and 92% (2023) figures. It may refer to a different year or basis. Quote it as his claim, not as fact.

### Gaps
- I could not open BPS's own table pages or the Bina Marga kemantapan time series for 2023, 2024 or 2025. The year-by-year national trend therefore rests on the 2020, 2023 and 2026 points only.
- No Kemendagri dataset on regional road condition was found.
- I did not check whether BPS's 2024 "rusak berat" kab/kota figure uses the same method as 2021.

## 2. Definitions: kerusakan ringan vs berat; SDI / IRI / PCI; distress types; why small potholes kill motorcyclists

### Takeaway
BPS classifies road condition by passable speed (rusak berat = 0–20 km/h). Bina Marga practice uses the Surface Distress Index (SDI, which counts cracks, potholes and ruts) together with IRI roughness. I found **no Indonesian standard that sets a "dangerous to motorcyclists" pothole size**. The fatal potholes in the news were **15–25 cm deep**.

### Cited Findings
- **BPS definitions:** "rusak" = passable at 20–40 km/h; "rusak berat" = passable at 0–20 km/h. — [Kumparan, 8 May 2023](https://kumparan.com/kumparannews/31-91-jalan-di-indonesia-rusak-paling-parah-di-ntt-riau-dan-papua-barat-20McV5n5wJj). BPS also says rusak berat roads need foundation repair. — [Databoks, 7 Dec 2025](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/utilities/statistics/692fea0141d54/distribution-of-severely-damaged-regencycity-roads-in-indonesia-2024)
- **SDI condition classes (Bina Marga):** baik SDI < 50; sedang 50–100; **rusak ringan 100–150; rusak berat > 150**. SDI is calculated in four steps:
  1. **Total crack area:** none = 0; <10% = 5; 10–30% = 20; >30% = 40.
  2. **Average crack width:** if >3 mm, SDI is doubled.
  3. **Number of potholes:** <10/km adds 15; 10–50/km adds 75; >50/km adds 225.
  4. **Rut depth:** <1 cm adds 2.5; 1–3 cm adds 10; >3 cm adds a value the paper prints as 2. That is lower than the 1–3 cm value, so it is probably a typo. Check it against the Bina Marga manual; the correct value was not verified here.

  The paper cites Dept. PU / Ditjen Bina Marga (2011), *Manual Konstruksi dan Bangunan: Tata Cara Pemeliharaan Jalan*, and Ditjen Bina Marga (2021), *Manual Aplikasi Sistem Program Pemeliharaan Jalan Provinsi/Kabupaten*. — [Jurnal Konstruksia (UMJ) Vol. 15 No. 1, Dec 2023](https://jurnal.umj.ac.id/index.php/konstruksia/article/download/18794/9675)
- **IRI condition bands (Roadlab Pro, cited in the same paper):** <2 m/km very good; 2–4 good; 4–6 fair; 6–10 poor; >10 very poor. — [Jurnal Konstruksia, Dec 2023](https://jurnal.umj.ac.id/index.php/konstruksia/article/download/18794/9675)
- **In that paper's survey,** cracking (15.45% of area) and potholes (2.42%) were the dominant SDI distresses. — [Jurnal Konstruksia, Dec 2023](https://jurnal.umj.ac.id/index.php/konstruksia/article/download/18794/9675)
- **Depth of potholes in fatal cases:** Pasar Kemis (Tangerang) holes were **15–25 cm deep**. — [VOI, Feb 2026](https://voi.id/en/amp/560365). **[OLDER]** A hole at the Ciputat flyover was estimated by a resident at **15–20 cm** deep and wide; it "started small and grew". — [detikNews, 12 Oct 2022](https://news.detik.com/berita/d-6342994/viral-jalan-rusak-di-flyover-ciputat-bikin-pemotor-tewas-kecelakaan)
- **How these crashes happen:** riders hit the pothole, lose control and fall into the lane, where a following or oncoming heavy vehicle runs them over. This happened at Pasar Kemis (milling truck, dump truck), Karanganyar (dump truck) and Wonogiri (bus). — [VOI, Feb 2026](https://voi.id/en/amp/560365); [detikJateng, 20 Oct 2025](https://www.detik.com/jateng/berita/d-8169590/mbah-nardi-tewas-tertabrak-truk-usai-terperosok-jalan-berlubang-di-karanganyar); [detikJateng, 30 Jan 2025](https://www.detik.com/jateng/berita/d-7755343/pemotor-tewas-usai-jatuh-dan-ditabrak-bus-di-jalan-berlubang-wonogiri/amp)
- **Patches (tambalan) are themselves a hazard:** at Matraman, patched sections were uneven, and a witness said crashes happen there often. — [JawaPos, 9 Feb 2026](https://www.jawapos.com/jabodetabek/2602090127/tragedi-matraman-raya-siswa-smkn-34-meninggal-dunia-usai-motor-hantam-jalan-berlubang)

### Inferences
- SDI is a strong anchor for PAVEMENT-PINN. Its inputs (crack area, crack width, potholes per km, rut depth) are things a phone photo plus a physics-informed model could estimate. The app's audit score could be mapped to SDI and IRI classes so that results speak Bina Marga's language.
- The deadliest pattern is a **small pothole plus a heavy vehicle close behind or oncoming**. A pothole that would be "rusak ringan" on an SDI sheet can still kill a motorcyclist.

### Gaps
- I did not obtain the primary Bina Marga documents: SDI manual (2011), Manual Desain Perkerasan Jalan (2017 / revised 2024), and the Pd T / SE Menteri on road condition surveys and distress catalogues. The full Indonesian distress catalogue therefore remains unverified here: retak buaya, retak memanjang/melintang, alur, amblas, pelepasan butir, tambalan, kerusakan tepi.
- I did not verify Bina Marga's own IRI bands for kemantapan. The commonly quoted <4 / 4–8 / 8–12 / >12 m/km were not confirmed in this research.
- I found no engineering or medical study that sets a pothole depth or width threshold at which motorcyclists fall.

## 3. Deaths and injuries caused by potholes: concrete cases (2023–2026) and aggregate statistics

### Takeaway
Pothole deaths are recurring and well documented in 2025–2026. In Jakarta and its surroundings alone, **27 pothole crashes were recorded in 1–28 January 2026 (1 dead, 8 seriously injured)**. In February 2026, **four people died in 13 days on one potholed road in Pasar Kemis**, and a **16-year-old student died at Matraman**. No national statistic isolates "faktor jalan" or potholes as a cause.

- The case pattern is consistent: victims are motorcyclists, including students, elderly riders and ojol drivers. A pothole makes them fall, and a truck or bus finishes the crash. In remote areas, the "victims" are patients and mothers who cannot reach care because ambulances cannot get through.

### Gaps

## 4. Legal accountability: UU 22/2009 LLAJ Pasal 24 and 273; lawsuits and reports against officials

### Takeaway
The law clearly obliges road operators to repair damage or put up warning signs, and allows prison terms of up to 5 years when a death results. In practice, **I found no conviction of an official**. A 2022 Constitutional Court challenge seeking clarity on who the "penyelenggara jalan" is was rejected.

### Cited Findings
- **Pasal 24(1) UU 22/2009:** the road operator must promptly and properly repair damaged roads that could cause accidents. **Pasal 24(2):** if not yet repaired, it must put up signs or markers. — [Hukumonline, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/mengulik-tanggung-jawab-hukum-jalan-berlubang-lt699592a17b4f9/?page=all)
- **Pasal 273 penalties:** up to 6 months' detention or a Rp 12 million fine for minor injury or vehicle damage. — [Hukumonline, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/mengulik-tanggung-jawab-hukum-jalan-berlubang-lt699592a17b4f9/?page=all). Up to 6 months for minor injury, **1 year for serious injury, 5 years if someone dies**. — [VOI, Feb 2026](https://voi.id/en/amp/560365)
- **Responsibility split (UU 22/2009):** central government for national roads, provincial governments for provincial roads, kabupaten/kota governments for kabupaten, city and village roads. — [VOI, Feb 2026](https://voi.id/en/amp/560365)
- **After the Pasar Kemis and Matraman deaths (Feb 2026),** Azas Tigor Nainggolan (KAWAT) said governors, bupati and walikota can be jailed. He urged police to investigate road operators, and said families can file civil suits. No actual police report against an official is mentioned. — [VOI, Feb 2026](https://voi.id/en/amp/560365)
- **MK case:** Irfan Kamil, a West Jakarta resident (counsel: Viktor Santoso Tandiasa), challenged Pasal 273(1), asking the court to define "penyelenggara jalan". **Putusan MK No. 98/PUU-XX/2022, pronounced 30 Sep 2022: rejected without hearing the government or DPR.** According to counsel, the court said UU 2/2022 and PP 34/2006 already settle responsibility. — [detikNews, 4 Dec 2022](https://news.detik.com/berita/d-6442173/kecewanya-warga-ke-mk-yang-tolak-gugatan-kecelakaan-jalan-rusak) **[OLDER]**
- **Komisi V DPR** asked the Ministry of PU to repair roads immediately after the February 2026 accidents. — [VOI](https://voi.id/en/amp/557304) **[HEADLINE ONLY]**
- **Toll roads:** under the SPM standard, potholes must be repaired within 2×24 hours. — [Hukumonline, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/mengulik-tanggung-jawab-hukum-jalan-berlubang-lt699592a17b4f9/?page=all). No comparable binding response time was found for regional roads.

### Inferences
- Pasal 24(2) makes warning signs a legal duty. This is a strong pitch hook: PAVEMENT-PINN's geotagged, timestamped reports create an **evidence trail** that the operator was notified, which is exactly what any Pasal 273 claim would need.

### Gaps
- No Indonesian court decision convicting an official, or ordering compensation, under Pasal 273 was found. Hukumonline's clinic pages on the procedure ([link](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/kemana-menggugat-jika-celaka-karena-jalanan-rusak-lt5ba9a14c17588)) were not opened.
- The fines for Pasal 273(2)–(4) (serious injury, death, failure to post signs) were not verified from the statute text.
- I did not confirm whether the Pasar Kemis road is a provincial (Banten) or kabupaten (Tangerang) road.

## 7. Economic cost; Jokowi on logistics; global context (WHO, India MoRTH, UK/US)

## 8. Aggravating factors: rainy season and flooding (water-filled potholes), ODOL overloading, poor drainage

### Takeaway
Rain and floods sharply multiply potholes. Jakarta logged about 6,000 in January 2026, and the head of Bina Marga DKI said asphalt is weak against water and that traffic "pumping" through standing water worsens damage. The puddle-covered pothole that killed an 18-year-old at Pasar Kemis shows why water-filled holes are lethal: riders cannot see them. ODOL trucks are the main structural cause cited by officials.

### Cited Findings
- **Jakarta, January 2026:** about **6,000 pothole points**, attributed to heavy rain and flooding. Damage rose compared with December 2025. Heru Suwondo (head of Dinas Bina Marga DKI) said asphalt is inherently weak against water and that vehicles passing through flooded roads worsen it ("pumping"). Only temporary patching was possible until the weather improved. — [CNN Indonesia, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/nasional/20260129164522-20-1322469/6000-titik-jalan-rusak-akibat-hujan-dki-lakukan-tambal-sementara)
- **27 pothole crashes in the Jakarta area in 1–28 Jan 2026** (rainy season). — [detikOto, 1 Feb 2026](https://oto.detik.com/berita/d-8334947/jalan-berlubang-bikin-celaka-ada-yang-meninggal-dunia)
- **Pasar Kemis, 13 Feb 2026:** an 18-year-old student died after falling into a **pothole covered by a puddle**. — [VOI, Feb 2026](https://voi.id/en/amp/560365)
- **Pasar Minggu potholes** appeared after the recent rain (Jan 2026). — [Kumparan, 27 Jan 2026](https://kumparan.com/kumparannews/tanda-silang-jalan-berlubang-di-pasar-minggu-jaksel-26iDokenfv0)
- **ODOL:** 2× load gives 16× damage. — [Bisnis.com, Mar 2023](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20230329/45/1641788/gara-gara-truk-batu-bara-biaya-perbaikan-jalan-di-jambi-bengkak-jadi-rp84-triliun). Rp 43.45 T a year in losses. — [detikFinance, 7 May 2025](https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-7902930/truk-obesitas-kian-merajalela-bikin-negara-rugi-rp-43-t-per-tahun). Komisi V's chair named ODOL, mining and plantation trucks, and CPO spills as the forces pushing national-road kemantapan down. — [CNBC Indonesia, 7 Sep 2026](https://cnbcindonesia.com/news/20260907135512-4-765806/kondisi-jalan-nasional-ri-mantap-935)

### Inferences
- The fatal combination is a **rainy-season pothole, filled with water and invisible, on a truck corridor**. PAVEMENT-PINN reports could be weighted by rainfall and heavy-vehicle exposure to prioritize the most lethal holes, not just the largest ones.
- Temporary patching in the wet season ("tambal sementara") produces the uneven patches seen at Matraman. Damage therefore recurs, which makes repeat audits valuable.
