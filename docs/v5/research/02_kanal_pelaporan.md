# Kanal Pelaporan Jalan Rusak di Indonesia: What Exists, How It Performs, Why It Fails (Gap Analysis for PAVEMENT-PINN)

Research date: 2026-11-01. Data from 2019 to Oct 2026; every item carries its date. Note on method: the shared web-search budget ran out partway through. Items not verified are listed under each "Gaps" heading. Some sources are cited by headline only, because the page body could not be retrieved (ombudsman.go.id often blocks automated fetch). These are marked "(headline only)".

## 1. SP4N-LAPOR! (lapor.go.id): routing, official SLA, volumes, completion, Ombudsman criticism

### Takeaway
SP4N-LAPOR! is a large national complaint switchboard: more than 1.1 million complaints from 2021 to Sep 2026, with 718 connected agencies, under a "No Wrong Door" policy. It has written SLAs: 3 working days to verify, 14 working days to follow up a complaint with no field check, and 60 working days if a field inspection is needed. It is a general-purpose ticketing system, though, and it does not route by road status. Even KemenPANRB, the ministry that runs it, missed the 3-day verification SLA on most of its own tickets in 2023. Ombudsman representatives keep reporting weak regional uptake and weak follow-up.

### Cited Findings
- **Scale (Sep 2026):** SP4N-LAPOR! received more than 1.1 million complaints from 2021 to September 2026. It connects 718 agencies: 546 regional governments, 119 ministries/agencies and 53 BUMN/other bodies. — [Investortrust, 30 Sep 2026](https://investortrust.id/national/117872/pemerintah-pastikan-aduan-masyarakat-di-sp4n-lapor-jadi-masukan-perbaikan-kebijakan)
- **Routing principle:** The government cites a "No Wrong Door Policy": citizens do not need to know which agency handles their complaint, and the system refers it. — [Investortrust, 30 Sep 2026](https://investortrust.id/national/117872/pemerintah-pastikan-aduan-masyarakat-di-sp4n-lapor-jadi-masukan-perbaikan-kebijakan)
- **Official time limits (as stated in KemenPANRB's own 2023 monitoring report):**
  - Admin Nasional or Admin Instansi verifies an incoming report within **3 working days**.
  - Normative information requests and complaints: **5 working days**.
  - Complaints with no supervision or field-inspection need: **14 working days**.
  - Complaints needing supervision and field inspection: **60 working days**.
  — [KemenPANRB, Laporan Monev Mekanisme Pengelolaan SP4N-LAPOR 2023](https://ppid.menpan.go.id/system/uploads/2025/10/Laporan-Monev-Mekanisme-Pengelolaan-SP4N-LAPOR-2023.pdf)
- **The SLA is missed even at the operator ministry (2023):**
  - KemenPANRB's own unit received 516 reports in 2023 and finished follow-up on 468 (≈90.7%, my calculation).
  - Of 464 reports verified, only 207 were verified in under 3 days; 257 took longer (≈55%, my calculation).
  - Average verification time was **5.2 days**, against the 3-day target.
  - The report also notes that changes in liaison officials (*pejabat penghubung*) made handling less than optimal, and it recommends push notifications for admins.
  — [KemenPANRB Monev 2023](https://ppid.menpan.go.id/system/uploads/2025/10/Laporan-Monev-Mekanisme-Pengelolaan-SP4N-LAPOR-2023.pdf)
- **Legal duty to respond:** Ombudsman writing cites UU 25/2009 on Public Services (incl. Art. 44(3)) and **Perpres 76/2013** on complaint management as the basis of the duty to respond. It says ignoring complaints can be maladministration under UU 37/2008. — [Ombudsman RI (Perwakilan Babel), "No Viral No Justice", 14 Jul 2026](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkmedia--no-viral-no-justice-cermin-krisis-kepercayaan-terhadap-pengaduan-publik)
- **Historic coverage gap:** As of May 2018, only about 303 regional governments (≈59% of Pemda) were connected to LAPOR!. — [Ombudsman RI, "Kanal Pengaduan Pelayanan Publik Setengah Hati", 28 Jan 2019](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/artikel--kanal-pengaduan-pelayanan-publik-setengah-hati)
- **Ombudsman regional monitoring (headline only):**
  - Ombudsman Kepri monitoring found Pemda "belum maksimal lakukan evaluasi" — [Ombudsman Kepri (headline)](https://ombudsman.go.id/perwakilan/news/r/pwksiaran--monitoring-laporan-sp4n-lapor-ombudsman-kepri-menilai-pemda-belum-maksimal-lakukan-evaluasi)
  - "Ombudsman Kepri ingatkan pemda untuk tindaklanjuti laporan SP4N LAPOR" — [Ombudsman (headline)](https://ombudsman.go.id/perwakilan/news/r/pwk--ombudsman-kepri-ingatkan-pemda-untuk-tindaklanjuti-laporan-sp4n-lapor-)
  - "Penerapan SP4N-LAPOR belum maksimal" — [Ombudsman (headline)](https://ombudsman.go.id/perwakilan/news/r/pwkmedia--penerapan-sp4n---lapor-belum-maksimal)
  - "Ternyata masih minim laporan di SP4N LAPOR" — [Ombudsman (headline)](https://ombudsman.go.id/artikel/r/pwkmedia--ternyata-masih-minim-laporan-di-sp4n-lapor)
  - "Ombudsman segera surati kepala daerah soal pelayanan SP4N-LAPOR" — [Ombudsman (headline)](https://ombudsman.go.id/perwakilan/news/r/pwkmedia--ombudsman-segera-surati-kepala-daerah-soal-pelayanan-sp4n--lapor-)
- **Academic evaluation:** A qualitative study of KemenPANRB's SP4N-LAPOR! management in 2021 concluded that the system was designed according to regulation but implementation was "not optimal". It cites commitment, coordination, technical/operational capability, technology and community involvement. — [Premaiswari, Siregar & Ghazali, JKAP 28(1), Jul 2024](https://journal.ugm.ac.id/jkap/article/view/94108/0)
- **Parallel national channel (Lapor Mas Wapres):**
  - VP Gibran's desk opened on 11 Nov 2024, and The Jakarta Post reported its efficacy being questioned. — [The Jakarta Post, 12 Nov 2024](https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2024/11/13/vp-gibrans-complaint-desk-efficiency-questioned)
  - In its first year it received 16,505 complaints; 66.07% came via WhatsApp.
  - The 2.0 version is integrated with SP4N-LAPOR! to speed referral.
  - No completion rate or road-specific share was published.
  — [RCTI+, 27 Nov 2025](https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/terkini/5273159/setahun-lapor-mas-wapres-ada-16505-aduan-masyarakat)

### Inferences
- SP4N-LAPOR! is a ticket router between agencies, not a road-asset system. Routing depends on a human admin choosing the instansi, and the admin has no road-status layer (national/provincial/kabupaten/village/toll). That makes mis-disposition likely for road reports. This is inferred from the "No Wrong Door" design plus the jurisdiction evidence in Sections 3–4; no published SP4N-LAPOR mis-routing rate was found.
- The 60-working-day ceiling (≈3 months) for complaints that need a field check is what applies to most potholes. That is far slower than NYC's 15-day legal shield or ~2-day actual average (Section 6).
- "Completion" in SP4N-LAPOR! means an answer was given. It does not mean a verified physical repair. The ~90% completion figures should not be read as "90% of potholes fixed".
- Lapor Mas Wapres adds yet another parallel intake channel instead of fixing routing. This shows the problem is fragmentation, not a lack of channels.

### Gaps
- **National road and infrastructure numbers:** No national SP4N-LAPOR! statistic was found for road or infrastructure complaints as a category (volume, share, completion rate or time to close). The KemenPANRB monev covers only its own unit.
- **PermenPANRB 46/2020:** Its content (believed to be the SP4N Road Map 2020–2024) was not verified. The 3/5/14/60-day SLAs were sourced from the KemenPANRB 2023 monev; the fetched text did not name the specific regulation (likely PermenPANRB 62/2018).
- **Ombudsman Annual Report (Laporan Tahunan):** National figures on infrastructure complaints and on SP4N-LAPOR compliance were not retrieved; fetching was blocked.
- No documented case or statistic was found for SP4N-LAPOR road tickets "closed without action".

## 2. Jakarta: JAKI / CRM, Qlue, response data, road share, Dinas Bina Marga channels

### Takeaway
Jakarta has the most mature system in Indonesia: CRM aggregates 14 channels, and in 2024 it reported 185,852 reports followed up. Roads are the #1 complaint category (≈20% of reports in Nov 2025). There is now a 6-day response deadline linked to pay cuts. Even here, the evidence shows weak points:
- Qlue collapsed after a political transition, with average response time going from 8 h to 72 h.
- "Selesai" (completed) is self-declared by the agency.
- In April 2026 field officers were sanctioned for AI-manipulated completion photos in JAKI.
- About 6,000 potholes appeared in the early-2026 rainy season, and city crews were still marking them by hand.

### Cited Findings
- **Integrated channels and early performance (data 2018–2020):**
  - CRM integrates 14 channels: Lapor! 1708, the Governor's social media, Balai Warga, jakarta.go.id, SMS, JAKI, Qlue, the Pemprov app, Twitter @dkijakarta, email, and in-person desks at Balai Kota, Walikota, Inspektorat, kecamatan and kelurahan.
  - About 300,000 complaints came from about 85,000 reporters over two years.
  - Average completion time was under 7 days, falling to about 2 days in 2020.
  - Report statuses: Menunggu, Proses, Disposisi, Koordinasi, Selesai.
  - "Jalan Rusak" is one of 289 categories.
  — [Jakarta Smart City/Diskominfotik presentation, undated, data 2018–2020](https://rumahpusbin.kemdikbud.go.id/datapusbin/580_CRM_Paparan_Pengelolaan_Aduan_Warga_Jakarta.pdf)
- **Status definitions:**
  - "Koordinasi" = one OPD passing the report to another OPD.
  - "Disposisi" = an agency ordering a subordinate office to act.
  - "Selesai" = "the report has been handled".
  - The help page gives no time targets and no reporter right to reject completion.
  — [JAKI Help, JakRespons statuses](https://jaki.jakarta.go.id/id/help/jak-respons/pending-coordination-disposition-process-and-completed-statuses-on-report/)
- **2024 volume:** "Throughout 2024, Cepat Respon Masyarakat has followed up on 185,852 reports." — [Jakarta Smart City, Kaleidoskop CRM 2024 (Jan 2025)](https://smartcity.jakarta.go.id/en/blog/kaleidoskop-crm-2024-laporan-warga-dalam-angka)
- **Road share (Nov 2025):**
  - CRM received 18,892 reports in November 2025.
  - **Roads (jalan) = 3,705 reports, ≈20%**, the largest category. Next were trees (2,594), illegal parking (2,020) and waste (1,583).
  - 90.4% were "followed up".
  — [GoodStats, 10 Jan 2026](https://goodstats.id/article/jalan-jadi-keluhan-terbanyak-warga-jakarta-0Su8L)
- **JAKI relaunch (28 May 2025):** Officers have **6 days** to respond. After that the system flags the case red and performance-based pay is automatically cut. The "Lapor Warga" feature receives ~91% of incoming complaints. — [INP Polri (relaying Diskominfotik head Budi Awaludin), 29 May 2025](https://inp.polri.go.id/artikel/jakarta-officers-face-sanctions-for-ignoring-complaints-on-jaki-app)
- **Manipulated completion evidence (Apr 2026):**
  - Three PPSU officers manipulated JAKI report follow-ups using **AI-altered photos**.
  - They received SP1 warnings.
  - The Lurah Kalisari and two Kasi were relieved of duty for negligence.
  - Governor Pramono held a town hall to fix the system.
  — [Metro TV, 15 Apr 2026](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/kewCjmzm-3-ppsu-dijatuhi-surat-peringatan-buntut-manipulasi-laporan-jaki)
  - A related headline (not retrieved) calls for layered verification of JAKI follow-ups: "Kasus Dugaan Manipulasi Laporan Kinerja Muncul Lagi, Tindak Lanjut Aduan JAKI Butuh Verifikasi Berlapis" — [RM.id (headline only)](https://rm.id/baca-berita/megapolitan/307276/kasus-dugaan-manipulasi-laporan-kinerja-muncul-lagi-tindak-lanjut-aduan-jaki-butuh-verifikasi-berlapis)
  - Another headline: "Pramono Akui Laporan JAKI Belum Ditindaklanjuti, Janji Perbaiki Sistem" — [RM.id (headline only)](https://rm.id/baca-berita/megapolitan/305838/pramono-akui-laporan-jaki-belum-ditindaklanjuti-janji-perbaiki-sistem)
- **Qlue collapse (2017):**
  - Qlue was launched in Dec 2014 under Gov. Basuki.
  - After Anies took office (Oct 2017), average response time rose from **8 hours (late 2016) to 72 hours (late 2017)**.
  - Complaints fell from 36,058 (Nov 2016) to 12,405 (Nov 2017).
  - Handling was shifted to 44 district offices with Saturday complaint sessions, from 18 Nov 2017.
  — [The Jakarta Post, 13 Feb 2018](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2018/02/13/anies-sidelines-smart-city.html)
- **Fragmentation (Jul 2019):**
  - Qlue was still the largest channel with 12,019 complaints that month, against Twitter 1,162, Facebook 312, LAPOR! SMS 1706 280, district offices 246 and email 227.
  - Headline: "Jakartans file complaints but get no clue on follow-ups."
  — [The Jakarta Post, 30 Aug/1 Sep 2019](https://newgelora.thejakartapost.com/news/2019/09/01/jakartans-file-complaints-but-get-no-clue-on-follow-ups)
- **Dinas Bina Marga DKI channels (Jan 2026):**
  - Bina Marga opened a pothole hotline, **021-3844444**, by phone and WhatsApp, alongside JAKI.
  - It promised "every report received will be addressed immediately", with no time target stated.
  - Emergency patches were used during the rains, with hot-mix promised later.
  — [Berita Jakarta, 30 Jan 2026](https://www.beritajakarta.id/en/read/61700/reporting-potholes-just-got-easier-with-jakartas-new-hotline); [detikOto, 1 Feb 2026](https://oto.detik.com/berita/d-8335320/laporkan-jalanan-rusak-ke-sini)
- **Scale of the early-2026 pothole wave:**
  - Gov. Pramono said ~6,000 potholes had been counted and most patched.
  - An ojol driver was injured on Jl. Letjen S Parman (9 Feb 2026). A student's death in Matraman was suspected to involve potholes plus speed.
  — [Republika, 12 Feb 2026](https://news.republika.co.id/berita/tac5yp348/jalan-rusak-di-jakarta-kembali-makan-korban)
  - Potholes on Jl. Raya Pasar Minggu were marked with white spray-paint X's (who painted them was not stated). The governor said repairs would wait until after cloud seeding, i.e. after 27 Jan. — [Kumparan, 27 Jan 2026](https://kumparan.com/kumparannews/tanda-silang-jalan-berlubang-di-pasar-minggu-jaksel-26iDokenfv0)

### Inferences
- Jakarta proves demand: roads are the #1 category. But "followed up" (90.4%) is self-reported by the same agencies that are graded on it. The April 2026 AI-photo manipulation shows that agency-uploaded proof photos cannot be trusted without independent verification. This is a direct opening for PAVEMENT-PINN's photo-verification and repair-audit layer.
- Qlue's collapse shows that a channel's performance depends on political leadership rather than on institutional design. An independent, citizen-side record can persist across administrations.
- Even Jakarta, with the best CRM, opened yet another pothole hotline in Jan 2026. Officials spray-painted or marked potholes by hand, which suggests the CRM is not working as a live pothole map.
- **Data-quality caveat:** CRM statistics count reports, not unique potholes. No deduplication or "unique defect" metric was found.

### Gaps
- No official date for Qlue's formal discontinuation was found.
- No JAKI/CRM breakdown by road authority (DKI roads vs national roads/toll inside Jakarta) was found, nor how out-of-authority reports are handled.
- No time-to-repair statistic specifically for road reports was found in CRM data; only the 6-day response deadline.
- No numbers were found on how many JAKI reports were manipulated, or how the AI-photo case was detected.
- @DinasBinaMarga X account response metrics: not researched (search budget exhausted).

## 3. Other regions and sector channels (Jabar, Jateng, Jatim/Surabaya, Lampung, Kementerian PU, toll roads)

### Takeaway
Every level of government has built its own intake:
- **Provinces:** Jalan Cantik (Jateng), Teman Jabar and Sapawarga (Jabar).
- **Cities:** Wargaku (Surabaya).
- **Ministry of PU:** "Jalan Kita" (2017), Bina Marga Command Center WhatsApp, and "Halo Pak Dody" via Instagram DM (2026).

None of them can route reliably across levels. In the clearest data point, Jawa Tengah's Jalan Cantik, provincial roads were only ~3–5% of reports received; the rest belonged to kabupaten, village or national authorities and had to be forwarded.

### Cited Findings
- **Jawa Tengah, Jalan Cantik (launched Jul 2019):**
  - 623 reports in about 5 months.
  - Breakdown: kabupaten/kota roads 315 (50.56%), village roads 52, provincial roads 21, national roads 9; ~226 uncategorized.
  - Provincial potholes have a 24-hour follow-up standard. Reports on other authorities' roads are "forwarded", with no time data.
  — [Antara Jateng, 23 Oct 2019](https://jateng.antaranews.com/berita/270924/aplikasi-jalan-cantik-dibanjiri-laporan-jalan-rusak-di-jateng)
  - A promotional story praised same-day handling of a provincial road report near Jembatan Jurug ("Kami langsung tangani hari ini juga"). It gave no data. — [detikJateng, 10 Mar 2023](https://www.detik.com/jateng/jawa-tengah-meriah/d-6611748/jalan-rusak-cepat-diperbaiki-warga-puji-aplikasi-jalan-cantik)
- **Jawa Barat (Jul 2026):**
  - Three online channels are recommended: **Teman Jabar** (temanjabar.jabarprov.go.id; reports verified, then forwarded), **SP4N-LAPOR!** (web, SMS 1708, X with #LAPOR) and **Sapawarga** ("Aduan Warga", complaint ID by email, "Cek Status Aduan").
  - No guidance on which channel handles which road class.
  — [Metro TV, 8 Jul 2026](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/KXyCW3zL-3-cara-mudah-lapor-jalan-rusak-di-jawa-barat-secara-online)
- **Surabaya (Feb 2026):**
  - Channels: the Wargaku app, 112, Radio Suara Surabaya, and official complaint channels taking coordinates and photos.
  - The city committed to follow up every road-damage report within **1×24 hours**. Repair teams grew from 5 to 9, using 50–60 t of hot-mix a day, on a budget of about Rp40 billion.
  - Mayor Eri ordered the city to also patch national and provincial roads with city funds ("mau jalan nasional atau provinsi... kita bisa handle").
  — [Suara Surabaya, 20 Feb 2026](https://www.suarasurabaya.net/kelanakota/2026/pemkot-surabaya-komitmen-perbaiki-laporan-jalan-berlubang-1x24-jam-siapkan-60-ton-hotmix-tiap-hari/)
  - A related headline claims "Hotline Lapor Cak Eri Catat 13.091 Aduan, 99 Persen Tertangani" — [RRI (headline only)](https://rri.co.id/surabaya/regional/2623093/hotline-lapor-cak-eri-catat-13091-aduan-99-persen-tertangani)
- **Kementerian PUPR, "Jalan Kita" app (2017):**
  - Reports on damaged roads and bridges, plus the Bina Marga Call Center.
  - Only about **500 downloads** and 1,998 reports at the time.
  - Target: repairs begin within 3 days, with a reprimand for the Balai or Satker if missed.
  — [Bisnis.com, 28 Jun 2017](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20170628/45/666665/aplikasi-jalan-kita-diunduh-500-kali)
- **Kementerian PU, "Halo Pak Dody" (6 Mar 2026):**
  - A complaint channel run through Minister Dody Hanggodo's Instagram DM (@dody_hanggodo), linked to a portal.
  - Mudik road-condition reports go to the **Bina Marga Command Center via WhatsApp**.
  - Violation reports go to the Inspektorat Jenderal.
  - No volume or outcome data.
  — [TVRI News, 6 Mar 2026](https://nasional.tvrinews.com/berita/tl6hqqk-menteri-pu-buka-jalur-laporan-publik-lewat-halo-pak-dody)
- **DI Yogyakarta (Feb 2023):**
  - The provincial PU office (PUPESDM) said complaints go by letter to the Dinas, Governor or Sekda, or via E-Lapor DIY.
  - It acts only on provincial roads, and only after weighing damage, urgency and budget. Major damage needs a budget proposal first.
  - Jalan Godean (a provincial road) was reportedly unrepaired for ~20 years and only patched.
  — [VICE Indonesia, 6 Feb 2023](https://www.vice.com/id/article/investigasi-kecil-kecilan-vice-gimana-ya-caranya-mengadukan-jalan-rusak-agar-diperbaiki/)
- **Toll operator vs regency (Klaten, Mar 2025):**
  - The Klaten–Gunungkidul road was damaged by toll-project trucks.
  - Dinas PUPR Klaten wrote twice to PT Jasamarga Jogja Solo because the road was covered by the toll MoU. JMJ said it would repair, but "repairs had been done and damage keeps recurring".
  - Residents planted banana trees.
  — [detikJateng, 22 Mar 2025](https://www.detik.com/jateng/berita/d-7836468/jalan-klaten-gunung-kidul-ditanami-pohon-pisang-dpu-surati-tol)
- **Lampung, Ombudsman study (Dec 2025):**
  - Road-damage complaints to Ombudsman Lampung rose from **1 in 2020 to 107 in 2024**.
  - The Ombudsman warned regional heads that infrastructure maladministration "keeps recurring". It gave 7 recommendations, including improving complaint handling, publishing maintenance plans and setting service standards.
  — [Ombudsman RI Lampung, 12 Dec 2025](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkmedia--ombudsman-lampung-warning-kepala-daerah)
- **Ombudsman Babel (Jul 2024):** More than 40% of 192 complaints received through Q2 2024 concerned road infrastructure. — [Ombudsman RI Babel, 31 Jul 2024](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkmedia--infrastruktur-jalan-dominasi-aduan-ke-ombudsman-babel-2024)
- **Ombudsman Jateng (Apr 2025):** 67 complaints in Q1 2025 (48 resolved, 19 in process), with road damage the dominant topic. Many damaged national, provincial, kabupaten and village roads are said to be "unreported". — [Antara Jateng, 11 Apr 2025](https://jateng.antaranews.com/berita/577721/ombudsman-jateng-aduan-didominasi-soal-jalan-rusak)
- **Police (Satlantas) as informal channel:** In Trenggalek, Satlantas inspected a banana-tree protest road and coordinated with Dinas PUPR. It reported that repairs were "still proposed" with no date. — [detikJatim, 18 Feb 2025](https://www.detik.com/jatim/berita/d-7783034/warga-tanam-pohon-pisang-di-jalan-rusak-ini-langkah-polisi-trenggalek)
  - A similar headline: "Banyak temui jalan berlubang, Satlantas Polres Purbalingga segera lapor ke dinas terkait" — [Times Indonesia (headline only)](https://timesindonesia.co.id/peristiwa-daerah/579055/banyak-temui-jalan-berlubang-satlantas-polres-purbalingga-segera-lapor-ke-dinas-terkait)

### Inferences
- Jalan Cantik's numbers are the strongest Indonesian quantification of the jurisdiction problem. Of 397 categorized reports, only 21 (≈5.3%) concerned the provincial roads the app's owner could actually fix; ≈95% had to be forwarded elsewhere, with no tracked time. Citizens do not know or care about road status, so any single-level app inherits a misrouting problem.
- National-level PU channels have repeatedly failed to reach scale: "Jalan Kita" had ~500 downloads in 2017, and in 2026 the channel is a minister's Instagram DM plus WhatsApp. National roads lack a citizen-facing, map-based reporting tool with public status.
- Surabaya's choice to patch national and provincial roads itself is a workaround for jurisdictional rigidity, not a fix to it.
- The toll and regency case (Klaten) shows a third axis of responsibility: concessionaires and project MoUs. Neither SP4N-LAPOR nor the regional apps model this.

### Gaps
- Not verified (search budget exhausted):
  - Jasa Marga call center 14080 and its toll-road pothole SLA (SPM Jalan Tol).
  - Korlantas/Polri digital road-hazard channels.
  - Bandung (Lapor!/Sampurasun).
  - Bekasi, Karawang.
  - The Jawa Timur provincial app.
  - "Sapu Lubang" or "Jaga Jalan" program names.
  - BBPJN email addresses.
- No performance data (time-to-repair, completion) was found for Teman Jabar, Sapawarga, Wargaku or the PU channels.
- The Surabaya "99% handled" figure is from a headline only.

## 4. Evidence that reporting is ignored, slow or bounced; "No viral no justice"; trust in complaint channels

### Takeaway
There is a well-documented pattern: formal complaints go unanswered, sometimes for years, and repairs follow viral social-media exposure. The canonical case is Lampung in 2023. A TikTok video led to a presidential visit, Rp800 billion and a national road program. Meanwhile residents of one road segment said it had gone 10 years without repair despite repeated complaints, and the critic was reported to police. Surveys show citizens trust government sources far less than social media: 2% vs 30% (Tempo Data Science, Aug 2026).

### Cited Findings
- **Lampung 2023, timeline:**
  - Mid-April 2023: Bima Yudho Saputro's TikTok "Alasan Kenapa Lampung Nggak Maju-maju" went viral.
  - 18 Apr: Kemendagri convened Lampung Sekdas.
  - 2–3 May: PUPR Minister Basuki announced the presidential inspection.
  - Budget context: Lampung's 2023 road-maintenance budget was Rp72.44 billion, **0.98%** of Rp7.38 trillion in spending.
  — [detikFinance, 3 May 2023](https://finance.detik.com/infrastruktur/d-6700909/perjalanan-heboh-jalan-lampung-dajjal-sampai-bikin-jokowi-turun-tangan)
- **Lampung, rushed repairs and a decade of complaints:**
  - Republika headline: "Seusai Viral, Jalan-Jalan Rusak di Lampung Dikebut Diperbaiki Jelang Kunjungan Jokowi".

### Inferences
- The Lampung case shows the incentive problem. Formal complaints cost the government nothing to ignore, while viral posts produce political cost and therefore money. PAVEMENT-PINN can be framed as creating a public, aggregated, evidence-backed record that produces accountability without needing virality or exposing an individual critic to police reports.
- Retaliation against critics (Lampung 2023, Banten per VICE) argues for privacy-preserving or anonymous reporting with public aggregation.
- The two trust surveys measure trust in information sources, not satisfaction with complaint channels specifically. They are suggestive but should not be presented as "trust in SP4N-LAPOR".

### Gaps
- No survey was found that specifically measures citizen trust in or satisfaction with complaint channels (SP4N-LAPOR, JAKI) from LSI, Litbang Kompas, Indikator or the Ombudsman's Penilaian Kepatuhan. The search budget ran out before this could be pursued.
- No quantitative study was found on "time to repair before vs after viral".
- No documented quote of an official saying "bukan kewenangan kami" for a specific road complaint was retrieved. The jurisdiction evidence is structural (Jalan Cantik, Yogyakarta, Klaten).

## 6. International comparators: features and published effectiveness

### Takeaway
Mature foreign systems share several features:
- Map-pin and photo intake.
- Automatic routing to the responsible authority without the citizen needing to know boundaries.
- Public status pages and notifications.
- Explicit repair targets: NYC has a 15-day legal shield and averages about 2 days.

FixMyStreet Pro's published UK council data shows measurable savings: per-report cost fell from £5.88 by phone to 9p, chasing calls fell 29%, and misrouted reports fell 19%. These systems still suffer from self-reported "fixed" status and duplicate counts, which leaves room for a verification layer.

### Cited Findings
- **FixMyStreet, routing design:** It triages reports "between councils at all levels, as well as to highways agencies and housing associations". It was designed so people do not need "any prior knowledge of council boundaries or responsibilities". — [FixMyStreet, Understanding report data](https://cy.fixmystreet.com/about/understanding-report-data)
- **FixMyStreet, data caveats:**
  - Where councils do not integrate, "fixed" status depends on reporters returning to mark it.
  - Stale reports become "unknown".
  - Some councils refuse third-party reports.
  - The data should not be used for comparisons.
  — [FixMyStreet](https://cy.fixmystreet.com/about/understanding-report-data)
- **FixMyStreet Pro, measured savings (Buckinghamshire County Council, Apr 2018–2019 vs prior year):**
  - Street-fault calls −13%; emails −40%.
  - Calls chasing progress −29%; unnecessary clarification requests −59%; misrouted reports forwarded elsewhere −19%.
  - Street-light reports −30%, because existing lights and reports were shown on the map.
  - Cost per call £5.88 vs email £7.81 vs FixMyStreet Pro report 9p.
  — [mySociety, 28 Jun 2019](https://www.mysociety.org/2019/06/28/measuring-the-savings-brought-by-fixmystreet-pro/)
- **FixMyStreet NZ, independent analysis:** In Wellington and Christchurch, "less than a quarter of the reported issues have been fixed" (or reported as fixed). The analysis is undated. — [Quicksand/Medium, "Who's fixing our streets?"](https://medium.com/quicksand/whos-fixing-our-streets-2bfb68296c38)
- **NYC 311 and DOT, legal SLA and audit:**
  - NYC Admin Code §7-201(c)(2) shields the City from pothole-damage suits if a pothole is repaired within **15 days** of a complaint.
  - DOT's internal goal was 30 days.
  - The MMR average was 5.6 days (FY2014–15). ~10% of street potholes were repaired after more than 15 days.
  - Repair records contained "numerous duplicates, and even some triplicates".
  - Arterial and bridge units did not track timeliness.
  — [NYC Comptroller audit ME15-114A, 29 Jun 2016](https://comptroller.nyc.gov/reports/audit-report-on-the-department-of-transportations-tracking-of-pothole-repairs)
- **NYC, 2025 performance:** 311 pothole complaints are closed in about **1.8 days** on average, against 3.4 days (de Blasio) and 4.4 days (Bloomberg). The city resolved 88,466 311 pothole complaints over 3 years. — [NYC Mayor's Office, 29 Jan 2025](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2025/01/mayor-adams-commissioner-rodriguez-celebrate-dot-crews-filling-administration-s-500-000th)
- **NYC, 2026 surge:**
  - 11,300+ 311 pothole complaints by early March 2026, up ~33% year on year; Queens accounted for nearly half.
  - More than a quarter were still open, pending or in progress.
  - DOT typically closes work orders in about 2 days.
  — [Hoodline, 10 Mar 2026](https://hoodline.com/2026/03/queens-roads-crater-as-nyc-pothole-complaints-hit-multi-year-high/)
- **SeeClickFix / Improve Detroit (vendor claim):** Nearly 190,000 submissions, 97% resolved, same-day acknowledgments, collaborative routing across departments. — [CivicPlus case study via CaseStudies.com (vendor material, undated)](https://www.casestudies.com/company/civicplus/case-study/detroit-mi-fixes-97-of-citizen-requests-with-seeclickfix)
- **India, NHAI (Jul 2025):**
  - The 1033 24×7 helpline handles potholes, accidents and tolls, with central logging, forwarding to field units and status updates.
  - The **Rajmargyatra app** takes geo-tagged complaints only from users on National Highways, with real-time forwarding, closure notification and feedback.
  - **CPGRAMS** routes complaints to the PIU or Regional Office and offers escalation.
  - No SLA or volume figures were published.
  — [DeshGujarat, 15 Jul 2025](https://deshgujarat.com/2025/07/15/toll-free-helpline-mobile-apps-portal-among-nhais-grievance-redressal-platforms)
- **Japan, My City Report (MCR):**
  - A citizen-collaborative reporting platform by the University of Tokyo (Sekimoto Lab), Urban X Technologies and partners, serving 13 municipalities including Chiba City.
  - "MCR for road managers" uses AI on images from smartphones or dashcams in ordinary vehicles to detect road damage in near real time.
  - No effectiveness figures were published on the page.
  — [University of Tokyo, project page](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/adm/uci/ja/projects/cyber/project_00025.html)

### Inferences
- The feature set that matters most for Indonesia is FixMyStreet-style boundary-based auto-routing, combined with a road-status layer (national/provincial/kabupaten/village/toll). Buckinghamshire's −19% misrouting and −29% chasing calls are the closest published evidence that routing and public status reduce wasted effort.
- NYC shows the value of a legally anchored repair clock. Indonesia's UU 22/2009 LLAJ duty-to-repair exists (per media; not verified here), but no channel exposes a public clock per pothole.
- Even top systems rely on self-reported closure (FixMyStreet) and have duplicate counts (NYC). Independent photo-based verification of repair quality, PAVEMENT-PINN's audit angle, goes beyond the international state of the art rather than merely copying it.

### Gaps
- Not researched (search budget exhausted):
  - Waze for Cities pothole data-sharing features and outcomes.
  - RoadBotics (acquired by Michelin) and Vaisala RoadAI effectiveness data.
  - Bengaluru "Fix Pothole" / BBMP app and Kerala PWD apps.
  - Peer-reviewed SeeClickFix responsiveness studies (a PUAR paper, DOI 10.1111/puar.13747, was identified but not retrieved).
- No independent (non-vendor) Detroit figures.
- No published effectiveness data for Japan's MCR.

## 7. Features missing in Indonesian channels (synthesized gap matrix)

### Takeaway
Across SP4N-LAPOR!, JAKI/CRM, the regional apps and PU channels, the documented gaps are:
1. No automatic routing by road status or authority (including toll concessionaires).
2. No independent photo verification of either the defect or the repair; "Selesai" is self-declared, and in Jakarta 2026 completion photos were AI-faked.
3. No deduplication or unique-defect map.
4. Public status tracking is per-ticket at best, with no public map of open defects.
5. Escalation is weak and slow (60 working days for field-check complaints; Ombudsman as last resort).
6. No repair-quality or durability check: repairs "keep recurring" in Klaten, and Jakarta uses temporary patches.
7. No link from complaints to maintenance budgets or costs: Lampung spent 0.98% of its budget on road maintenance in 2023, and the Ombudsman asked for maintenance plans to be published.

  - Jalan Cantik: ≈95% of categorized reports were for roads outside provincial authority and had to be forwarded. — [Antara Jateng, 2019](https://jateng.antaranews.com/berita/270924/aplikasi-jalan-cantik-dibanjiri-laporan-jalan-rusak-di-jateng)

### Inferences
- **Suggested answer to the judges.** "Channels exist, but they are complaint inboxes, not road-accountability systems." Indonesia has many intake points (14 in Jakarta alone, plus several national ones). What is missing is the layer between intake and repair:
  1. Knowing whose road it is.
  2. Proving the defect and the repair with tamper-evident evidence.
  3. Merging duplicates into one defect.
  4. Exposing a public clock.
  5. Auditing whether the patch lasted and what it cost.

  PAVEMENT-PINN can position itself as complementary infrastructure that can feed SP4N-LAPOR!/JAKI, not as a new inbox. That framing avoids the "yet another channel" objection, which Lapor Mas Wapres attracted.
- The strongest single data points for a pitch:
  - Roads ≈20% of Jakarta CRM reports (Nov 2025).
  - ≈95% of Jalan Cantik reports outside the app owner's authority (2019).
  - KemenPANRB missed its own 3-day verification SLA on ~55% of tickets (2023).
  - Ombudsman Lampung road complaints rose from 1 to 107 (2020→2024).
  - 2% of citizens trust government sources (2026).
  - AI-faked completion photos in JAKI (Apr 2026).
  - Lampung: 10 years of complaints versus Rp800 billion after one viral TikTok (2023).

### Gaps
- No Indonesian channel was found that publicly documents photo-forensic verification, deduplication or reporter-confirmed closure. This absence is inferred from status definitions and press coverage, not from a systematic feature audit of each app.
- No cost-per-report or staff-time data for Indonesian channels (to compare with Buckinghamshire's £5.88 vs 9p).
- Because the search budget was exhausted, these were not checked: Jasa Marga 14080 (toll SPM response time), Korlantas, Bandung, Bekasi, Karawang, the Jawa Timur provincial app, @DinasBinaMarga X performance, LSI/Litbang Kompas complaint-channel surveys, Waze for Cities, RoadBotics/Vaisala, and Indian city pothole apps. A follow-up search pass is recommended for these.
