## 1. Road classification by status, who sets status, and what counts as "part of the road"

### Takeaway
Public roads fall into five status groups: **nasional, provinsi, kabupaten, kota, desa**. On top of these sit **jalan tol**, which are a sub-type of national roads and are run by toll operators, and **jalan khusus**, which are private or agency roads. Under UU 38/2004 as amended by UU 2/2022, each level of government holds authority over its own class. A periodic decree sets each road's status: Menteri for national roads, Gubernur for provincial, Bupati for kabupaten and desa, Walikota for kota. PP 34/2006 is still the implementing PP. The road authority's domain includes the **carriageway, roadside drainage (saluran tepi jalan) and safety margin**, and **trotoar** is defined as part of the road.

### Cited Findings
- **Legal hierarchy.** UU 2/2022 is the *second* amendment to UU 38/2004 tentang Jalan. — [BPK JDIH: UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/195878/uu-no-2-tahun-2022); [UU 2/2022 PDF](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
- **Classes (Pasal 9(1)).** Public roads are "dikelompokkan ke dalam Jalan nasional, Jalan provinsi, Jalan kabupaten, Jalan kota, dan Jalan desa." — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
  - **Jalan nasional (9(6)):** primary arterials linking national activity centres, national strategic roads, **and toll roads**. Some primary collectors are also included.
  - **Jalan provinsi (9(7)):** primary collectors linking the provincial capital with kabupaten/kota capitals, plus provincial strategic roads.
  - **Jalan kabupaten (9(8)):** remaining primary collectors, primary local roads, secondary-network public roads within the kabupaten, and kabupaten strategic roads.
  - **Jalan kota (9(9)):** secondary-network public roads within a city.
  - **Jalan desa (9(10)):** public roads linking areas or settlements inside a village. — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
- **Duty to set status (Pasal 9(2)).** "Pemerintah Pusat dan Pemerintah Daerah wajib menetapkan status Jalan sesuai dengan pengelompokan." — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
- **Authority by level:**
  - Pemerintah Pusat: penyelenggaraan jalan "secara umum" and jalan nasional (Pasal 14(1)); setting national status (Pasal 18(2)(b)).
  - Provinsi: jalan provinsi (15(1)) and setting its status (19(1)(d)).
  - Kabupaten: jalan kabupaten, plus regulation and guidance of jalan desa (16(1)); status (20(1)(c)).
  - Kota: jalan kota (16(2)); status (21(1)(c)).
  - **Pemerintah Desa: jalan desa (Pasal 16A(1)).** — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
- **Takeover ("pengambilalihan") clauses:**
  - Pusat may take over *construction* of provincial roads if the province cannot do it (15(3)).
  - Province and/or Pusat may take over for kabupaten/kota (16(4)).
  - Kabupaten/kota/province may take over for desa (16A(3)).
  - Construction may be delegated downward to lower governments or desa (30(3)).
  - Pusat gives budget support to regions (33(2)).
  - Pusat provides guidance (pembinaan) to regional road authorities that have not met SPM (35F(4)). — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
- **Preservation duty.** "Penyelenggara Jalan wajib melaksanakan preservasi Jalan" (Pasal 35G(2)). Preservation includes routine and periodic maintenance, rehabilitation, reconstruction and widening. Works must ensure "penempatan perlengkapan Jalan secara jelas" (35G(3)). — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
- **Public participation (Pasal 62(1)).** The public may "memberi masukan kepada Penyelenggara Jalan," "memperoleh informasi mengenai Penyelenggaraan Jalan," and sue for losses. The extracted text has no explicit complaint (pengaduan) mechanism. — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
- **Implementing PP deadline.** Pasal 67A(1) required implementing regulations within 1 year. Many articles defer to a PP (e.g., 9(11), 15(4), 16(5), 16A(4), 36(7), 51A(7)). — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
- **Status decrees (PP 34/2006 Pasal 62):**
  - Nasional: periodically by **Keputusan Menteri** (62(1)).
  - Provinsi: **Keputusan Gubernur**, taking the Menteri's decision into account (62(2)).
  - Kabupaten: **keputusan bupati** (62(3)).
  - Kota: **keputusan walikota** (62(4)).
  - **Desa: keputusan bupati** (62(5)).
  - All are reviewed periodically per Menteri guidelines (62(6)). — [PP 34/2006](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp34-2006.pdf)
- **Parts of the road (PP 34/2006):**
  - Pasal 33: ruang manfaat, ruang milik and ruang pengawasan jalan.
  - Ruang manfaat jalan = badan jalan + **saluran tepi jalan** + ambang pengaman (Pasal 34–37).
  - **Trotoar** is reserved for pedestrians (Pasal 34(4)).
  - Minimum ROW widths (Pasal 40): 30/25/15/11 m.
  - Ruang pengawasan widths from 15 m (arteri) to 2 m (lingkungan sekunder) (Pasal 44). — [PP 34/2006](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp34-2006.pdf)
- **National roads in a province (PP 34/2006):**
  - Pusat may delegate permits, recommendations and dispensations on national (non-toll) roads to the gubernur (Pasal 55(1)).
  - The province may carry out part of national-road construction and maintenance via dekonsentrasi or tugas pembantuan (Pasal 59).
  - The PP does not provide for a kota taking over national road sections. — [PP 34/2006](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp34-2006.pdf)
- **Maintenance (PP 34/2006 Pasal 97):** maintenance is the "prioritas tertinggi". It comprises pemeliharaan rutin, pemeliharaan berkala and rehabilitasi, and must follow a maintenance plan. — [PP 34/2006](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp34-2006.pdf)
- **Public reporting right (Pasal 120(1)):** the public may report misuse of ruang manfaat, milik and pengawasan to the road authority. Procedures are left to a Permen (Pasal 120(2)). — [PP 34/2006](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp34-2006.pdf)
- **Jalan khusus:**
  - UU 2/2022 Pasal 57A–57D: built and maintained by a BUMN/BUMD, business, individual, community or agency for its own use. It may carry public traffic if this doesn't harm the owner (57C(1)), and may be handed over to government (57D(1)). — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
  - PP 34/2006 Pasal 123–124: after handover and designation by the bupati/walikota, it becomes a jalan kabupaten/kota. — [PP 34/2006](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp34-2006.pdf)
- **Current national-road decree:**
  - **Kepmen PUPR No. 1688/KPTS/M/2022** sets national road segments. Under it, non-toll national roads total **47,604.34 km** and national toll roads **2,841.12 km** (Semester II 2024). — [Ditjen Bina Marga, Kondisi Jalan Nasional 2024 S-2](https://data.pu.go.id/sites/default/files/Buku%20Kondisi%20Jalan%20Nasional%202024%20S-2%20TTD.pdf)
  - Kepmen 1688/2022 replaced Kepmen 290/KPTS/M/2015. — [Ombudsman RI Kepri, 13 Aug 2024](https://ombudsman.go.id/artikel/r/pwkinternal--implikasi-kontroversi-kepmen-pupr-nomor-1688kptsm2022-tentang-penetapan-ruas-jalan-)
  - The Bina Marga NSPK index also lists a Kepmen PUPR 430/KPTS/M/2022 "tentang Penetapan Ruas Jalan Menurut Statusnya sebagai Jalan Nasional". Its relationship to 1688/2022 was not verified. — [Bina Marga NSPK listing](https://binamarga.pu.go.id/index.php/nspk/detail/keputusan-menteri-pupr-nomor-430kptsm2022-tentang-penetapan-ruas-jalan-menurut-statusnya-sebagai-jalan-nasional)
- **Status changes shift responsibility.** Under Kepmen 1688/2022, Kota Batam had **no national road segments from 2022**. After SK Gubernur Kepri No. 485 (3 Apr 2023), it also had no provincial segments, so Pemkot Batam handles all its roads. The Ombudsman Kepri head called this a cause of deteriorating maintenance. — [Ombudsman RI Kepri](https://ombudsman.go.id/artikel/r/pwkinternal--implikasi-kontroversi-kepmen-pupr-nomor-1688kptsm2022-tentang-penetapan-ruas-jalan-). The article cites the legal basis as "UU Nomor 34 Tahun 2006"; this is evidently a slip for **PP 34/2006**.
- **Duty to repair (UU 22/2009 LLAJ):**
  - **Pasal 24(1):** "penyelenggara Jalan wajib segera dan patut untuk memperbaiki Jalan yang rusak yang dapat mengakibatkan kecelakaan."
  - **Pasal 24(2):** if repair can't be done yet, the operator must put up signs.
  - **Pasal 273:** criminal sanction. Minor injury or vehicle damage: up to 6 months or Rp12 juta. Serious injury: 1 year or Rp24 juta. Death: 5 years or Rp120 juta. Failing to sign a damaged road: up to 6 months or Rp1.5 juta. — [Hukumonline opinion, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/mengulik-tanggung-jawab-hukum-jalan-berlubang-lt699592a17b4f9?page=2); [Ombudsman RI, 3 Sep 2021](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkinternal--jalan-rusak-dan-cara-melaporkannya); [GNFI, 19 Feb 2026](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2026/02/19/menjaga-jalan-jakarta-menjaga-keselamatan-bersama). The Ombudsman article loosely attributes the 6-month/Rp12 juta penalty to "Article 24". The penalties actually sit in Pasal 273.
- **Responsibility summary by status:** national roads → Kementerian PU; regional roads → gubernur/bupati/walikota; toll roads → BUJT as operator. — [Hukumonline, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/mengulik-tanggung-jawab-hukum-jalan-berlubang-lt699592a17b4f9?page=2)

### Inferences
- **Routing must use the SK, not the road's name or apparent importance.** Status is set by periodic decree and can change (the Batam case). The app should keep a versioned ruas registry with source SK number and date for each segment, and re-sync it whenever a new Kepmen, SK Gubernur or SK Bupati/Walikota is issued.
- **Drainage and sidewalk defects fall to the road authority by default.** Saluran tepi jalan and trotoar are legally part of the road (PP 34/2006 Pasal 34). Local exceptions should be configurable, e.g. in Jakarta, where Dinas Bina Marga holds PJU (see §5).
- **Construction vs maintenance.** UU 2/2022 lets higher governments take over *construction* of lower-level roads. Absent a formal takeover or assignment, *preservation* stays with the status holder. The app should model "assisted/taken-over projects" as an overlay, not a status change.
- **Legal hook for the app.** UU 2/2022 Pasal 62(1) (input and information) and PP 34/2006 Pasal 120 (reporting right) give a legal basis for a citizen channel. UU 22/2009 Pasal 24/273 gives timestamped reports evidentiary weight. Report creation time and authority acknowledgement time should therefore be immutable.

### Gaps
- **New PP.** No new PP replacing PP 34/2006 was found. Searches returned only PP 34/2006, which appears to remain in force despite the Pasal 67A 1-year deadline. This needs one more confirmatory search on peraturan.bpk.go.id/jdih.pu.go.id.
- **Status-setting Permen.** The Permen PUPR guideline for status setting (penetapan status ruas jalan) and its current number were not retrieved.
- **430 vs 1688.** The relationship between Kepmen 430/KPTS/M/2022 and 1688/KPTS/M/2022, and any post-2022 amendment of 1688, was not verified.
- **Who installs perlengkapan jalan outside Jakarta.** Not verified: who installs and maintains rambu, marka, APILL and PJU outside Jakarta. The likely split is Dishub via UU 22/2009 Pasal 25–26, with PJU often under Dishub or Perkim, but no source was retrieved.
- **National roads inside cities.** No source found on how national roads inside cities are handled in practice (e.g., city-maintained sidewalks on national roads).

## 2. National level: Kementerian PU, Ditjen Bina Marga, BBPJN/BPJN, Satker and PPK

### Takeaway
Since **Perpres 170/2024 (5 Nov 2024)**, the former Kementerian PUPR has been split. Roads now sit in **Kementerian Pekerjaan Umum (PU)** under **Ditjen Bina Marga**, while housing moved to a separate ministry. Field work on national roads is done by UPTs, now governed by **Permen PU 1/2025**: **9 Balai Besar Pelaksanaan Jalan Nasional (7 Tipe A, 2 Tipe B), 24 Balai Pelaksanaan Jalan Nasional, and 4 Balai Teknik**. Each Balai has a Bagian Umum dan TU and Bidang for Keterpaduan, Pembangunan, and Preservasi.

### Cited Findings
- **Perpres 170/2024 tentang Kementerian Pekerjaan Umum.** Enacted and promulgated 5 Nov 2024 (Pasal 65: effective on promulgation). The ministry "menyelenggarakan urusan pemerintahan di bidang pekerjaan umum" (Pasal 5). — [Pasal.id: Perpres 170/2024](https://pasal.id/peraturan/perpres/perpres-no-170-tahun-2024); [JDIH Kemenko Infra PDF](https://jdih.kemenkoinfra.go.id/cfind/source/files/perpres/2024/perpres-nomor-170-tahun-2024.pdf)
- **Eselon I units (Pasal 7):** Sekretariat Jenderal; Ditjen Sumber Daya Air; **Ditjen Bina Marga**; Ditjen Cipta Karya; Ditjen Prasarana Strategis; Ditjen Bina Konstruksi; Ditjen Pembiayaan Infrastruktur PU; **Inspektorat Jenderal**; Badan Pengembangan Infrastruktur Wilayah; Badan Pengembangan SDM. — [Pasal.id: Perpres 170/2024](https://pasal.id/peraturan/perpres/perpres-no-170-tahun-2024)
- **Ditjen Bina Marga:** its duty (Pasal 15) is formulating and implementing policy in "penyelenggaraan jalan". Its functions (Pasal 16) include implementing national-road policy, NSPK, technical guidance and supervision. UPTs may be formed (Pasal 41), with ministerial establishment after MenPAN-RB approval (Pasal 42). — [Pasal.id: Perpres 170/2024](https://pasal.id/peraturan/perpres/perpres-no-170-tahun-2024)
- **Current officials:** Minister **Dody Hanggodo** signed Permen PU 2/2025 (17 Oct 2025). — [Permen PU 2/2025](https://jdih.kemenkoinfra.go.id/cfind/source/files/permen-pu/2025pmpu2.pdf). **Dirjen Bina Marga Roy Rizali Anwar** appeared before DPR Komisi V in July 2026. — [detikFinance, 9 Jul 2026](https://finance.detik.com/infrastruktur/d-8567118/aturan-spm-tol-dikebut-bujt-melanggar-siap-siap-didenda)

  - **Permen PU No. 1 Tahun 2025** on organisasi dan tata kerja UPT of Kementerian PU lists the Bina Marga UPTs: **7 BBPJN Tipe A, 2 BBPJN Tipe B, 24 BPJN, 4 Balai Teknik**. — [Renstra BKJTK 2025–2029](https://binamarga.pu.go.id/balai-kjtk/silat/uploads/documents/1786331203000-renstra-bkjtk-2025-2029-final.pdf)

### Inferences
- **National-road routing key = province → Balai.** For Papua, use the 2024 nomenclature: Prov. Papua and Papua Pegunungan → BBPJN Papua–Papua Pegunungan; Papua Barat and Papua Barat Daya → BBPJN Papua Barat–Papua Barat Daya; Papua Selatan and Papua Tengah → their own BPJN. This is inferred from the Balai names and should be confirmed against Permen PU 1/2025's lampiran.
- **Below the Balai, the actual approver of works is the PPK (contract owner).** Bidang Preservasi plans and controls; per-segment PPKs hold the contracts. The app needs a sub-routing level of **Balai → (Satker) → PPK ruas**, so each report lands with the PPK whose contract covers that segment.
- **Kementerian PU's APIP is the Inspektorat Jenderal** (eselon I under Perpres 170/2024). It is the natural internal-audit role for national roads.

## 3. Toll roads: BPJT, BUJT, SPM

### Takeaway
Toll roads are national roads (UU 2/2022 Pasal 9(6)) operated by **Badan Usaha Jalan Tol (BUJT)** under concession (PPJT). BUJT must meet **SPM Jalan Tol** (Pasal 51A) and are responsible for preservation. **BPJT** is under the Minister and supervises the concessions. **Ditjen Bina Marga** runs the laik-fungsi certification. The SPM still in force is **Permen PU 16/PRT/M/2014**, which has no sanctions. In 2026 a new SPM Permen with administrative fines was being finalised.

### Cited Findings
- **UU 2/2022 provisions:**
  - Pengusahaan Jalan Tol by "Pemerintah Pusat dan/atau Badan Usaha" (43(3)).
  - Regulation by the central government (50(3)).
  - Tariff adjustment "setiap 2 (dua) tahun sekali", set by the Menteri (48(3),(7)).
  - Operator "wajib memenuhi SPM Jalan Tol" (51A(1)), with administrative sanctions (51B(1)).
  - Operator must provide a usable alternative road (52(1)). — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
- **Permen PU No. 2 Tahun 2025 tentang Pelaksanaan Wewenang Penyelenggaraan Jalan Tol** (17 Oct 2025) revokes Permen PUPR 20/2020. It splits toll authority among DJBM, DJPI, BPJT and Badan Usaha:
  - **BPJT** sits "di bawah dan bertanggung jawab kepada Menteri" (Pasal 1 angka 7). It facilitates SPM compliance and reports to the Minister, evaluates tariffs, runs tenders/PPJT and supervises pengusahaan (Lampiran C, D.3).
  - **DJBM** runs uji laik fungsi, issues the sertifikat laik fungsi dan operasi, does technical QA and KPI supervision (Lampiran C.1 items 9–13; Pasal 6(2)).
  - **BUJT** is responsible for **preservasi**, meeting SPM, self-evaluating SPM and reporting to the Minister (Lampiran C.1 items 14–15).
  - **No provision on handling user complaints.** — [Permen PU 2/2025](https://jdih.kemenkoinfra.go.id/cfind/source/files/permen-pu/2025pmpu2.pdf)
- **Permen PU 16/PRT/M/2014 (SPM Jalan Tol):**
  - 8 substansi: kondisi jalan tol, kecepatan tempuh rata-rata, aksesibilitas, mobilitas, keselamatan, unit pertolongan/penyelamatan, lingkungan, TI/TIP (Pasal 3(1)).
  - "Wajib dipenuhi oleh Badan Usaha Jalan Tol" (Pasal 5).
  - Evaluated periodically, with supervision "dilaksanakan oleh BPJT" (Pasal 6).
  - No sanction provision. — [Permen PU 16/2014 (BPK)](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/119238/PermenPU16-2014.pdf)
- **SPM content as reported:**
  - Main carriageway: "tidak ada lubang".
  - Drainage and median: 100% functional.
  - Tow truck within **30 minutes** of information.
  - Traffic obstruction handling: max **30 minutes**.
  - **No pothole handling deadline is stated** in the secondary source. — [detikOto, 13 Sep 2021](https://oto.detik.com/berita/d-5721058/termasuk-kecepatan-dan-kualitas-jalan-ini-standar-pelayanan-jalan-tol)
- **SPM reform 2026:**
  - The current SPM has 8 substansi and 49 indicators, no sanctions, and only one skid/roughness test a year.
  - The draft Permen consolidates this into **3 parameters and 64 indicators**. It requires skid/roughness tests at least every 3 months and CCTV every 500 m (urban) or 1 km (intercity).
  - Sanctions: **teguran tertulis, penundaan penyesuaian tarif, denda administratif (as PNBP), pembatalan PPJT**.
  - Target enactment was mid-October 2026, pending Kemenkeu approval of the PNBP fine. — [detikFinance, 9 Jul 2026](https://finance.detik.com/infrastruktur/d-8567118/aturan-spm-tol-dikebut-bujt-melanggar-siap-siap-didenda)
- **BUJT reporting to BPJT (draft guideline):**
  - Quarterly operations reports, due on "Hari keduapuluh setiap bulan berikutnya".
  - Special reports on significant incidents within **24 hours**.
  - SPM data from BUJT self-assessment, consultant checks and **BPJT random checks**; daily SPM checklists signed by BUJT staff. — [BPJT draft Pedoman Laporan Pengoperasian dan Pemeliharaan Jalan Tol](https://simk.bpjt.pu.go.id/file_uploads/ketentuan/Penyampaian_Pedoman_Laporan__Pengoperasian_dan_Pemeliharaan_Jalan_Tol_pdf_21-06-2023_07-35-58.pdf)
- **BPJT includes a public member.** The Hukumonline author describes himself as "Anggota BPJT unsur masyarakat, Kementerian Pekerjaan Umum". — [Hukumonline, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/mengulik-tanggung-jawab-hukum-jalan-berlubang-lt699592a17b4f9?page=2)

### Inferences
- **For toll reports, BUJT is both the "road authority" and the "contractor".** The app's oversight role over BUJT should be **BPJT (Toll Regulator)**, not a PPK. Toll reports should go to the BUJT operator desk, with BPJT read and escalation access.
- **Do not hard-code a pothole SLA for toll roads.** None was verified, and the SPM is being replaced. Make the SLA a configurable, regulation-referenced parameter.

- **2026 SPM Permen:** whether it was actually enacted after the October 2026 target was not verified.

## 4. Provincial, kabupaten/kota and desa road authorities

### Takeaway
Regional roads are run by the regional public-works dinas. Names vary (Dinas PUPR, Dinas Bina Marga, Dinas PU Bina Marga dan SDA, etc.), and they typically have a Bina Marga field and UPTD units. Village roads belong to the Pemerintah Desa under UU 2/2022 Pasal 16A, with kabupaten guidance and takeover powers. Desa road status is set by the Bupati under PP 34/2006 Pasal 62(5). The general reporting path is: responsible agency first, then Ombudsman if there is no response.

### Cited Findings
- **Regional authority and status-setting:** see §1 (UU 2/2022 Pasal 15, 16, 16A, 19–21A; PP 34/2006 Pasal 62). — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf); [PP 34/2006](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp34-2006.pdf)
- **Reporting path (Ombudsman RI):** report location and condition to the responsible agency — the ministry for national roads, "dinas pekerjaan umum" for regional roads — and escalate to the Ombudsman if there is no response. The article frames roads as a public service under **UU 25/2009** (Pelayanan Publik). — [Ombudsman RI, 3 Sep 2021](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkinternal--jalan-rusak-dan-cara-melaporkannya)
- **Village governance (KPK, 2020):**
  - Village fund (Dana Desa) risks: "Penggunaan Dana Desa tidak transparan" and "Penyalahgunaan Dana Desa".
  - Controls: SISKEUDES and SISWASKEUDES, LPJ to the **Inspektorat**, publication of APBDes, and Inspektorat audit of high-risk Dana Desa. — [KPK Korwil VI deck, 10 Mar 2020](https://binapemdes.kemendagri.go.id/Download/Paparan_KPK_-_10_Maret_2020.pdf)

- **Regional dinas nomenclature is not uniform.** The app should model "Regional Road Authority" generically, with a display name and org-tree per region. It should not hard-code "Dinas PUPR".
- **For jalan desa, the accountable party is the Kepala Desa / Pemerintah Desa, with Inspektorat Kabupaten as auditor.** Kabupaten Dinas PU has a guidance role. This is inferred from UU 2/2022 Pasal 16(1) and 16A and the KPK deck.

## 6. Official procurement and project roles: legal basis, duties, documents

### Takeaway
Under **Perpres 16/2018 jo. Perpres 12/2021 jo. Perpres 46/2025**, the pelaku pengadaan are:
- **PA** and **KPA**: budget authority.
- **PPK**: contract owner. Prepares HPS, specifications and contract, controls the contract, and hands results to PA/KPA by berita acara.
- **Pejabat Pengadaan**: pengadaan langsung / penunjukan langsung / e-purchasing.
- **Pokja Pemilihan**, under the **UKPBJ**: tenders.
- **Agen Pengadaan**.
- **Penyelenggara Swakelola**: Tim Persiapan / Tim Pelaksana / Tim Pengawas.
- **Penyedia**.

**PjPHP/PPHP were abolished in 2021.** In APBD settings, KPA may double as PPK, and a PPTK may be assigned PPK duties.

### Cited Findings
- **PjPHP/PPHP abolished (Perpres 12/2021):** Pasal 8 huruf g "dihapus"; Pasal 9(1)(i) "dihapus"; Pasal 15 "Dihapus". — [BPK Kaltim matrix](https://kaltim.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Matriks-Perubahan-Perpres-Barjas-Pemerintah.pdf)
- **Serah terima (amended Pasal 58):** "PPK menyerahkan barang/jasa ... kepada PA/KPA" and "Serah terima ... dituangkan dalam berita acara". New PPK task in Pasal 11(1)(l): "menyerahkan hasil pekerjaan pelaksanaan kegiatan kepada PA/KPA dengan berita acara penyerahan". — [BPK Kaltim matrix](https://kaltim.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Matriks-Perubahan-Perpres-Barjas-Pemerintah.pdf)
- **Allowed role overlaps (Perpres 12/2021):** "KPA pada Pengadaan Barang/Jasa yang menggunakan anggaran belanja dari APBD, dapat merangkap sebagai PPK" (Pasal 10(5)). Where no PPK is set (APBD), PA/KPA assigns a **PPTK** to perform PPK tasks (Pasal 11(3)). — [BPK Kaltim matrix](https://kaltim.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Matriks-Perubahan-Perpres-Barjas-Pemerintah.pdf)
- **Perpres 46/2025 (second amendment):**
  - **PA (Pasal 9(1))** sets RUP; appoints PPK, Pejabat Pengadaan and Penyelenggara Swakelola; names winners for Tender/E-purchasing/PL above **Rp100 miliar** and for consultancy above **Rp10 miliar**. The new 9(1)(f2) lets the PA adjust procedures "untuk mengisi kekosongan hukum". For APBD, the PA may delegate to the KPA (9(3)).
  - **KPA (Pasal 10)** handles sanggah banding for construction tenders, may perform PPK duties, and "wajib memiliki pengetahuan tentang pengadaan barang dan jasa serta PPK" (10(6)).
  - **PPK (Pasal 11)** handles specifications, draft contract, HPS, advance payments and e-purchasing above Rp200 juta. PPKs must hold a **Sertifikat Kompetensi PPK** for their typology (11(2a)). A PPTK acting as PPK must meet PPK competency (11(3)–(4)).
  - **Pokja Pemilihan (Pasal 13)** runs selection except pengadaan langsung and direct e-purchasing. It sets winners up to Rp100 miliar (construction) and Rp10 miliar (consultancy), with 3 members (odd number).
  - **UKPBJ** is the "pusat keunggulan" procurement unit (Pasal 1 angka 11). **Agen Pengadaan** is a UKPBJ or business acting mutatis mutandis as Pokja/PPK (Pasal 14).
  - **Pengadaan Langsung thresholds:** construction up to **Rp400 juta**; goods/other services up to **Rp200 juta**; consultancy up to **Rp100 juta** (Pasal 1 angka 40, 40a, 41).
  - **Other changes:** an electronic transactional system is mandatory above Rp50 juta for PL and for tenders (Pasal 38(8)); "Tender cepat"; PL by presidential direction (Pasal 41A); 4 swakelola types (Pasal 47); ≥40% of budget to UMK/cooperatives (Pasal 20(3)).
  - **Conflict-of-interest rules (Pasal 7(2)):** they cover cooperative managers doubling as PPK/Pokja/Pejabat Pengadaan, and anyone who "baik langsung maupun tidak langsung mengendalikan atau menjalankan badan usaha Penyedia".
  - **No general ban** on one person being both PPK and Pokja on the same package appears in the extracted text. — [Perpres 46/2025 (OCR, BKD Jabar)](https://bkd.jabarprov.go.id/ppid/storage/files/shares/Peraturan/PERPRES_NO_46_2025.pdf)
- **Swakelola organisation (Pasal 16(1)):** "Tim Persiapan, Tim Pelaksana, dan/atau Tim Pengawas".
  - Tim Persiapan: targets, plan, schedule, budget.
  - Tim Pelaksana: executes, records, reports progress.
  - **Tim Pengawas: oversees physical and administrative implementation.**
  - The PA designates the Penyelenggara Swakelola (Pasal 9(1)(j)). — [BPK Kaltim matrix](https://kaltim.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Matriks-Perubahan-Perpres-Barjas-Pemerintah.pdf)
- **Perpres 46/2025 policy emphasis:** e-Katalog priority, contracts as a risk-control tool (performance, advance and maintenance guarantees), and HPS based on verifiable market references. This is an opinion article. — [DJPb KPPN Watampone](https://djpb.kemenkeu.go.id/kppn/watampone/id/data-publikasi/artikel/4129-perpres-46-2025-percepat-pengadaan,-transparansi-jadi-kunci.html)
- **Internal oversight bodies:**
  - Kementerian PU has an **Inspektorat Jenderal** (eselon I). — [Perpres 170/2024](https://pasal.id/peraturan/perpres/perpres-no-170-tahun-2024)
  - Regional **Inspektorat** audit village funds. — [KPK deck 2020](https://binapemdes.kemendagri.go.id/Download/Paparan_KPK_-_10_Maret_2020.pdf)
- **External escalation:** the **Ombudsman** is the public-service oversight body to escalate to when road agencies don't respond. — [Ombudsman RI](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkinternal--jalan-rusak-dan-cara-melaporkannya)

### Inferences
- **The PPK is the legally accountable "Approver" for contracted works.** The PPK prepares and controls the contract and hands results to the PA/KPA. The **PA/KPA** is the "Budget Owner" who receives the handover.
- **Since PjPHP/PPHP no longer exist,** the app should not create a separate "Acceptance Committee" role. Acceptance evidence comes from the PPK plus supervision consultant/Tim Pengawas, with APIP auditing afterwards.
- **The app must allow, and log, the legal overlaps:** KPA=PPK (APBD) and PPTK-as-PPK. It must **block** any user linked to a Penyedia from holding any government role on the same package (Pasal 7(2)(e)).

- **Contract document chain:** SPK/SPMK, MC-0 (Mutual Check 0%) and MC-100, laporan harian/mingguan/bulanan, addendum/CCO, BA Serah Terima Pertama (PHO) and Akhir (FHO) after masa pemeliharaan, as-built drawings, BA pembayaran/termin.
  - These are standard in Permen PUPR 14/2020 (Standar dan Pedoman Pengadaan Jasa Konstruksi melalui Penyedia), as amended, and in Perpres 16/2018 Pasal 57. **The article text was not retrieved.**
- **Konsultan Perencana / Konsultan Pengawas (Supervisi) duties and Direksi Teknis / Direksi Lapangan:** no source retrieved.

## 8. Where corruption risk concentrates

### Takeaway
KPK data put about **25% of its cases (446 of 1,782)** in procurement. Rigging is often designed **before tendering** (planning stage), with commitment fees, "ijon" and "panjer" payments. For toll roads, KPK flagged weak supervision of BUJT non-compliance, BUMN Karya conflicts of interest and BPJT officials sitting as BUJT commissioners. For village roads, the risks are non-transparent Dana Desa use and misuse, with Inspektorat audit as the control.

### Cited Findings
- **Procurement share and modus:**
  - 446 of 1,782 KPK cases (~25%) relate to procurement.
  - Irregularities can be designed before the planning and tender stage.
  - Modus: advance "panjer" payments, "ijon" project bribes, commitment fees.
  - National procurement remains a "zona merah" in MCSP and SPI assessments.
  - KPK spokesperson Budi Prasetyo: "sektor pengadaan masih menjadi ruang rentan yang dimanfaatkan". — [Hukumonline, 21 Apr 2026](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/kpk-ingatkan-25-persen-perkara-korupsi-berkaitan-dengan-pengadaan-lt69e75d09c3674/?page=all)
- **Toll roads (KPK, Pahala Nainggolan, 9 Mar 2023):**
  - Outdated rules in planning, and tender documents lacking technical information.
  - **No mechanism for repeated BUJT non-compliance.**
  - BUMN Karya hold 61.9% of construction investment, creating a conflict of interest.
  - **Five BPJT officials serve as commissioners of BUJT** ("BPJT itu kan dia mengawasi semua yang mengoperasikan jalan tol ... ada lima orang BPJT ternyata komisaris di jalan tol").
  - Rp4.5 triliun potential loss from unrepaid land-acquisition bridge funds. — [IDX Channel, 9 Mar 2023](https://www.idxchannel.com/news/kpk-endus-kerawanan-korupsi-di-proyek-jalan-tol-ri/all)
- **Village funds (KPK 2020):** "Penggunaan Dana Desa tidak transparan", "Penyalahgunaan Dana Desa". Mark-up and spec reduction appear in the general procurement risk table. — [KPK deck, 10 Mar 2020](https://binapemdes.kemendagri.go.id/Download/Paparan_KPK_-_10_Maret_2020.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Highest-value app controls map onto these risks:**
  1. **Independent, geotagged, timestamped before/after evidence.** This counters fictitious or under-spec work in the execution and handover stages: the PPK–contractor–supervisor triangle.
  2. **Tamper-evident audit log readable by APIP.**
  3. **Separation of duties: no one both executes and verifies the same job.**
  4. **Recurrence analytics:** the same segment repaired repeatedly may signal sub-spec work. Feeding this to Inspektorat/BPJT addresses KPK's "no mechanism for repeated non-compliance" finding.
- **BPJT conflict risk:** because KPK found BPJT officials holding BUJT commissioner seats, the app's Toll Regulator role should have **read and flag** rights over BUJT jobs, never the ability to close them.

- **LKPP procurement risk map** for construction was not retrieved.

## 9. Recommended app role model (English name ↔ Indonesian equivalent, with separation of duties)

### Takeaway
Model the app on **three lines** that mirror Indonesian law:
1. **The road authority chain:** status holder → Balai/Dinas → PPK or Suku Dinas/Satpel → crew or contractor.
2. **Independent verification:** supervision consultant or Tim Pengawas, plus the citizen.
3. **Oversight:** APIP, BPJT for toll roads, Ombudsman, BPK/BPKP and DPRD.

Route every report by **road status from the SK registry**. Enforce separation of duties where Perpres 16/2018 jo. 12/2021 jo. 46/2025 requires it, and allow the overlaps it explicitly permits.

### Cited Findings (legal anchors for the model)
- **Citizen rights to input, information and reporting:** UU 2/2022 Pasal 62(1); PP 34/2006 Pasal 120. — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf); [PP 34/2006](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp34-2006.pdf)
- **Authority by status, and status by SK:** UU 2/2022 Pasal 14–16A; PP 34/2006 Pasal 62. — [UU 2/2022](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf); [PP 34/2006](https://peraturan.go.id/files/pp34-2006.pdf)
- **PPK is the contract owner and hands results to PA/KPA by berita acara; PjPHP/PPHP abolished.** — [BPK Kaltim matrix](https://kaltim.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Matriks-Perubahan-Perpres-Barjas-Pemerintah.pdf)
- **Permitted overlaps:** KPA=PPK (APBD); PPTK as PPK. **Forbidden:** any control of a Penyedia by procurement officials. — [Perpres 46/2025](https://bkd.jabarprov.go.id/ppid/storage/files/shares/Peraturan/PERPRES_NO_46_2025.pdf); [BPK Kaltim matrix](https://kaltim.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Matriks-Perubahan-Perpres-Barjas-Pemerintah.pdf)
- **Swakelola** has distinct Tim Pelaksana and Tim Pengawas. — [BPK Kaltim matrix](https://kaltim.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Matriks-Perubahan-Perpres-Barjas-Pemerintah.pdf)
- **Toll:** BUJT runs preservation and SPM self-evaluation; BPJT supervises and reports to the Minister; DJBM handles laik fungsi. — [Permen PU 2/2025](https://jdih.kemenkoinfra.go.id/cfind/source/files/permen-pu/2025pmpu2.pdf); [Permen PU 16/2014](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/119238/PermenPU16-2014.pdf)
- **Escalation:** Ombudsman, after the agency fails to respond. — [Ombudsman RI](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkinternal--jalan-rusak-dan-cara-melaporkannya)

### Inferences (recommended design; this is the researcher's proposal, not a legal requirement)

**A. Roles**

| # | App role (English) | Indonesian equivalent(s) | Core duties in app | Key permissions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Citizen Reporter** | Masyarakat / Pelapor (UU 2/2022 Ps. 62; PP 34/2006 Ps. 120) | Submit geotagged report with photo; track status; confirm or dispute fix | Create report; view own; comment; confirm/dispute closure. **Cannot** close or reassign. |
| 2 | **Public Viewer** | Publik (hak memperoleh informasi, Ps. 62(1)) | View anonymised map, SLA stats per authority | Read-only public data; no personal data |
| 3 | **Agency Administrator** | Admin Penyelenggara Jalan: Bagian Umum & TU (BBPJN/BPJN), Sekretariat Dinas, BUJT ops admin | Manage org tree, users, ruas registry (with SK no.), contract-to-segment links | User/role admin within own agency only; cannot act on reports |
| 4 | **Dispatcher / Triage Coordinator** | Operator Posko / Pusdatin (e.g., Posko Dinas Bina Marga DKI), Bidang Preservasi team (Balai), Koordinator Satpel | Validate report; confirm jurisdiction; re-route misrouted reports with SK citation; assign to field unit or PPK | Assign/reassign; merge duplicates; mark "bukan kewenangan" (not our road) with reason + SK ref |
| 5 | **Field Inspector** | Petugas Penilik Jalan / Tim Preservasi / Satpel Bina Marga Kecamatan / Seksi Pemeliharaan | Site verification, severity rating, measurements, recommend treatment (rutin/berkala/rehab) | Update assessment; upload evidence; cannot approve own recommendation for payment |
| 6 | **Maintenance Crew (In-house)** | Tim Pelaksana Swakelola; PJLP "pasukan kuning" (DKI) | Execute swakelola repairs; upload before/after; mark "temporary patch" vs "permanent" | Update job progress; upload evidence; **cannot** close job |
| 7 | **Contractor** | Penyedia Jasa (kontraktor); for toll roads the **BUJT** | Execute instructed work under contract; upload daily evidence and completion | Upload evidence on assigned jobs only; **cannot** verify or close; no access to other bidders' data |
| 8 | **Supervision Verifier** | Konsultan Pengawas/Supervisi; Tim Pengawas Swakelola; Direksi Teknis | Independently verify quality and quantity of completed work | Verify/reject completion; must be a different person/organisation from executor |
| 9 | **Commitment Officer (Approver)** | **PPK** (or PPTK assigned PPK duties; or KPA acting as PPK in APBD) | Issue work instruction under contract; accept verified work; record handover to PA/KPA | Approve/close contract jobs; requires competency flag; cannot approve jobs where they are linked to the Penyedia |
| 10 | **Budget Owner** | PA / KPA (Kepala Balai as KPA for national roads; Kepala Dinas as PA) | Receive handover; view portfolio, SLA and recurrence dashboards | Read-all within agency; acknowledge handover; no field edits |
| 11 | **Toll Regulator** | BPJT (and DJBM for laik fungsi) | Monitor BUJT SLA/SPM compliance; flag non-compliance | Read all toll jobs; flag/escalate; **cannot** close BUJT jobs |
| 12 | **Internal Auditor** | APIP: Inspektorat Jenderal Kementerian PU; Inspektorat Provinsi/Kab/Kota (also audits Dana Desa) | Audit trail review; sample checks; recurrence/anomaly review | Read-only everything in scope plus immutable log; can open audit flags; no operational actions |
| 13 | **External Overseer** (optional, scoped) | Ombudsman RI / Perwakilan; BPK; BPKP; DPRD Komisi | Receive escalations (Ombudsman); read exports on request | Read-only scoped exports; escalation inbox for Ombudsman |
| 14 | **Village Road Officer** | Pemerintah Desa / Kepala Desa (Ps. 16A), Tim Pelaksana desa | Handle jalan desa reports; Dinas PU Kab is guidance; Inspektorat Kab audits | Same as Dispatcher + Approver for desa scope |

**B. Separation-of-duties rules**

| Rule | Basis |
|---|---|
| **SoD-1** Executor ≠ Verifier on the same job (Crew/Contractor vs Supervision Verifier/Inspector) | Mirrors Perpres Ps. 16 distinct Tim Pelaksana / Tim Pengawas, and the contractor–supervisor split |
| **SoD-2** Anyone linked to a Penyedia cannot hold Dispatcher, Inspector, Verifier, PPK, PA/KPA or Auditor roles on that package | Perpres Ps. 7(2)(e) |
| **SoD-3** PPK approval requires prior independent verification (SoD-1) and closes into a "handover to PA/KPA" record | Ps. 11(1)(l), Ps. 58 |
| **SoD-4** Auditor roles are read-only and cannot be combined with any operational role in the same agency | Inference from APIP independence; legal text not retrieved |
| **SoD-5** Permitted overlaps are configurable but always logged and visible to the Auditor: KPA+PPK (APBD only); PPTK acting as PPK | Ps. 10(5), 11(3)–(4) |
| **SoD-6** Toll Regulator (BPJT) can flag but never close BUJT jobs | KPK finding on BPJT–BUJT conflicts |
| **SoD-7** Changing a report's jurisdiction requires citing the ruas status SK, plus a second Dispatcher or Agency Admin confirmation when re-routing across government levels | Status is legally set by SK; Batam case shows status changes |
| **SoD-8** Citizen confirmation is recorded but not required for closure; a citizen "dispute" automatically reopens a verification task and is visible to the Auditor and Ombudsman escalation | Design choice |

**C. Routing algorithm**

1. Geolocate the report to a segment.
2. Look up the segment's status in the versioned SK registry.
3. Route by status:
   - **Tol** → BUJT operator desk (Toll Regulator = BPJT watcher).
   - **Nasional** → BBPJN/BPJN by province, then to the segment PPK. For DKI and Jabar: BBPJN DKI Jakarta–Jawa Barat.
   - **Provinsi** → provincial Dinas. For DKI: Dinas Bina Marga → Suku Dinas [kota] → Satpel [kecamatan].
   - **Kabupaten/Kota** → regional Dinas PU / Bina Marga, then UPTD/seksi.
   - **Desa** → Pemerintah Desa, with Kab Dinas PU as guidance.
   - **Khusus** → registered owner (BUMN/BUMD/company). The app has no enforcement role here, so give the citizen a notice.
4. Defect type can override the target unit where local rules differ (e.g., PJU in DKI → Bidang/Seksi PJU of Dinas Bina Marga).
5. SLA timers come from configurable, regulation-referenced parameters, e.g. UU 22/2009 Ps. 24 "segera dan patut" plus a signage duty if repair is not yet possible, the toll SPM, or a local SOP.

### Gaps
- **PPK competency:** whether certification (Ps. 11(2a)) can be checked against an LKPP registry via API is unknown.
- **APIP independence rules:** the exact rules (PP 60/2008 SPIP) behind SoD-4 were not retrieved.
- **Official English names:** no official English names exist in regulations. The English role names above are proposals and should be checked against any LKPP or Kementerian PU English glossaries.
