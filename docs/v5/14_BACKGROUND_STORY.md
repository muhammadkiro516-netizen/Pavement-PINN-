## 0. The story in 30 seconds (spoken, English)

"In January 2026, police counted 27 pothole crashes in Greater Jakarta in 28 days. In February, four riders died in 13 days on one damaged road near Tangerang. People do report — but reports land in inboxes that don't know whose road it is, 'done' is declared by the same office that is graded on it, and too often repairs come only after a video goes viral. And when money is spent, courts and auditors keep finding the same thing: paid in full, built short, broken again. PAVEMENT-PINN connects a verified citizen photo to the office legally responsible for that road, keeps the case visible until it is fixed, and checks — with physics and a fair-cost estimate — whether the repair was the right size, the right price, and actually lasted."

One-line pitch (from shared decisions): **We feed existing channels like SP4N-LAPOR! and JAKI; we are the accountability layer between a complaint and a lasting repair — not another inbox.**

## 1. Problem 1 — Road damage, small and large, is deadly

### 1.1 Small damage kills riders
- **Jakarta area, 1–28 Jan 2026:** 27 pothole-caused crashes — 1 dead, 8 seriously injured, 18 lightly injured (Ditlantas Polda Metro Jaya). — [detikOto, 1 Feb 2026](https://oto.detik.com/berita/d-8334947/jalan-berlubang-bikin-celaka-ada-yang-meninggal-dunia)
- **Pasar Kemis, Kab. Tangerang, 1–13 Feb 2026:** four motorcyclists died in 13 days; holes 15–25 cm deep; all four fell and were then run over by heavy vehicles; the last victim (18) fell into a hole **hidden under a puddle**. Reported by VOI quoting Azas Tigor Nainggolan (KAWAT). — [VOI, Feb 2026](https://voi.id/en/amp/560365)
- **Matraman Raya, Jakarta, 9 Feb 2026:** a 16-year-old SMKN 34 student died after his motorcycle hit a deep pothole on a road with uneven patches; the Governor ordered all potholes covered "even if temporarily". (Sources conflict on whether another vehicle was involved and on speed — say "died after hitting a pothole", nothing more.) — [JawaPos, 9 Feb 2026](https://www.jawapos.com/jabodetabek/2602090127/tragedi-matraman-raya-siswa-smkn-34-meninggal-dunia-usai-motor-hantam-jalan-berlubang); [Akurat, 9 Feb 2026](https://www.akurat.co/megapolitan/833822/pramono-minta-maaf-soal-pelajar-smk-34-jakarta-tewas-kecelakaan-bakal-segera-perbaiki-jalan-berlubang)
- **Other dated deaths:** Singaraja–Gilimanuk, Bali, 25 Dec 2025 (student, wearing a helmet) — [detikBali](https://www.detik.com/bali/berita/d-8278955/jalan-berlubang-di-jalan-singaraja-gilimanuk-tewaskan-mahasiswi-asal-jember); Karanganyar, 20 Oct 2025 (80-year-old rider run over by a dump truck after falling at a pothole) — [detikJateng](https://www.detik.com/jateng/berita/d-8169590/mbah-nardi-tewas-tertabrak-truk-usai-terperosok-jalan-berlubang-di-karanganyar).
- **The pattern:** a pothole makes a rider fall; a following or oncoming truck or bus finishes the crash (Pasar Kemis, Karanganyar, Wonogiri). — sources above + [detikJateng, 30 Jan 2025](https://www.detik.com/jateng/berita/d-7755343/pemotor-tewas-usai-jatuh-dan-ditabrak-bus-di-jalan-berlubang-wonogiri/amp)
- **Rain makes it worse:** about **6,000 potholes** in Jakarta after January 2026 rains and floods, handled with **temporary patches**; the head of Dinas Bina Marga DKI said asphalt is weak against water and traffic "pumping" worsens it. — [CNN Indonesia, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/nasional/20260129164522-20-1322469/6000-titik-jalan-rusak-akibat-hujan-dki-lakukan-tambal-sementara)
- **Patches can be hazards too:** at Matraman, uneven patched sections were cited by witnesses. — [JawaPos, 9 Feb 2026](https://www.jawapos.com/jabodetabek/2602090127/tragedi-matraman-raya-siswa-smkn-34-meninggal-dunia-usai-motor-hantam-jalan-berlubang)

**What "small damage" means (explain on the Why page):**
- Bina Marga's Surface Distress Index (SDI) counts crack area, crack width, **number of potholes per km/100 m** and rut depth; condition classes Baik < 50, Sedang 50–100, Rusak Ringan 100–150, Rusak Berat > 150. SDI does **not** score pothole depth. — [Jurnal Konstruksia UMJ, Dec 2023](https://jurnal.umj.ac.id/index.php/konstruksia/article/download/18794/9675)
- International practice (ASTM D6433-style, via FAA PAVEAIR) grades pothole severity by **maximum depth** (13–25 mm, 25–50 mm, > 50 mm) × diameter. — [FAA PAVEAIR](https://faapaveair.faa.gov/Help/Documents/levelsofseverityforpotholes.htm)
- **No Indonesian standard defines a pothole size that is "dangerous to motorcyclists"** (none found). The holes in the reported fatal cases were 15–25 cm deep (Pasar Kemis) and ~15–20 cm (Ciputat 2022, resident estimate). So a hole that counts as "rusak ringan" on a survey sheet can still kill a rider. Say "small enough to ignore on paper, deep enough to throw a rider into traffic".
- BPS classifies road condition by passable speed: "rusak" = 20–40 km/h, "rusak berat" = 0–20 km/h. — [Kumparan, 8 May 2023](https://kumparan.com/kumparannews/31-91-jalan-di-indonesia-rusak-paling-parah-di-ntt-riau-dan-papua-barat-20McV5n5wJj)

### 1.2 Large damage cuts people off
- **Lampung 2023:** kabupaten roads (17,774 km) were only **50 % mantap** in 2022 data, provincial 77 %, national 95 %. — [Kontan, 6 May 2023](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/anggaran-rp-800-miliar-disiapkan-untuk-perbaiki-15-ruas-jalan-daerah-di-lampung)
- **Jambi coal trucks:** Dirjen Bina Marga told Komisi V that repairs would cost ~**Rp 824 billion under normal traffic but Rp 8.4 trillion** under overloaded coal trucks; trucks at 2× legal load do **16× the damage** (29 Mar 2023). — [Bisnis.com](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20230329/45/1641788/gara-gara-truk-batu-bara-biaya-perbaikan-jalan-di-jambi-bengkak-jadi-rp84-triliun)
- **Overloading loss:** Rp 43.45 trillion a year in state material losses from ODOL trucks damaging roads (Coordinating Minister AHY, 6 May 2025; method not given — say "government estimate"). — [detikFinance, 7 May 2025](https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-7902930/truk-obesitas-kian-merajalela-bikin-negara-rugi-rp-43-t-per-tahun)
- **When ambulances can't pass:** Bima, NTB, Jan 2025 — a woman pregnant with twins was carried 2 km on a bamboo-and-sarong stretcher; both babies died at hospital. — [detikBali/Nusra, 26 Jan 2025](https://www.detik.com/bali/nusra/d-7751082/bayi-kembar-ibu-hamil-yang-ditandu-gegara-jalan-rusak-di-bima-meninggal). Polewali Mandar/Majene, Sep 2024 — a village midwife carried 23 km. — [Metro TV, 9 Sep 2024](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/NrWC5zdZ-jalan-rusak-warga-polewali-mandar-ditandu-23-kilometer-menuju-rumah-sakit)

### 1.3 Most damage is on local roads
- PUPR (May 2023): road condition "mantap" (good + fair) — **national 92 %, provincial 72 %, kabupaten/kota 58 %**. — [Kontan, 14 May 2023](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/perbaiki-jalan-daerah-yang-rusak-berat-pemerintah-anggarkan-rp-149-triliun-di-2023)
- National roads 2026: 93.5 % mantap (Dirjen Bina Marga to Komisi V). — [CNBC Indonesia, 7 Sep 2026](https://cnbcindonesia.com/news/20260907135512-4-765806/kondisi-jalan-nasional-ri-mantap-935)
- Kabupaten/kota roads total **432,294 km** (BPS 2024 via Databoks) out of ≈ 539,500 km in all three levels → **about 4 of every 5 km** are regency/city roads (our calculation from BPS 2024 figures reported by GoodStats/Databoks). **[check]** against the BPS table before using the exact share. — [Databoks, 7 Dec 2025](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/utilities/statistics/692fea0141d54/distribution-of-severely-damaged-regencycity-roads-in-indonesia-2024); [GoodStats, 6 Dec 2025](https://goodstats.id/article/ragam-kondisi-jalan-indonesia-menurut-tingkat-kewenangan-C0JMA)
- Responsibility is split by law: national roads → central government; provincial → province; kabupaten, kota, desa → local governments (UU 38/2004 as amended by **UU 2/2022**; status decrees per PP 34/2006). National road decree: **Kepmen PUPR 1688/KPTS/M/2022**. — [UU 2/2022 PDF](https://peraturan.go.id/files/uu2-2022.pdf)
- Legal duty: **UU 22/2009 Pasal 24** — road operators must promptly repair damage that can cause accidents, and must put up warning signs until repaired; Pasal 273 sets penalties up to 5 years if someone dies. No conviction of an official was found. — [Hukumonline, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/mengulik-tanggung-jawab-hukum-jalan-berlubang-lt699592a17b4f9/?page=all); [VOI](https://voi.id/en/amp/560365)

## 2. Problem 2 — Reporting channels fail: "no viral, no justice"

### 2.1 Channels exist — many of them
- **SP4N-LAPOR!**: > 1.1 million complaints 2021–Sep 2026, 718 connected agencies, "No Wrong Door" policy. — [Investortrust, 30 Sep 2026](https://investortrust.id/national/117872/pemerintah-pastikan-aduan-masyarakat-di-sp4n-lapor-jadi-masukan-perbaikan-kebijakan)
- **Jakarta CRM** joins 14 channels; roads were the **#1 category: 3,705 of 18,892 reports (≈ 20 %) in Nov 2025**. — [GoodStats, 10 Jan 2026](https://goodstats.id/article/jalan-jadi-keluhan-terbanyak-warga-jakarta-0Su8L); [JSC presentation](https://rumahpusbin.kemdikbud.go.id/datapusbin/580_CRM_Paparan_Pengelolaan_Aduan_Warga_Jakarta.pdf)
- New channels keep appearing: Bina Marga DKI pothole hotline (Jan 2026), "Halo Pak Dody" Instagram DM for the PU Minister (Mar 2026), Lapor Mas Wapres (16,505 complaints in year one, 66 % via WhatsApp). — [Berita Jakarta](https://www.beritajakarta.id/en/read/61700/reporting-potholes-just-got-easier-with-jakartas-new-hotline); [TVRI](https://nasional.tvrinews.com/berita/tl6hqqk-menteri-pu-buka-jalur-laporan-publik-lewat-halo-pak-dody); [RCTI+](https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/terkini/5273159/setahun-lapor-mas-wapres-ada-16505-aduan-masyarakat)
- **So the problem is not "no channel". It is what happens after the report.**

### 2.2 Why reports stall
1. **Nobody knows whose road it is.** Central Java's "Jalan Cantik" app (2019): of 397 categorised reports, only 21 (≈ 5 %) were on provincial roads the app owner could fix; the rest had to be forwarded, with no tracked time. — [Antara Jateng, 23 Oct 2019](https://jateng.antaranews.com/berita/270924/aplikasi-jalan-cantik-dibanjiri-laporan-jalan-rusak-di-jateng). DIY acts only on provincial roads; a toll operator and a regency exchanged letters while residents planted banana trees (Klaten, 2025). — [VICE, 2023](https://www.vice.com/id/article/investigasi-kecil-kecilan-vice-gimana-ya-caranya-mengadukan-jalan-rusak-agar-diperbaiki/); [detikJateng, 22 Mar 2025](https://www.detik.com/jateng/berita/d-7836468/jalan-klaten-gunung-kidul-ditanami-pohon-pisang-dpu-surati-tol)
2. **Slow clocks, missed even at the top.** SP4N-LAPOR! rules: verify within 3 working days; complaints needing a field check up to **60 working days**. KemenPANRB's own unit averaged **5.2 days** to verify in 2023; 257 of 464 reports took longer than 3 days. — [KemenPANRB Monev 2023](https://ppid.menpan.go.id/system/uploads/2025/10/Laporan-Monev-Mekanisme-Pengelolaan-SP4N-LAPOR-2023.pdf)
3. **"Done" is self-declared.** JAKI's "Selesai" means "the report has been handled", set by the agency; no reporter confirmation step documented. — [JAKI Help](https://jaki.jakarta.go.id/id/help/jak-respons/pending-coordination-disposition-process-and-completed-statuses-on-report/). In **April 2026, three PPSU officers were sanctioned for using AI-altered photos** to close JAKI reports. — [Metro TV, 15 Apr 2026](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/kewCjmzm-3-ppsu-dijatuhi-surat-peringatan-buntut-manipulasi-laporan-jaki)
4. **Email and SMS go unanswered.** Ombudsman (2019): complaints by email/SMS are "often merely received without a reply". — [Ombudsman RI, 28 Jan 2019](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/artikel--kanal-pengaduan-pelayanan-publik-setengah-hati)
5. **Repairs follow virality.** Ombudsman (Jul 2026) calls "no viral no justice" a crisis of trust: agencies act fast only after a complaint goes viral; "Negara tidak boleh membiarkan keadilan pelayanan bergantung pada algoritma media sosial." — [Ombudsman RI, 14 Jul 2026](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkmedia--no-viral-no-justice-cermin-krisis-kepercayaan-terhadap-pengaduan-publik)
   - **Lampung 2023:** a resident said "10 years, not a single repair"; after one TikTok went viral, the President visited (5 May 2023) and pledged **Rp 800 billion** for 15 road sections; the critic was reported to police. — [Republika, 3 May 2023](https://news.republika.co.id/berita/ru1bwh409/seusai-viral-jalanjalan-rusak-di-lampung-dikebut-diperbaiki-jelang-kunjungan-jokowi); [Kontan, 6 May 2023](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/anggaran-rp-800-miliar-disiapkan-untuk-perbaiki-15-ruas-jalan-daerah-di-lampung); [Kabar24/Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20230505/15/1653000/jokowi-tinjau-jalan-rusak-tiktoker-bima-sindir-pemprov-lampung-kena-loh). Lampung's 2023 road-maintenance budget was **0.98 %** of spending. — [detikFinance, 3 May 2023](https://finance.detik.com/infrastruktur/d-6700909/perjalanan-heboh-jalan-lampung-dajjal-sampai-bikin-jokowi-turun-tangan)
   - **Jakarta Feb 2026:** a Grogol pothole was patched the night after an ojol driver crashed there. — [Republika, 12 Feb 2026](https://news.republika.co.id/berita/tac5yp348/jalan-rusak-di-jakarta-kembali-makan-korban)
   - Road complaints to Ombudsman Lampung rose from **1 (2020) to 107 (2024)**. — [Ombudsman Lampung, 12 Dec 2025](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkmedia--ombudsman-lampung-warning-kepala-daerah)
6. **Low trust in official information:** asked which source they trust to judge government performance, **2 %** chose official government sources vs 30 % social media (Tempo Data Science, n = 1,260, Aug 2026). This measures trust in information sources, not in complaint channels specifically. — [GoodStats Data, 2026](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/sumber-informasi-yang-dipercaya-publik-untuk-menilai-kinerja-pemerintah-2026-HvEQM)

### 2.3 When channels fail, people act themselves
- Ojol drivers (PSDI) in Medan **filled a pothole with stone chips** on 6 Feb 2023 because riders, including a mother and child, kept falling. — [detikSumut, 8 Feb 2023](https://www.detik.com/sumut/hukum-dan-kriminal/d-6556348/ojol-di-medan-tambal-jalan-berlubang-yang-kerap-bikin-pengendara-terjatuh)
- Potholes on Jl. Raya Pasar Minggu, Jakarta, were marked with **white spray-paint X's** (27 Jan 2026) — **who painted them was not stated**. — [Kumparan, 27 Jan 2026](https://kumparan.com/kumparannews/tanda-silang-jalan-berlubang-di-pasar-minggu-jaksel-26iDokenfv0)
- Residents plant **banana trees** in damaged roads as protest (Trenggalek Feb 2025, Klaten Mar 2025, Tulungagung Sep 2025 — "repeatedly reported", Blora May 2026 — self-funded patching first). — [detikJatim](https://www.detik.com/jatim/berita/d-7783034/warga-tanam-pohon-pisang-di-jalan-rusak-ini-langkah-polisi-trenggalek); [detikJateng](https://www.detik.com/jateng/berita/d-7836468/jalan-klaten-gunung-kidul-ditanami-pohon-pisang-dpu-surati-tol); [detikJatim](https://www.detik.com/jatim/berita/d-8112324/jalan-rusak-parah-warga-ngunut-tulungagung-tanami-pohon-pisang); [Warta Ekonomi](https://wartaekonomi.co.id/read614442/sindir-gubernur-jateng-warga-blora-tanam-pohon-pisang-di-jalan-provinsi-yang-rusak-parah)
- Our framing: **spray-paint marks and banana trees are analog hazard reports.** PAVEMENT-PINN turns them into a geotagged, timestamped notice to the legally responsible office — evidence that the operator was told (relevant to UU 22/2009 Pasal 24).

### 2.4 What works elsewhere (to show the gap is solvable)
- FixMyStreet routes reports to the right authority without the citizen knowing boundaries; in Buckinghamshire (2018–19), misrouted reports −19 %, chasing calls −29 %, cost per report 9p vs £5.88 by phone. — [mySociety, 28 Jun 2019](https://www.mysociety.org/2019/06/28/measuring-the-savings-brought-by-fixmystreet-pro/)
- NYC: a 15-day legal repair window; 311 pothole complaints closed in ~1.8 days on average (2025). — [NYC Comptroller audit](https://comptroller.nyc.gov/reports/audit-report-on-the-department-of-transportations-tracking-of-pothole-repairs); [NYC Mayor's Office, 29 Jan 2025](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2025/01/mayor-adams-commissioner-rodriguez-celebrate-dot-crews-filling-administration-s-500-000th)
- Even these rely on self-reported "fixed" and have duplicates — independent repair verification goes beyond them.

## 3. Problem 3 — Waste and corruption: paid in full, built short, broken again

### 3.1 Court-proven and audit-proven patterns (neutral wording; status as of source date)
- **Paid far ahead of real progress:**
  - Simeulue, Aceh (TA 2019): **95 % paid when work was 65 % done**; forensic engineering audit found asphalt thinner than drawings and a base layer never built; Supreme Court raised the ex-Kadis PUPR's sentence to 3 yrs 6 mo (6796 K/Pid.Sus/2022, reported 26 Feb 2025). — [Dandapala (MA)](https://www.dandapala.com/article/detail/ma-lipatgandakan-vonis-terdakwa-korupsi-aspal-jalan-kadis-pupr-dari-aceh)
  - Teluk Bintuni, West Papua (2023): physical progress **51.11 %, 100 % paid**; state loss Rp 7.326 billion (investigation stage, Mar 2025). — [Antara Papua Tengah, 19 Mar 2025](https://papuatengah.antaranews.com/berita/61749/kejaksaan-terima-pengembalian-rp2-miliar-dari-kasus-korupsi-jalan-mogoy-merdey)
  - Bengkalis, Riau: documents manipulated to show 100 % so the final payment came **before handover (PHO)**; KPK-estimated loss ≈ Rp 129 billion (suspects 2021). — [CNN Indonesia, 3 Sep 2021](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/nasional/20210903213727-12-689589/3-tersangka-korupsi-proyek-jalan-di-bengkalis-ditahan-kpk)
- **Less volume or quality than paid for:** Batu Bara, North Sumatra (TA 2023): 12 suspects incl. **4 supervision consultants**, volume reduced, paid 100 % (Kejati Sumut, Sep 2025). — [detik, Sep 2025](https://www.detik.com/sumut/hukum-dan-kriminal/d-8091434/kejati-sumut-kembali-tahan-4-tersangka-korupsi-jalan-batu-bara-total-12-orang). Pidie, Aceh (2022 maintenance): PPTK approved 100 % payment without checking materials; **after the maintenance period the surface sank and cracked** (P21, Apr 2025). — [Antara Aceh, 14 Apr 2025](https://aceh.antaranews.com/berita/382865/kejati-kasus-korupsi-pemeliharaan-jalan-di-pidie-sudah-p21)
- **BPK finds the same thing nationally:** IHPS II 2024 — **"kekurangan volume pekerjaan" Rp 141.13 billion in 83 local governments**. — [BPK IHPS II 2024](https://jatim.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ihps_ii_2024_1762309845-red-1.pdf). Aceh Tamiang: 17 PUPR packages, mostly roads, > Rp 3 billion shortfall (2024). — [BPK clipping](https://sultra.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2.21-Temuan-BPK-RI-17-Paket-Pekerjaan-di-Aceh-Tamiang-Kekurangan-Volume-Rp-3-Miliar.pdf). At PUPR (LK 2023), BPK noted unit-price calculation errors, unbalanced unit prices and **payments ahead of physical progress**. — [DDTCNews, 21 Jul 2024](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1804167/bpk-temukan-beberapa-isu-pada-belanja-pekerjaan-fisik-kementerian-pupr)
- **Fees taken from road projects (bribery cases):** 4 % + 1 % (North Sumatra 2025, convicted), up to 15 % (Labuhanbatu 2024, Regent sentenced to 6 years), 22 % "pokir" fee (OKU 2025, suspects). Say "court and KPK case documents show commitment fees of 1 % to 22 % of project value" — not a single "norm". — [detik](https://www.detik.com/sumut/berita/d-8218259/eks-kadis-pupr-topan-ginting-didakwa-terima-suap-di-kasus-korupsi-jalan-sumut); [Jakarta Post, 27 Sep 2024](https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2024/09/27/north-sumatra-regent-gets-6-years-for-accepting-bribes); [Jawa Pos, 21 Nov 2025](https://www.jawapos.com/kasuistika/2511200384/kpk-tahan-wakil-ketua-dan-anggota-dprd-kabupaten-oku-karena-fee-proyek-pupr-pantas-apbd-naik-2-kali-lipat)
- **Procurement is a big share of cases:** ≈ 25 % of KPK cases (446 of 1,782) relate to procurement (KPK, 21 Apr 2026). — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/kpk-ingatkan-25-persen-perkara-korupsi-berkaitan-dengan-pengadaan-lt69e75d09c3674/?page=all). ICW 2023: utilities sector 103 cases, ~51 % in infrastructure subsectors such as roads and bridges. — [GoodStats, 28 Jun 2024](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/5-sektor-rawan-korupsi-di-indonesia-pada-tahun-2023-ExTbY)

### 3.2 Why repairs fail again
- **Alor, NTT (2021–22):** new hot-mix on a Rp 28.2 billion provincial road cracked **1–2 days after paving**. — [BPK NTT clipping of Kompas, 23 May 2022](https://ntt.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Jalan-Provinsi-di-Alor-NTT-Senilai-Rp-28-Miliar-Rusak-Sebelum-Tuntas-Dikerjakan.pdf)
- Execution matters more than mix type: a UK field study of hot-mix pothole repairs found no tack coat at 6 of 10 sites, big temperature losses, low-density edges, and **edge failures at about 3 months**. — [Byzyka et al., Aston](https://publications.aston.ac.uk/id/eprint/41779/1/Juliana_et_al.pdf). US/Canada SHRP H-106 (> 1,250 patches) inspected at **1, 3 and 6 months, then every 6 months**; 56 % survived to the final evaluation. — [FHWA InfoPave 99202](https://infopave.fhwa.dot.gov/InfoPave_Repository/Reports/C40/C40_80/99202.pdf)
- Indonesian QC needs compaction density **≥ 98 %** (Spesifikasi Umum 2018 rev. 2, as cited in a core-drill study that still found points below 98 % and under-thickness on a supervised toll project). — [Jurnal PNJ](https://jurnal.pnj.ac.id/index.php/cmj/article/view/7938)
- Contracts include a **maintenance period** with a maintenance guarantee; the provider must request final handover (FHO) 15 days before it ends (Perpres 16/2018 as amended by Perpres 46/2025; Perlem LKPP 12/2021, per a UKPBJ guide). — [UKPBJ Temanggung](https://ukpbj.temanggungkab.go.id/assets/informasi/ukpbj_tmg_1749709400.pdf). That is the window where an early failure can still be claimed — and where our follow-ups are timed.
- **The loop (present as a mechanism supported by case fragments, not proven for one segment):** fee or volume cut → thinner/weaker layer → early damage → new maintenance package → new spending. No national dataset tracks repair lifespan or repeat contracting of the same segment — that missing evidence is what PAVEMENT-PINN's per-segment history creates.
- **What existing transparency tools miss:** opentender.net (ICW + LKPP) scores procurement risk with 7 indicators — **none uses physical outcome data** (condition, durability, re-damage); contract documents are not openly disclosed. — [Open Contracting Partnership, 20 Feb 2025](https://www.open-contracting.org/2025/02/20/6-steps-for-civic-monitors-to-identify-fraud-risks-in-procurement-in-indonesia/)
